# Silver Amethyst Ring

## Description
A **smallish silver ring**, *"somewhat crackled and worn"* and tarnished, in a **male style**, set with a **single amethyst**. Under the stone is a **grey cloud that seems still but is moving** — it shifts position whenever you look away or catch it in your peripheral vision.

## Qualities
- **Value:** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] guessed **ten to twenty crowns** — *"not a fine piece of expensive jewelry"*
- **Magical?** Not established. [[Ewalt Wrangel]] said he did not recognise it, and nobody put it on

-

## Properties / Special Rules
- None known. A **certificate of authenticity** from the **Vermillion Pawn** — a **dwarf-run Middenheim appraiser of rare and magical antiquities** — says it was **assessed there**; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] kept the certificate

## History / Provenance
- **[[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]:** found on the sign-in table at the gate between the catacombs and the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], beside a nearly spent candle and a note signed **"A.B."**: *"You've made this town too warm for me. Keep the ring. I have no further use for it. Enjoy your victory while it lasts."*
- **The party left it behind** as bait for the person it was meant for, with an ambush set

## Notes
- **Whose?** [[Azmus Beham]] (A.B.) seems to have **surrendered** it. Berin: someone brought it from Middenheim, perhaps [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]. Hildegarde: it was **taken from him by someone else** who *"knows how to use it better than us"*
- The **Vermillion Pawn** is a place [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and [[Hildegarde]] — both from Middenheim — had never been able to afford

## [[Session 42 - I Almost Killed You All|Session 42]]
- **Not mentioned on the recording.** The Lurkers came expecting something *"left for us"* by [[Azmus Beham]] (a magical trinket to sell); the table never said whether the ring was taken
