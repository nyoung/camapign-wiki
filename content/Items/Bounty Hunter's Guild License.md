# Bounty Hunter's Guild License

## Description
[[Hildegarde]]'s newly acquired **Bounty Hunter's Guild License** — proudly produced and **passed around the table** at [[Sam Bugman's Brewery]] in [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]. Formal certification of her status as a licensed bounty hunter under the auspices of the guild.

## Properties / Special Rules
- Confers official standing as a member of the Bounty Hunters' Guild
- Useful when claiming bounties (such as the standing **10/- per head** on the [[Howling Ones]] in [[Ferlangen]])

## History / Provenance
- Acquired by [[Hildegarde]] some time before [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]
- First publicly displayed by her during the brewery scene in [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]

## Notes
- May ease bounty-claim formalities on the [[Wolfenburg]] road, where the [[Howling Ones]] reward is in play
