# Strange Shallya Painting

## Description
A painting hung between two minor shrines at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], depicting Shallya unlike any traditional image — androgynous, tall and thin, with spiked hair, a smile on its lips, and tears on both cheeks. The paint was barely dry, added less than a month before the party found it.

## Properties / Special Rules
Not a magic item in the mechanical sense — significant as a clue. Nearly identical to a shrine Hildegarde recalled from [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]'s private office.

## History / Provenance
Donated by a local artist [[Azmus Beham]] claimed he couldn't quite recall, commissioned by an unnamed group who see Shallya "in a different light." Discovered by Berin in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]; Hildegarde immediately recognized the resemblance to Stradovski's shrine.
