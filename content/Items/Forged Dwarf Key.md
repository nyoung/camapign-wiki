# Forged Dwarf Key

## Description
A **copy of a dwarven sewer key**, found on the remains of one of two dead [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] in the ghoul nest of [[The Ghoul Cavern]]. It has **two paddles on either side of the stem**, punched with holes in different shapes (a small square, a circle and so on), **oriented and twisted in different ways**.

## Qualities
- Skilled work: [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] said it *"takes some great skill. It has to be extremely accurate to work."*

## Flaws
- **Broken.** Snorri snapped it in two

## Properties / Special Rules
- Presumably opened the dwarf-locked doors of the [[Wolfenburg Sewers]], as the party's borrowed key does (e.g. the hidden street door, opened with *a half turn one way, a full turn back*)

## History / Provenance
- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]]:** found with two dead Lurkers, their black hooded cloaks and two swords. Snorri seized it and examined it by lamplight: *"A forgery. Somebody's been copying their key… This isn't the one that we made."* He **twisted and snapped it**, called it *"manling trash"*, and swore to find *"these fiends and take our key back from them."* [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] kept the pieces as evidence of the party's partial success for the dwarves

## Notes
- **❓ Copied from the stolen key?** [[Borin Anvilhart]] reported a four-tumbler sewer key **stolen in broad daylight** ([[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]). Snorri's words (*"take our key back"*) suggest he thinks the thieves still have the original
- Its presence on dead Lurkers is the **first physical link** between the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] and the dwarves' keys. Until now the Lurkers were only an obvious suspect
- Related quest: [[Investigate the Linen-Wrapped Corpses]]
