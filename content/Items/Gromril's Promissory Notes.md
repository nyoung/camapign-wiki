# Gromril's Promissory Notes

## Description
A bundle of **promissory notes** — debts collected by the gnome merchant [[Gromril van Aelst]] from merchants in [[Ferlangen]], owed onward to moneylenders in [[Wolfenburg]]. Gromril carries them in a **hidden case beneath his shirt**, not in his belt-purse. A sample shown to the party in [[Session 26 - A Night in the Country|Session 26]] is a **printed banknote stamped with a family crest, with amounts filled in by hand** — paper-money / proto-banking instruments, **near-impossible to forge convincingly** without the right equipment.

## Properties / Special Rules
- **The object of the entire road conflict.** The [[Howling Ones]] are not raiding for random plunder — [[Astrid Hofhaimer]] demanded *these specifically* at parlay, by noon, on pain of harassment all the way to [[Dassel]].
- Their near-unforgeable nature is exactly why a debtor would rather see them **seized or destroyed** than repay.

## History / Provenance
- **[[Session 24 - Hawkeye|Session 24]]** — Gromril revealed to [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] that the notes, not ordinary cargo, are his real value; Berin immediately suspected the attacks were directed by someone with inside knowledge
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country|Session 26]]** — [[Astrid Hofhaimer]] demanded them at parlay; Gromril named **[[Augustus van Kolditz]]** (an antique dealer who owed him 500+ crowns) as his suspected paymaster, and produced an actual banknote to show the party what was at stake. The party resolved to **bait a counter-ambush** with a **decoy bundle** ([[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s cookbook dressed as a note-satchel) rather than hand the real notes over.

## Notes
- Whoever wants them gone has a **financial motive and inside knowledge** of Gromril's route — the thread tying the [[Ferlangen]] alley ambush to the road attacks.
