# Melmoth's Mask

## Description
The mask worn by the dark priest **[[Melmoth]]**, who led the attack outside the [[Forest Inn]] clad in crimson and black. Taken from his body after [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] ran him through and beheaded him.

## Properties / Special Rules
No supernatural properties have been established in play. Its practical use so far is mundane: it **covers the wearer's face**, hiding both identity and [[Hildegarde]]'s scarring.

## History / Provenance
**[[Session 15 - Stradovski's Guide to Selling Out Your Friends|Session 15]]:** [[Melmoth]] was killed outside the [[Forest Inn]] by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]. His gear was stripped — gold, glyph-marked weapons, silver knuckle dusters, a red-skull shield, and the tome *Tenebris Geheimnis*. The mask he wore came away with the rest.

**[[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]]:** **Worn in play for the first time.** [[Hildegarde]] put it on to conceal her scars and her identity while shadowing [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] and her bodyguard through the crowds along the [[Wolfenburg Docks|Wolfenburg riverfront]]. It did not help — she was spotted almost at once, and the covert tail collapsed into an open chase. A masked figure trailing a target through a busy dockland is, in hindsight, conspicuous.

Either this is a **second, distinct mask**, or the confiscated one was returned at some point not captured in the notes. Worth a GM ruling
