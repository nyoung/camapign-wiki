# Classical Medical Note

## Description
A single intact sheet of parchment, found hidden beneath a heavy dresser in [[Schraeder's House]]. Written in Classical — an uneducated, poorly translated copy of [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]'s forbidden research into using disease as a medical tool.

## Qualities
- Written in Classical, the ancient tongue of an empire older than Sigmar
- Reads as though copied by someone with only a rudimentary grasp of the language

## Properties / Special Rules
Berin identified both the language and the content. Directly ties [[Jaie Schraeder]] to [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]'s network.

## Notes
- Found by Ganav, nearly missed — tucked beneath a heavy dresser
- Matches the Classical-language documents in the City Watch's own evidence box, which explicitly name Stradovski
