# Khaine

## Summary
A god of murder. Identified by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] in [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]] as the deity whose symbols mark the **dark sword taken from [[Melmoth]]** and the **tome** the party recovered from the same encounter — both items now in the effective custody of [[Christina Asper]].

## Full Details
[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] performed a brief turn of research in the **library of the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]]** in [[Ferlangen]], cross-referencing the heretical symbols the party had seen during their confrontation with the cultist [[Melmoth]]. He uncovered the name **Khaine — god of murder** before the library staff politely but firmly showed him the door.

The party have not yet learned more — the temple's library was willing to confirm the basic identification but not to host extended research on so dark a subject.

## Implications / Party Relevance
- The dark sword from [[Melmoth]], the **black mask** taken from [[Hildegarde]], the **Khainite-marked crossbow** taken from her, and the **tome** are now understood to be sacred to a god of murder
- All four items are presently in [[Christina Asper]]'s custody; reclaiming them is a confrontation that has been postponed but not avoided
- Suggests that [[Melmoth]]'s ghoul cult was not merely a cult of decay or undeath but tied to a deliberate religion of murder — sharpening the moral and theological stakes of the work the party has done so far
- The party have been carrying these items unknowingly across the Empire for weeks
