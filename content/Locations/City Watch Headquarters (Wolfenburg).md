# City Watch Headquarters (Wolfenburg)

## Description
The seat of the Wolfenburg City Watch, where the party reports on the [[Jaie Schraeder]] murder investigation under the consultancy status granted to them after they cashed in the [[The Howling Ones Bounty|Howling Ones bounty]].

## Notable Features
- The party's official channel for trading information with the Watch

## Notable NPCs
- [[Captain Crais]] — Watch captain; receives the party's investigation updates

## History
**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** The party's final stop of the morning. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] informed [[Captain Crais]] that the body previously thought to be [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was likely a plant. Crais reciprocated with reports of embalmed and long-decayed corpses drifting downriver and collecting around the city's docks, and a briefing on the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] and [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]].
