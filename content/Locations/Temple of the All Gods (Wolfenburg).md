# Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)

## Description
A buttressed, domed building tucked against the city wall, its interior ringed with shrines to dozens of minor spirits and forgotten gods. Beneath it, catacombs far older than the temple above wind deep under the city in a maze of intersecting corridors lined with burial niches.

## Notable Features
- A freshly painted (less than a month old) shrine depicting an androgynous, twisted Shallya — nearly identical to one recalled from [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]'s private office — see [[Strange Shallya Painting]]
- A shrine/scroll cataloguing a Kislevite nature spirit (ox + howling wolf) matching markings found at [[Schraeder's House]]
- Catacombs beneath the temple, older than the building itself, with Sigmar and Shallya religious markers deliberately scratched out on unused burial slots
- A heavy iron gate at the far end of the catacombs, lock and hinges freshly oiled, base worn smooth from repeated use, beyond which a tunnel smelling of river water runs roughly north–south beneath a major city road

## Notable NPCs
- [[Azmus Beham]] — proprietor; jovial and sales-driven until asked about missing bodies, then visibly frightened
