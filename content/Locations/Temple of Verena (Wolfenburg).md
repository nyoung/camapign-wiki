# Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)

## Description
Wolfenburg's temple of law and learning. The party had already left [[Pilpoul Ivri]] here for care and turned [[Ganav]] over to the courts through its offices before visiting in person.

## Notable Features
- A library holding scrolls cataloguing the various spirits and minor deities housed at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]

## Notable NPCs
- [[Hoffman Jannitzer]] — repaid a four-crown debt by Hildegarde here

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg]]

## History
**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]:** First visited in person, to collect wages and settle affairs. Berin received a slightly larger share than the others. Hildegarde repaid a four-crown debt to [[Hoffman Jannitzer]]. Berin researched the temple library and found a scroll matching a Kislevite nature spirit symbol (ox + howling wolf) to markings found at [[Schraeder's House]] — the same symbol later found associated with the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]].
