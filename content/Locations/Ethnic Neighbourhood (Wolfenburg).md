# Ethnic Neighbourhood (Wolfenburg)

## Description
Wolfenburg's non-human quarter, on the city's south side. Architecture shifts sharply within it — sturdy dwarven stone longhouses along the south wall, cramped halfling dwellings further north, leaning multi-story tenements with external wooden staircases housing the area's poorer human residents.

## Notable Features
- Dwarven stone longhouses along the south wall
- Halfling dwellings to the north
- A ramshackle four-to-five-story tenement with external wooden staircases — home to [[Madame Reitenbach]]

## Notable NPCs
- Unnamed dwarf resident — gave directions in exchange for the names [[Gromril van Aelst]] and [[Mangold]]
- Tenement landlord — more interested in unpaid rent than his tenant's grief
- [[Madame Reitenbach]]
