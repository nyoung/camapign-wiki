# Wolfen Bridge

## Description
A bridge over the river at [[Wolfenburg]], standing near the [[Wolfenburg Docks|docks]]. Its **supports** sit close enough to the quay that [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s barge, cut loose and spinning on the current, **struck them** before drifting away downriver.

## Notable Features
- **Bridge supports** in the river channel, close to the dock moorings
- Overlooks the [[Wolfenburg Docks]] — [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] took high ground near the bridge in [[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]] to open a longbow line over the quay
- A **watch presence**: the six watchmen who arrested [[Hildegarde]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] came down the quay from here

## Notable NPCs
- An unnamed watch sergeant and six watchmen

## Factions Present
- [[The Watch]] — patrols and responds from this direction

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg Docks]] — immediately adjacent, downhill onto the quay
- [[Wolfenburg]]

## History
Named for the first time in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]], though the party had already used the high ground near it during the [[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]] ambush. Two things happened here in Session 37: the **drifting barge struck its supports** after [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] severed the last mooring rope, and **whistles and raised voices went up from this direction** moments later, bringing the watch down onto the party.

## Notes
- The bridge is the **watch's approach route** to the docks — worth remembering before starting anything else on that quay
