# Sam Bugman's Brewery

## Description
A cavernous, active brewery and taproom on the corner of **First Strasse and Wallstrasse** in [[Ferlangen]]. Three-quarters full of **mining dwarves** at the time of the party's first visit — many bearing the evidence of their trade in **crushed limbs and missing eyes**. The kind of room where ordering wine earns a blank stare, and the dwarven ale is stronger than most humans can survive at a normal pace.

The proprietor's name on the sign is **Sam (Samuel) Bugman**.

## Notable Features
- Corner of **First Strasse and Wallstrasse**
- Two house ales sold:
  - **Beardtangle**
  - **Bugman's Best**
- A waitress capable of dispensing both with the quiet confidence of someone who has watched many a human make a terrible decision — and who will, if asked nicely, sell a **hangover remedy** on the way out
- Audience reception of unwanted music: **rotten vegetables** are kept handy

## Notable NPCs
- **Brewery waitress** (unnamed) — recommends ales, sells hangover remedies on departure

## Factions Present
- A regular clientele of **mining dwarves**

## History
- **[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** Party visited following a sandwich-board advertisement. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] ordered the strongest available; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] ordered a round; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] declined entirely (his request for wine was met with a blank stare); [[Hildegarde]] used the occasion to show off her [[Bounty Hunter's Guild License]]. The dwarven ale put [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] thoroughly under, and he climbed onto his feet to sing rowdy dwarven tavern songs to an audience of actual dwarves who were not amused. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] hustled the group out (asking for a hangover remedy on the way) before fists replaced vegetables. Outside, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] dunked his head in a water barrel; [[Hildegarde]] held his collar so he wouldn't drown in three inches of water. Moments later the party witnessed [[Mangold]] chasing a thief and the ambush on [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] in the alley opposite.

## Notes
- Wine is not on the menu
- Tipping a brawl in a room full of mining dwarves is a poor career move; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s call to leave was the right one
