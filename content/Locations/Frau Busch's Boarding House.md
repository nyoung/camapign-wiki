# Frau Busch's Boarding House

## Description
A pristine, freshly whitewashed building in [[Ferlangen]], standing like **a small fortress of propriety amid surrounding disorder**. The neighbourhood around it has slid into rough taverns, balconied brothels, and red-lamped streets over the years; the boarding house has not. Run by the widow **[[Frau Busch]]**, with her daughter **Gertrude** doing the practical work (baths, laundry, lice-checks). The temple-arranged billet for visiting investigators in [[Ferlangen]].

## House Rules (laid out at the door)
- Wash before bed
- Be present for meals at **8 a.m. and 5 p.m.**
- **No drinking** on the premises, and no arriving in a drunken state
- Each guest receives a **single beeswax candle** to last the week

## Notable Features
- Front door key normally retained by [[Frau Busch|Frau Busch]]; granted as a one-night exception to [[Hildegarde]] for an investigation errand
- Plain but well-prepared meals — boiled vegetables, unseasoned meat
- Overnight laundry and bath service

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Frau Busch]]** — proprietress; widow; matronly; devout; strict
- **Gertrude** — Frau Busch's daughter; mechanically efficient with baths and lice-checks

## Factions Present
- Used by the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] to lodge visiting investigators

## History
- **[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** [[Hoffman Jannitzer]] led the party here through the rough neighbourhood. [[Frau Busch]] laid out her rules; Gertrude bathed and deloused the party; clothes were taken for laundry; each guest issued a beeswax candle. Over dinner [[Christina Asper]] arrived with **[[Lucas Pollack]]**; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] formally apologised to Asper, who recorded the party's news of the [[Wolfenburg]] disease cluster. Late at night [[Frau Busch]] granted [[Hildegarde]] a one-night key exception so she could visit the nearby brothel for information, with strict instructions to relock and return the key by morning.
