# Physicians' Guild (Wolfenburg)

## Description
A professional establishment in Wolfenburg where secretaries work in a shared bullpen of desks rather than individual offices. Distinct from the [[Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)|Physicians' Guild in Ferlangen]].

## Notable Features
- Shared bullpen of secretary desks, rather than private offices

## Notable NPCs
- Danel Koberger — clerk, took over after [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s death
- Professor Melancthon — former guildmaster, retired to Altdorf ~5 years ago
- [[Jaie Schraeder]] — murdered former secretary

## Connected Locations
