# Megrita's Anchor Boarding House

## Description
A well-appointed corner building on the Wolfenburg riverfront, marked with the sign of a boarding house.

## Notable Features
- Riverfront corner location, within sight of the docks

## Notable NPCs
- An older woman on staff who greeted a raven-haired woman and her bodyguard at the door
- Watched by [[Reiner Kolber]]'s boy Eyeball for a full day prior to the party's arrival

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg]] (riverfront)

## History
**[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]]:** Identified by [[Reiner Kolber]]'s street-orphan network as where a raven-haired woman and her broad-shouldered bodyguard had been staying — description matches [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] and her companion exactly, though not yet confirmed by name. The party watched from afar and saw the pair go inside and not re-emerge. She reportedly dined out at the Trout and Pike that evening before returning here for the night.

**[[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]]:** The party **scouted the route from here down to the [[Wolfenburg Docks|docks]]** as the basis for their ambush on [[Elfrieda Teuschel]], buying manacles and caltrops along the way and settling on the quayside warehouses as their strike point. Teuschel left the boarding house for the water and never returned to it — she is now aboard a boat on the river.
