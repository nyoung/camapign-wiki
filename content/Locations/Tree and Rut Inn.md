## History
**[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]]:** The party regrouped here after Hildegarde's encounter with [[Astrid Hofhaimer]]. A runner from [[Reiner Kolber]]'s network reported here that the raven-haired woman had dined at the Trout and Pike before returning to [[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House|Megrita's Anchor]]. Hildegarde's wounds were treated overnight.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** The party returned here for the night after [[The Pig and Tack]], walking moonlit streets by the better-lit routes at [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s insistence. The common room was full of folk musicians playing "The Reik is Wide" with locals singing along. Berin fished the patrons for gang-war rumours and got only vague nods; [[Hildegarde]] asked the innkeeper to recommend a reputable tailor. [[Reiner Kolber]] was instructed to send word here by end of day on [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s movements.
