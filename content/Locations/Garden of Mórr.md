# Garden of Mórr

## Description
Wolfenburg's cemetery and catacombs, near the Cathedral of Sigmar — a stark black-brick building with black tiles and a gate adorned with raven symbols. The interior is cool and lily-scented.

## Notable Features
- Catacombs the party suspects may double as a secret passage in and out of the city (unconfirmed)
- A viewing room where bodies pending burial are kept

## Notable NPCs
- [[Ewalt Wrangel]] — the cleric who met the party and granted them access

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg]]

## History
**[[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]:** Discussed as a possible next stop, to examine the bodies from the Schraeder townhouse fire. Berin noted the catacombs could serve as a secret passage used by parties connected to [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]].

**[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]]:** Visited in person. [[Ewalt Wrangel]] led the party to a viewing room holding the three bodies from the Schraeder townhouse fire — the two servants and [[Jaie Schraeder]] himself, burials still pending. Berin's examination confirmed all three were killed before the fire was set: the female servant's throat was slashed cleanly; the male servant showed signs of prolonged suffering before death; Schraeder was killed by a single expert sword thrust through the heart.

**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]:** Returned to press [[Ewalt Wrangel]] further. He confirmed the body attributed to [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was older, malnourished, and missing teeth — inconsistent with a well-kept servant — and confirmed the Temple of the All Gods missing-bodies rumour, naming its proprietor [[Azmus Beham]] as a contact.

## Notes
- Connection to the disturbed-corpses rumour from the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] confirmed by [[Ewalt Wrangel]] in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]
- The Breytenbach body's mismatched wounds and description suggest it may be a planted assassin, not the actual butler
