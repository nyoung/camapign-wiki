# Wolfenburg Courthouse

## Description
The center of provincial justice in [[Wolfenburg]], adjacent to a permanent gallows in the plaza and also serving as the administrative hub for bandit-bounty collections. Presided over by Magistrate Gawinus, known as "the hanging judge."

## History
**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]:** [[Ganav]] was tried here for his time with the [[Howling Ones]]. Magistrate Gawinus sentenced him to hang despite an eloquent defense (and mid-speech marriage proposal to [[Hildegarde]]); [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] invoked a rare statute and paid three gold crowns to take personal custody of Ganav, saving him from the gallows.

**[[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]:** Also doubles as City Watch HQ. The party was marched here by Watch guards after being caught inside the sealed [[Schraeder's House]] crime scene; Captain Crais was furious but relented after Berin offered to translate the Watch's Classical-language evidence, revealing more documents naming [[Professor Udo Stradovski]].

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **[[Hildegarde]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] were marched here** from the [[Wolfenburg Docks]] by a watch sergeant and six watchmen, over a dead stevedore on the quay
- The surviving stevedore **accused Berin of slashing him and killing a companion**. [[Hildegarde]] produced her **bounty hunter's licence** and the **Imperial warrant** for [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]; the sergeant was unmoved by conflicting stories over a body
- **[[Captain Crais]] heard the matter** and was weary and frustrated: warrants or not, **he cannot have bodies lining the streets of his city**
- The stevedore **dropped a freshly minted Nordland crown on the desk** as evidence of his payment; Crais noted the warrant covers Stradovski, **not stolen coin**
- **Crais demanded corroborating names** — [[Hoffman Jannitzer]], [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]] were given. **Asper and Pollack arrived within half an hour**, spoke with Crais privately, and **the party was released** with a firm warning
- It was here that [[Hildegarde]] learned, **for the first time**, that Berin had cut a man in the groin during the fight — *"The groin, really?"*
