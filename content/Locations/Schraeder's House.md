# Schraeder's House

## Description
The late [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s townhouse, boarded up under City Watch seal after a fire gutted the upper floor. The interior bears every sign of a staged crime scene: a bloodstained hallway with drag marks, a ransacked study, and a master bedroom deliberately burned along with Schraeder's books, papers, and personal shrine to Sigmar.

## Notable Features
- Bloodstained hall carpet with drag marks leading upstairs
- Ground floor undisturbed but impersonal; kitchen shows a meal abandoned mid-preparation
- Guest bedroom: a crude, inaccurate blood-painted howling wolf — a deliberate Howling Ones frame-up
- Study ransacked, all books and papers removed
- Master bedroom gutted by a bonfire fueled by books, papers, and Schraeder's personal shrine to Sigmar
- Two body-shaped ash marks in the master bedroom
- A hidden cubby behind a shifted wall painting, already emptied
- Searched twice, per Berin's read of the wreckage — once by the killers, once by someone else afterward

## Notable NPCs
- [[Jaie Schraeder]] — the murdered occupant
