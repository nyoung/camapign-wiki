# Amman Estate

## Description
A massive, wealthy estate in Wolfenburg's Estates District, behind a towering wall and an iron gate bearing a raven seal. Marble stairs and columned double doors lead into an exquisitely appointed foyer.

## Notable NPCs
- **Dr. von Bora** — currently staying here; a weathered scholar of spirituality from the University of Altdorf, seeking bodyguards for a research expedition to the Kislev–Ostland border

## History
**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]:** The party called here to meet Dr. von Bora, hoping to secure his goodwill and his attendance at [[Ganav]]'s hearing. He agreed to both, after [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] privately warned him (in Classical) about [[Christina Asper]]'s zealotry and vouched for Ganav's skill as a healer.
