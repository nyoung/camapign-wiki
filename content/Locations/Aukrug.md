# Aukrug

A tiny village a day's travel south of [[Ferlangen]], huddled around a working wooden palisade. The village's entire public life seems to centre on its coaching inn, [[The Dangling Mutant]]. Surrounded by the forest and hills that characterise the road toward the [[Middle Mountains]]. The miners who stay here work the surrounding hills with pickaxes, trooping out before dawn each morning.

## Key Location

- **[[The Dangling Mutant]]** — coaching inn, the heart of the village

## History with the Party

**[[Session 23 - Ambush!]]:** The caravan arrived here by nightfall on 7 Jahrdrung 2512, after a day of uneasy forest travel. The Hofbauer Bodelstein caravan had passed through one day prior, confirmed by the stable hands. The caravan stayed the night and departed the following morning into the ambush.
