# The Hanging Highwayman

A medium-sized coaching inn at [[Hasselhund]], its sign depicting a body dangling from a scaffold. Despite the grim imagery, a reasonable overnight stop on the road south toward [[Dassel]].

## Description

Medium-sized coaching house with enough room that [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] could pay for a large private room for the injured [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]. Staffed by a named innkeeper (Loflinger Grünewald) and serving staff, including the maid who remembered [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]].

## Stradovski Connection

A **serving maid** here remembered [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Professor Stradovski]] — he passed through roughly two weeks before the party arrived (c. 25 Nachexen 2512) with a large merchant caravan. He noticed her **Shallyan dove talisman** and offered to "show her a real Shallyan miracle." She declined. A solid, specific confirmation that Stradovski passed this exact point on the road to [[Wolfenburg]].
