# Forest Inn (Brein Kloff)

## Description
A remote inn a day's ride east of Wolfenburg on the road toward Kislev, near where [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s stabbed body was discovered. **Not yet visited by the party** — known only by reputation, via the Watch's investigation.

> [!warning] Naming collision
> This is a **different location** from the party's own [[Forest Inn|Forest Inn]] on the Nordland Road (the ghoul-infested inn cleared in Sessions 13–15). Same name, unrelated place — kept as separate notes deliberately.
