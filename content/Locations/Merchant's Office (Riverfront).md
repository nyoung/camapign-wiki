## History
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Berin, posing as a Wissenland merchant house representative investigating missing medical supplies and amber tied to [[Marretta Schongauer]], got a clerk to confirm two recent Ferlangen shipments handled by the [[Hofbauer Bodelstein]] company included goods from [[Klaus von Kolditz|Von Kolditz]]. A bribe from Boris loosened the clerk further: Von Kolditz has been leaking caravan movements to the [[Howling Ones]], keeping his own shipments safe while rivals are plundered — independent confirmation of the informant theory from the [[Escort Gromril van Aelst to Wolfenburg]] job. Stolen goods reportedly move on via forged papers through boat captains bound for Nuln or Altdorf.
