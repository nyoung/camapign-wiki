# The Pig and Tack

## Description
A seedy riverside tavern in [[Wolfenburg]] and a testament to optimistic engineering: roughly **two-thirds of the building juts out over the river**, held up by a chaotic accumulation of lumber added piecemeal over the years. Inside it is nearly pitch black, lit only by a single candle on each table. Around twenty pairs of eyes — and likely as many weapons — turn on anyone who comes through the door. The house rule, as delivered by the barman, is that this is an establishment for drinking, not conversation.

## Notable Features
- Two-thirds cantilevered over the river on accumulated lumber
- Near-total darkness; single candles per table
- The darkest corner is [[Anselm Rathsky]]'s, and he is effectively invisible in it
- The dwarf bartender is the **standing contact channel to Rathsky**

## Notable NPCs
- [[Anselm Rathsky]] — leader of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]; holds court from the darkest corner
- [[Fiedler]] — gambler and smuggling contact, plays cards here with his crew
- The bartender — a stodgy, cigar-chewing dwarf; unwelcoming, but the way to reach Rathsky

## History
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Named by one of three fleeing thugs as where their frontman, [[Anselm Rathsky]], could be found.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** **First visit.** The party changed out of their loaned Myrmidian robes into common street clothes before coming, and still drew the whole room's attention. [[Hildegarde]] downed her ale and talked her way into a card game with [[Fiedler]], playing badly on purpose to keep him talking about getting someone out of the city. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], drunk off a single pint of the rock-gut ale, bought a round for the entire bar — eight shillings that bought considerably more goodwill than it should have. [[Anselm Rathsky]] then emerged from the dark, revealed himself as the Lurkers' leader, explained the gang war, and offered the party a deal (see [[Stop the Steel Fists' Pursuit]]).
