# The Ghoul Cavern

## Description
A **natural cavern** of stalactites and stalagmites, its ceiling rising and falling from about **four feet to very high**, reached from the end of a dead-end **side tunnel** off the [[Wolfenburg Sewers]] through **a rough three-foot hole** someone punched through the wall. It smells of **rotting flesh**, not sewage. It lies near — **possibly under** — the city wall.

## Notable Features
- **Discarded bones** near the entrance; **piles of bones** further in
- **Linen-wrapped corpses** dragged in — at least one with its head-covering torn away, leaving **a bare skull** *(already skeletal, or stripped)*
- **Drag marks lead straight through the hole**
- Home to a **ghoul colony** including an **elder ghoul** — see [[Ghoul]]. **All killed** in [[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]]
- **A ghoul nest**: round the bend, the largest heap of old bones has been built into a nest. **The cavern is a dead end**; the only way in is the punched-through hole
- Among the bones: **burial wrappings**, **slips of paper** like those on the tunnel bodies, and **silver coins** (40 shillings; local Ostland and Imperial, a few from other provinces, none from [[Beeckerhoven]]). The dead were buried **naked, without jewellery or belongings**; only gold teeth or old breaks could identify them. One body had its **ribcage opened and eaten**
- **Two dead [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]** in black hooded cloaks, with swords, and a **[[Forged Dwarf Key]]**
- The GM said the party's fight there was cramped and dark; long weapons were impractical

## Notable NPCs
- The **elder ghoul** and its pack — see [[Ghoul]]

## History
- **[[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]:** entered following the drag marks. **A fight with ghouls began; the session ended mid-combat**, with the elder ghoul blinded, prone and stunned but not confirmed dead
- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]]:** the fight was finished: [[Hildegarde]] killed the elder, [[Ganav]] and [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] a ghoul each, and a fourth was cut down. The party searched the nest, found the dead Lurkers and the forged key, took two Lurker cloaks and a strip of burial cloth as evidence, and left by the side tunnel. Berin prayed over the dead
