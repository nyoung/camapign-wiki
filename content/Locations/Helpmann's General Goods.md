# Helpmann's General Goods

## Description
A cluttered little storefront in [[Ferlangen]] presided over by **[[Karl Helpmann]]** — bespectacled, balding, and irrepressibly mercantile — and watched over by a **large orange cat sleeping on a shelf**. Smaller and more shop-like than the "warehouse operator" framing the party heard at the [[Fist of Amber]] would suggest. Stock includes letter openers, magnifying glasses, and at least one used set of **Averland sun-livery armour** tucked into a dusty corner.

## Notable Features
- Orange shop-cat (always asleep on its shelf)
- Counter where Helpmann inspects coins through a magnifying glass — and offers to sell the magnifying glass
- Dusty corners holding miscellaneous unlicensed goods (armour, sundries) bought from passers-through at a fraction of value

## Notable NPCs
- [[Karl Helpmann]] — proprietor

## Factions Present
- (Unaffiliated peddlar; conducts a side trade moving serfs to [[Averland]] farms — see [[Karl Helpmann]])

## Connected Locations

- [[Averland]] — southern end of Helpmann's serf-relocation pipeline
