# Levudaldorf

## Description
The seat of a **manor** whose servant came to [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s house to collect [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] and Schraeder. The party has not been there. *(Whisper rendered it in several ways; the spelling is from the module.)*

## Notable Features
- A manor, home to someone "of note, somebody important", whom Stradovski and Schraeder were to meet

## Notable NPCs

- The "somebody of note" at the manor *(unnamed)*
