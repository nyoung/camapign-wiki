# Wolfenburg Docks

## Description
[[Wolfenburg]]'s riverfront working quayside, strung along the water below the city proper. The dockland is built on two levels — an upper walkway and a **lower quay** reached by flights of stairs — and is backed by **quayside warehouses**. A **bridge** nearby offers high ground with a clear line of sight over the open water. A distinctive **grey house** stands at the edge of the quay. The boards are busy with dockworkers, and the surrounding trade draws hired muscle in numbers.

The **far north end** of the docks is where river traffic slips out of the city, and where the **river nets** are raised at sundown to close the waterway for the night — the detail that set the deadline in [[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]].

## Notable Features
- **Two levels** — upper walkway and lower quay, connected by stairs
- **Quayside warehouses** — the party's chosen ambush ground
- **A bridge** with high ground and sightlines across the water
- **The grey house at the edge of the quay**
- **River nets**, raised at sundown to close the river
- Working dockhands and a ready supply of hired muscle

## History
**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** [[Reiner Kolber]] reported that [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] had cut a deal with a barge captain **at the far north end of the docks** and meant to flee at sundown, just before the river nets went up. [[Captain Crais]] separately reported **embalmed and long-decayed corpses drifting downriver** and collecting around the city's docks.

**[[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]]:** The party scouted the route here from [[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House]] and set an ambush among the quayside warehouses to take [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] before she sailed. The plan collapsed when [[Hildegarde]]'s tail was spotted, and the docks became a running fight: Hildegarde leapt from the stairs to the lower quay and was caught in a headlock by a brute (almost certainly [[Ansgar Knochenbrecher]]); [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] crippled an attacker on the boards; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] took the high ground by the bridge and struck a boatman at **thirty-six yards across open water**, setting Teuschel's departing vessel drifting. The session ended mid-fight with the party split between the dock and the drifting boat.

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **The [[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]] brawl concluded here.** [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], holding elevated ground, **incapacitated the entire opposing crew** — an arrow through the boat captain's knee, crew wounded until none were standing. **One stevedore died on the deck**
- [[Hildegarde]] fought [[Ansgar Knochenbrecher|Ansgar]] across the **rain-slicked deck** and was caught in a bear hug; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] cut through the hired stevedores
- **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]] surrendered from behind the ship's cargo walls**, and both sides met to parley **by the cargo crane** — where the interrogation took place
- **Boris severed the barge's last mooring rope**; the vessel spun out into the current, **struck the [[Wolfen Bridge]] supports**, and drifted away downriver with Teuschel aboard
- **The watch responded fast.** Whistles and raised voices went up from the [[Wolfen Bridge]] almost immediately, and **six armed watchmen** marched down the quay. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and the others slipped away; [[Hildegarde]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] stayed and were taken to the [[Wolfenburg Courthouse]]
- **⚠️ Operational note:** the quay is **overlooked by the [[Wolfen Bridge]]**, which is the watch's approach route. Anything done on these docks is on a short fuse
