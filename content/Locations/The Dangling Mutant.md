# The Dangling Mutant

The coaching inn at [[Aukrug]], a rough establishment whose sign depicts a mutant being hanged from a tree. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] cheerfully reinterpreted this as "The Dangling Appendages," insisting the common folk had simply misread the artistry.

## Description

Inside: floors buried under years of trampled nutshells, furniture broken in places, air thick with the smell of rotten whiskey the locals call **orc bitters**. The common room fills nightly with miners (human and dwarf) and bar girls. A **sunken pit ring** at the centre hosts a pit fighter named [[Igor]] who taunts the crowd and pummels every challenger to near-death while bets fly across the room.

The innkeeper claims ignorance when shown suspicious coins. Paid well for his silence on other matters.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Igor]]** — pit fighter; regular entertainment at the ring
- **Innkeeper** (unnamed) — dismissed [[Hildegarde]]'s counterfeit coin inquiry

## History with the Party

**[[Session 23 - Ambush!]]:** Caravan stayed overnight on 7 Jahrdrung 2512. Stable hands confirmed the Hofbauer Bodelstein caravan had passed through one day prior. The inn roared with pit fight bets until well past midnight.
