# Mountain Pass Choke Point

A natural ambush point on the rocky highland road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]], where the [[Howling Ones]] sprang their **second ambush** on the caravan. Also referred to in the session notes as the **Rocky Mountain Trail**.

## Description

A **narrow pass** with a sheer cliff on one side and dense old-growth forest on the other, choked with rocky outcroppings and boulders. Rocky debris on both sides of the road prevents wagons from turning around — once a caravan is committed to the climb, it cannot easily retreat. A **[[Ruined Watchtower]]** crowns the high ground above, giving archers a commanding field of fire over anyone below. The trail forks into a **less-steep left path and a steeper, more direct right path** up the hill. Heavy rains wash rock and timber down across the road, worsening the going.

## History with the Party

- **[[Session 24 - Hawkeye]]:** The caravan reached the choke point as the session ended; the hidden attackers revealed themselves from the ruins above — a cliffhanger.
- **[[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf]]:** The second ambush played out here and was **won**. The opening volley killed [[Danderich Flisshausten|Sir Danderich]]'s and [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s horses; a **monstrous wolf** charged from the forest and mauled [[Christina Asper|Asper]] before [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] drove it off with an arrow; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Mangold]] broke the outlaws on the high ground; [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] fell into a hidden pit trap. Several outlaws were killed and one more captured before the rest fled. The party then cleared the rain-washed road and moved on to the [[Wayside Shrine]].
