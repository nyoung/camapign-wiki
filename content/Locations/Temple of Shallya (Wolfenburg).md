# Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)

## Description
A sprawling, mismatched complex of interconnected structures held together by love and necessity, filled in every corner with the sick and the destitute. Includes a ward where children draw on the walls with chalk, and a communal dining room handing out free stew.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]]** — high priestess; received the party in a private back room
- [[Nina Trielffen]] — lay servant; [[Marla Hengist]] — orphan *([[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]])*
- Shallyan Initiate with tribal tattoos — summoned to bring protective talismans; later seen exchanging secret hand signals with [[Christina Asper]]

## History
**[[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]]:** The party delivered a letter of introduction from the Shallyan priestess in [[Ferlangen]]. [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] listened to their full account of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] and shared three leads: children under seven vanishing after being collected from local farms; the murder of Physicians Guild secretary [[Jaie Schraeder]]; and a pattern of rare wasting-disease victims arriving through the eastern gate. She wrote a letter of introduction to Dr. von Bora and provided protective talismans.

**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]:** As the party left at day's end, Boris and Hildegarde caught a tattooed initiate making discreet hand signals at [[Christina Asper]] — later tracked to a secret meeting in the alley between here and the Temple of Myrmidia, then to the [[Witch Hunters Guild]].

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **🔴 [[Hilde Muntz]] is trapped inside.** A former [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] member who has since sought a Shallyan calling, and [[Christina Asper]]'s close friend, she is sheltering here and **cannot leave**
- **Both gangs are watching every exit** — the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] and the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] — over the stolen lottery purse
- **🔴 Neither gang will violate the temple's sanctuary.** Per [[Lucas Pollack]], it is **too important a source of healing and charity for their own people** to be worth breaching. The sanctuary holds — but it is also a siege
- The party has agreed to extract her, with a working plan to **smuggle her out via the merchant network previously used to move people to Averland**

## Session 39 — Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently
- **🔴 A way out from below.** The temple's **laundry** drains through a **20-inch ceramic pipe** into the [[Wolfenburg Sewers]]. The pipe climbs about **20 feet** to a **round, riveted metal floor grate** that sits loose like a manhole cover and **can be lifted**
- [[Ganav]] climbed it, heard **a woman humming** as she worked by candlelight, waited for her to leave, **chalked the rim** and **wedged the grate slightly open**
- Too tight for [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s breastplate. The party's thinking: enter by the front door, **leave by the drain**, then go through the sewers and away by boat, taking [[Hilde Muntz]] with them

## [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]
- **The party went in and got Hilde out.** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] entered by the front door, past **Steel Fist watchers** (a man in a **copper-coloured beret** on a corner; a man in an **upper window**, recognised by Hildegarde from an alley behind a cart), through a courtyard full of the **sick and hungry**
- **Berin's bucket speech** (a vision from Myrmidia: *disease and intrigue, flee*) set the courtyard arguing, giving them cover
- **Inside:** *a maze of small rooms, corridors and linked buildings*; a **long-stay ward** of burn victims and the dying on makeshift beds; a **linen closet** where *"nobody goes"*; a **circular stair** down to a **basement laundry** (large basins, sheets on hooks, humid, smelling of cleaning supplies) whose floor slopes to **one grate**
- **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] was out** at the Cathedral of Sigmar
- **A hidden guest:** [[Marla Hengist]] told Hildegarde the sisters are **sheltering a man called Gerd** here (see [[Gerd Breytenbach]])
- **Hilde Muntz left by the drain**, on a **rope of knotted bedsheets**

## [[Session 43 - A Mighty Gift|Session 43]]
- **A public haven.** The party reached the courtyard in the late afternoon; [[Nina Trielffen]] was pacing it. The temple is overcrowded with people seeking healing (an "emergency room" in a crowded city); the party was seen at once because they brought [[Marla Hengist|Marla]]

- **Isolation room:** a private room for contagious patients, where [[Gerd Breytenbach]] lies in a hospital gown with a removable linen mask
- **Staff:** the initiate [[Hermann Roeging]] is distrusted by Nina
