# Wolfenburg

## Description
Provincial capital of [[Ostland]], astride a wide river in a farmed valley. Squat, thick walls bristle with circular towers and angular buttresses; a massive Sigmarite cathedral dominates the eastern hill. Population roughly nine thousand — one of the largest cities in the eastern Empire. Gates close strictly at sundown; visitors pay a gold crown toll and face weapon-length restrictions. Purple bellflowers, treated with obvious local reverence, are planted at the gates and hung above the arches.
