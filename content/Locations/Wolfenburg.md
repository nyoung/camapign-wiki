# Wolfenburg

## Description
Provincial capital of [[Ostland]] and a regional crossroads — described to the party as the city where all roads out of Ostland converge. Not yet visited; introduced as a destination to the party by the harried Shallyan priestess in [[Ferlangen]] in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]].

## Notable Features
- **Recent simultaneous outbreaks of rare and exotic diseases** — Shallyan colleagues arriving from Wolfenburg brought word that ailments uncommon enough that *seeing one would be alarming* are appearing *several at once*. Rumours reached [[Ferlangen]] roughly **one week before Session 20**, suggesting the outbreaks themselves are recent.
- Crossroads where the eastern Ostland routes converge

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Matron Isolde Uricht]]** — high priestess of the [[Temple of Shallya]] in Wolfenburg; named to the party as their best contact for information about the disease outbreak

## Factions Present
- [[Cult of Shallya]] — Wolfenburg temple

## Connected Locations
- [[Ferlangen]] — west; party's current location
- [[Ostland]] — provincial parent

## History
- **[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Named as the most likely next destination for **[[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]** — the disease cluster aligns suggestively with his methods, and Wolfenburg is the natural junction of the routes east. Party plans to pursue.
