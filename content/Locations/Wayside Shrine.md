# Wayside Shrine

A small roadside shrine on the rocky highland road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]], roughly ten miles short of [[Dassel]]. The battered caravan made camp here for the night after the second [[Howling Ones]] ambush.

## Description

Built of **rough-hewn stone columns** in a style suggesting the **old nature spirits** — possibly [[Taal]]'s domain. It sits on a slight rise with **good sightlines in both directions** and a **sheer cliff protecting one flank**, which [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] judged the best defensible position the caravan was likely to find before dark. A thin trail of woodsmoke rising from the shrine first drew the party's attention.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Mathis Haack]]** — emerged from within the shrine when the party arrived; claims to be forest warden for Lord Jürgen Kimmelman; shared a bag of rabbits; under [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s suspicion; **slipped away during the night** (Session 26)
- **[[Astrid Hofhaimer]]** — [[Howling Ones]] leader who rode to a **morning parlay** in a clearing near the shrine (Session 26), demanding [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s promissory notes by noon

## History with the Party

**[[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf]]:** After winning the second ambush at the [[Mountain Pass Choke Point]] and spending the afternoon clearing the flooded road, the caravan spotted woodsmoke from the shrine and circled the wagons here for the night (evening, 9 Jahrdrung 2512). [[Mathis Haack]] appeared from inside; [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] and [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]] cooked his rabbits into a restorative broth for the wounded. The party kept a long, watchful night against the rain, the wounded ([[Christina Asper|Asper]], [[Hawkeye]]/Ganav) resting as best they could.

**[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]:** The shrine became the caravan's fortified night camp. The party wove branch barricades around the wagons and stood a four-shift watch through a night of **[[Howling Ones]] psychological harassment** — manufactured howls, branch-snapping, and white-fletched arrows thumping into the wagons every twenty minutes. The defensible rise (good sightlines, a cliff at one flank) held. Come morning, **[[Mathis Haack]] had vanished** without a word, and **[[Astrid Hofhaimer]]** called the party to a parlay in a nearby clearing. The party left the shrine intending to spring a counter-ambush rather than meet her noon deadline.
