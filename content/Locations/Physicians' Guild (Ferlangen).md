# Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)

## Description
A well-appointed building near the main plaza in [[Ferlangen]]. The Guild Master's office is lined floor-to-ceiling with **medical texts in a dozen languages** — a discreet boast about the company that the guild keeps. Cooperative on the surface; carefully unhelpful on the substance.

## Notable Features
- Near the main plaza
- Guild Master's office: floor-to-ceiling library of medical texts in many languages
- Sells small remedies over the counter (a healing poultice was bought here in [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]])

## Notable NPCs
- **Guild Master** (unnamed) — administrator; pleasant, careful, unwilling to commit to anything beyond the registry
- **[[Derich Kölderer]]** — physician registered with the guild ~one month before Session 21 (mentioned, not present)

## Factions Present
- [[Physicians Guild]] (Ferlangen branch)

## Connected Locations
- [[Ferlangen]] (parent town)

## History
- **[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** [[Hildegarde]] presented [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s warrant (upside down, as is her habit); [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] spun a tale about miraculous youth-restoring cures. The administrator had **no record of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]**, but mentioned a single new practitioner — **[[Derich Kölderer]]** — registered ~a month prior. He also volunteered that the **road to [[Wolfenburg]] is plagued by the [[Howling Ones]]**, with a **standing bounty** for anyone willing to fight them. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] purchased a healing poultice on a chafing pretext to maintain cover.

## Notes
- The Guild Master volunteered the **[[Derich Kölderer]]** name and the **[[Howling Ones]] / [[Wolfenburg]] road** warning without being pressed — possibly genuinely helpful, possibly steering the conversation away from something else
- Worth a return visit if the party wants Kölderer's address, his description, or any patient cross-references
