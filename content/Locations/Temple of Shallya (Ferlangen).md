# Temple of Shallya (Ferlangen)

## Description
The local temple of [[Shallya]] in [[Ferlangen]]. Welcoming to the desperate; the priestess at the door in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]] was harried, with wild hair escaping her habit, but accepted [[Herga]] into the temple's care without hesitation.

## Notable Features
- Took in **[[Herga]]** and promised to find a trusted worshipper to escort her home to [[Nordland]]
- Receives Shallyan colleagues from other Ostland temples — including from **[[Wolfenburg]]**, where rare and exotic disease outbreaks have begun
- Forwards regional contacts to investigators when warranted (e.g. **[[Matron Isolde Uricht]]**, the Wolfenburg high priestess)

## Notable NPCs
- **Harried Shallyan priestess** (unnamed) — wild hair escaping her habit; received [[Herga]]; passed on the [[Wolfenburg]] disease rumours and the contact name

## Factions Present

## History
- **[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Party delivered [[Herga]] into the temple's care. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] donated generously. The harried priestess shared news from Shallyan colleagues recently arrived from [[Wolfenburg]]: **rare and exotic diseases appearing simultaneously** in the provincial capital, rumours reaching Ferlangen only a week prior. She named **[[Matron Isolde Uricht]]**, high priestess in [[Wolfenburg]], as the party's best contact. [[Hildegarde]] pressed a dagger into [[Herga]]'s hands at parting.

## Notes
