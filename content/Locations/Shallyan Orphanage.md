# Shallyan Orphanage

## Description
Formally **The Hospital for the Maintenance and Education of Exposed and Deserted Children**. A charitable institution in Wolfenburg, reportedly in difficult financial straits, treating poor patients — including the mysterious eastern-gate disease cases — who can't afford the Physicians' Guild.

Seen up close, it is a fortress in all but name: **three storeys of solid granite**, standing in stark contrast to the sagging half-timbered buildings around it. Every window is barred. The courtyard is enclosed by an **eight-foot spiked wrought-iron fence anchored directly into the stone road**, with no gaps and no climbable sections. At its centre stands a weathered statue of Shallya, her features worn smooth by time, her hand still outstretched with the faint shape of a dove resting in the palm. **The party has scouted the exterior but not been inside.**

## Notable Features

## History
**[[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]:** Named by clerk Danel Koberger as where the eastern-gate disease patients are being sent. The party met Dr. Valera and Dr. Imhot in person at the Physicians' Guild — Dr. Imhot personally invited Berin to visit and review Ganav's surgery. Tied to both the missing-children thread and the disease pattern reported by [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] in [[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]]. The party is considering a visit.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** **Scouted from the outside for the first time**, two blocks from Madame Reitenbach's tenement. [[Hildegarde]] walked the perimeter looking for gaps or climbable sections and found none — the fence is solidly anchored and the walls are granite. With forced or stealthy entry ruled out, the party settled on entering **by invitation**: [[Ganav]] pointedly suggested Hildegarde, a childless woman of a certain age with motherly instincts, might volunteer there or pose as half of a couple looking to adopt. Hildegarde agreed, with exasperated resignation, that it wasn't actually a terrible idea. See [[Infiltrate the Shallyan Orphanage]].
