# Local Bookstore and Scrivener (Wolfenburg)

## Description
A bookshop near [[Schraeder's House]] where [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was a loyal customer for years, exchanging books twice a week.

## Notable Features
- Records of Breytenbach's exchange visits, confirming his reading habits and the date he stopped coming in

## Notable NPCs
- The Bookseller — unnamed proprietor

## Connected Locations
- [[Schraeder's House]] — nearby

## History
**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]:** Visited by the party while canvassing the neighbourhood around Schraeder's former townhouse. The bookseller confirmed [[Gerd Breytenbach]] had worked through the shop's stock of heroic tales, philosophy, medicine, and metallurgy, and had not returned to exchange a book in two weeks — timing that aligns exactly with the townhouse fire. Berin bought a cheap student medical treatise as cover for a future visit to the [[Shallyan Orphanage]]; Ganav bought a text on wound treatment as a gift.
