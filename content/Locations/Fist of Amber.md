# Fist of Amber

## Description
A quiet coaching inn on the road to [[Ferlangen]], its sign a weathered carving of a fist gripping a lump of orange-golden amber. The inn sits beside a working farmstead, but in [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]] the yard was nearly empty — only a few chickens and a patient ox. The farm's outbuildings sat unused and silent, and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] noticed while settling the horses that **all the feed and hay were stored inside the inn's stable** rather than in the farm itself.

## Notable Features
- Carved sign of a fist gripping a lump of amber, worn smooth
- Attached but disused farmstead — feed and hay all kept inside the stable instead
- Low traffic this early in the season — the party were nearly the only paying guests

## Notable NPCs
- The innkeeper — confided to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] the truth about the two travellers and about [[Marretta Schongauer]]
- [[Marretta Schongauer]] — pockmarked, pipe-smoking woman who hired [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] to deliver a sealed letter
- Two unnamed Ostlander travellers — runaway serfs presenting themselves as poor peddlars

## Factions Present
- A cluster of nearby Ostland farmers, who came in for [[Hildegarde]]'s performance

## Connected Locations
- [[Hemp Merchant]] — west along the coast road

## History
- **[[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]:** Party arrived at evening from the [[Hemp Merchant]]. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] sat down with [[Marretta Schongauer]], who hired him to deliver a sealed letter to [[Karl Helpmann]] in [[Ferlangen]] for 5/-. Two Ostlander travellers asked to walk behind the wagon to the city; the party cautiously agreed. [[Hildegarde]] performed for the locals; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] gathered gossip — confirming **[[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s passage on the 17th Nachexen** and **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s passage two nights prior**, and learning of a violent group of highwaymen called the **[[Howling Ones]]**. That night the innkeeper confided to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] that the travellers were **escaped serfs** and Schongauer was a **human trafficker**. Party departed the next morning with the refugees in tow; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] gave the male refugee his own brown cloak.

## Notes
- The strange feed-storage arrangement (all feed and hay inside the inn's stable, the farm buildings unused) is unexplained
- Both [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s and [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s recent stays confirmed here
- Only [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] knows what the innkeeper said in confidence
