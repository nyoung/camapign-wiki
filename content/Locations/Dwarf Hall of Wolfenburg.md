# Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg

## Description
A **stout granite longhouse** flanked by **giant stone dwarf statues**, entered through **twenty-foot-tall doors**. Inside, a hall full of dwarfs drinking malt beer — which **fell completely silent** when the party walked in, every eye fixed on the unusual visitors.

## Notable Features
- **Giant stone dwarf statues** flanking the entrance
- **Twenty-foot-tall doors**
- A drinking hall, where **malt beer** is served
- The seat of the clan elder, [[Borin Anvilhart]]
- The authority controlling **four-tumbler dwarven sewer keys** and the city's **sewer jacks**

## Notable NPCs
- [[Borin Anvilhart]] — clan elder, who presides here
- [[Snorri Belgrimson]] — sewer jack, assigned from here as the party's guide
- An unnamed younger dwarf who reported the linen-wrapped bodies in the tunnels

## Factions Present
- The dwarf clan of [[Wolfenburg]]

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg]] — the city it stands in
- [[Wolfenburg Sewers]] — the dwarfs here work and hold the keys to them

## History
**First visited in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]].** The party came seeking information on their investigation and left with considerably more than they arrived with: word that **a four-tumbler sewer key had been stolen in broad daylight** from one of the hall's sewer jacks, and that a younger dwarf had seen **bodies wrapped in linen and sewn shut** being moved surreptitiously through the tunnels. [[Borin Anvilhart]] loaned them a sewer key for **48 hours under oath of return** and assigned [[Snorri Belgrimson]] as their escort.

## Notes
- The hall responds to **respect and formality** — [[Hildegarde]]'s curtsy preceded a cooperative hearing
- **Oaths matter here.** The borrowed key carries a sworn 48-hour term; failing to return it would presumably carry real consequence with the clan
