# Hasselhund

A small town on the road south from [[Aukrug]] toward [[Dassel]] and [[Wolfenburg]], defended by a palisade. The caravan arrived here on the evening of 8 Jahrdrung 2512, pounding on the gates until a bleary-eyed guard admitted them. The Hofbauer Bodelstein caravan had departed that morning — the promise to wait had meant nothing.

## Key Locations

- **[[The Hanging Highwayman]]** — medium coaching inn; sign depicts a body hanging from a scaffold

## Stradovski's Trail

A serving maid at [[The Hanging Highwayman]] remembered **[[Professor Udo Stradovski|Professor Stradovski]]** well — he passed through roughly two weeks prior with a large merchant caravan and made an impression by noticing her Shallyan dove talisman and offering to "show her a real Shallyan miracle." She declined without hesitation.

## History with the Party

**[[Session 24 - Hawkeye]]:** Arrived late evening 8 Jahrdrung after the ambush. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] rested here. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] gathered road intelligence from local miners; learned about the brutal Dassel–Wolfenburg stretch and three established shelter points. Caravan departed into rain on the morning of 9 Jahrdrung.
