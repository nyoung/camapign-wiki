# Dassel

A small, permanent hamlet of transient farm buildings clustered around a well-populated inn, on the rocky highland road south toward [[Wolfenburg]], the next major waypoint past [[Hasselhund]].

## History with the Party

**[[Session 28 - A Knight to Dismember]]:** The party arrived at sunset after the werewolf ambush, announcing the [[Howling Ones]] defeated and the road clear. Skeptical local farmers were convinced by [[Hildegarde]]'s werewolf fang and [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s armour, and bought the party a round of drinks in celebration. [[Hildegarde]] and [[Ganav]] performed a song and dance at the inn, earning a discounted large room. The party rose at sunrise the next morning and set off for [[Wolfenburg]], with [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] paying out the escort contract in full en route.

## Known Information (player-side)

- Lies on the caravan road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Wolfenburg]]
- The stretch of road **between Dassel and [[Wolfenburg]] is notoriously brutal in wet weather**, sometimes slowing caravans to as little as five miles a day (learned by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] from local miners in [[Session 24 - Hawkeye|Session 24]])
- **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** and the **Hofbauer Bodelstein** company are travelling south toward [[Wolfenburg]] via Dassel, roughly a day ahead of the party
- The lands near Dassel's north spur are claimed by **Lord Jürgen Kimmelman**, according to [[Mathis Haack]]
