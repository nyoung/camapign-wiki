# Dassel

A settlement on the rocky highland road south toward [[Wolfenburg]], the next major waypoint past [[Hasselhund]]. **Not yet reached by the party** as of [[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf|Session 25]] — they are camped at the [[Wayside Shrine]] roughly ten miles short of it.

## Known Information (player-side)

- Lies on the caravan road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Wolfenburg]]
- The stretch of road **between Dassel and [[Wolfenburg]] is notoriously brutal in wet weather**, sometimes slowing caravans to as little as five miles a day (learned by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] from local miners in [[Session 24 - Hawkeye|Session 24]])
- **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** and the **Hofbauer Bodelstein** company are travelling south toward [[Wolfenburg]] via Dassel, roughly a day ahead of the party
- The lands near Dassel's north spur are claimed by **Lord Jürgen Kimmelman**, according to [[Mathis Haack]]
