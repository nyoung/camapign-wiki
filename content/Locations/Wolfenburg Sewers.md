# Wolfenburg Sewers

## Description
The tunnel network beneath [[Wolfenburg]], worked and maintained by **dwarven sewer jacks** out of the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]]. Access is controlled by **four-tumbler dwarven sewer keys**. **Not yet entered by the party** as of [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]] — the descent is the immediate next objective.

## Notable Features
- **Locked by four-tumbler dwarven keys**, held by the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]] — one of which was **stolen in broad daylight**, almost certainly by one of the city's gangs
- The **northern route** connects toward the **catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]**
- [[Borin Anvilhart]] describes that route as **treacherous** — slippery platforms and winding passages
- **Bodies wrapped in linen and sewn shut** have been seen being moved through the tunnels

## Notable NPCs
- [[Snorri Belgrimson]] — the dwarven sewer jack assigned as the party's guide
- [[Borin Anvilhart]] — controls access and loaned the party a key

## Factions Present
- The dwarf sewer jacks of the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg]]
- **Unknown** — whoever is moving the linen-wrapped corpses, and whichever gang stole the key

## Connected Locations
- [[Wolfenburg]] — the city above
- [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg]] — controls the keys and the jacks
- [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)]] — the catacombs beneath it are reachable by the northern route

## History
Learned about in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]] at the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]]. The party holds a **borrowed key on a 48-hour oath** and a dwarven guide, and has not yet gone down.

## Notes
- **🔴 This is the first physical route** connecting the corpse thread — [[Captain Crais]]'s embalmed bodies drifting downriver, and the [[Shallyan Orphanage]] / disease leads — to somewhere the party can actually walk
- **A gang stole a sewer key.** Whoever moved the bodies had a way in; the theft is the likely means
- ⏱️ The **48-hour clock** on the borrowed key runs from [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]
