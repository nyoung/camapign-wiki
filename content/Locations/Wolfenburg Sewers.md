# Wolfenburg Sewers

## Description
The tunnel network beneath [[Wolfenburg]], worked and maintained by **dwarven sewer jacks** out of the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]]. Access is controlled by **four-tumbler dwarven sewer keys**. **First entered in [[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]** — by way of the catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], not from the street.

## Notable Features
- **Locked by four-tumbler dwarven keys**, held by the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]] — one of which was **stolen in broad daylight**, almost certainly by one of the city's gangs
- The **northern route** connects toward the **catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]**
- [[Borin Anvilhart]] describes that route as **treacherous** — slippery platforms and winding passages
- **Bodies wrapped in linen and sewn shut** have been seen being moved through the tunnels

- **[[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]] — the catacomb gate:** a short (4½–5 ft) iron gate with a **non-dwarven lock**, **freshly oiled and heavily used**. The **dwarven security gate on the far side has been wrenched out**. Beyond it, a **round, downward-sloping tunnel** with foul liquid **smeared both ways** by dragging
- A **four-foot side tunnel** — a **makeshift collector** the dwarves built, fed by many small drains from the street — where **two linen-wrapped corpses** and a **note under four silver coins** were found. **Downhill** is the **main channel**, running along the road past the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] to the main bridge; **uphill** is a dead end with a **punched-through hole** to [[The Ghoul Cavern]]
- The dwarves **rerouted a mountain stream** to keep the main channel flowing to the river
- The **Temple of Shallya is half a mile or less** from the catacomb exit, by [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]]'s map

- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]] — the main channels:** **arched vaults** of dwarf height (5′5″–6′; taller humans brush the walls), **yard-wide walkways** either side of a **6–8-foot channel** of fast black water. Holes at intervals lead up to **street drains**, with **no ladders**
- **[[Screaming Mushrooms]]** infest the **eastern** channel, an alarm for anyone passing. None in the western channel
- **The laundry drain:** a **20-inch ceramic pipe**, angled steeply up off a twisty side passage, carrying soapy lye water about **20 feet** up to a **liftable floor grate** in the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]'s laundry. [[Ganav]] has **chalked and loosened** it
- **The junction:** where two great channels cross, with crisscrossed arches, **sluice mechanisms** to divert the flow, and **rusty iron bridges**. A regular **[[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurker]]** haunt, until they agreed to stay away for a week
- **The hidden street door:** about two storeys of stairs up from the junction, a dwarf-locked door (*half a turn one way, a full turn back*) opening onto **a main street between two businesses**, disguised as an entrance to one of them; roughly **halfway between the Temple District and the waterfront**
- **The stream access:** the dwarves redirected a **tributary of the Wolfen** underground to flush the sewers. It enters behind **a large grate**, the **only sewer route out of the city**, which takes **several dwarves several hours** to remove
