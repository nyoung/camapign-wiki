# Madame Reitenbach

## Description
An old woman living alone in a cramped tenement in Wolfenburg's ethnic neighbourhoods. Her door, crooked in its frame, carries a small pine bough wreath.

## Role / Occupation
[[Gerd Breytenbach]]'s mother. Widow of a dock worker.

## Personality
Grief-stricken but instinctively hospitable — fell back on the habits of a lifetime, setting water to boil for tea and offering crackers to the party even while mourning. Her grief was convincing enough that [[Ganav]], who initially suspected her of holding something back, revised his opinion. When told the body might not be her son's, she moved from wailing to a careful, fragile hope rather than seizing on it.

## Relationships
- Mother of [[Gerd Breytenbach]]
- Widow of a dock worker who taught Gerd to read and died in the **Red Pox plague of '02**
- Knows of [[Louisa May]] as a maid her son was close to

## History with the Party
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Found via a tip from [[Fazzini House]]'s servant and directions from a local dwarf and the tenement's landlord. Hildegarde disarmed herself and approached alone to avoid frightening her, and gently delivered news of her son's death. Despite her grief, she invited the party inside and began preparing tea.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** Interviewed at length. Told carefully that the body might not be Gerd's, she shifted from grief to cautious hope. She described a quiet, bookish son who frequented the booksellers near the **Temple District**, and a dock-worker husband who had taught the boy to read before dying in the Red Pox of '02. Most importantly, she recalled that on Gerd's **last visit, about a week prior**, he had made an offhand mention of **helping out a questionable group he called "the Lurkers"** — a comment that had clearly been weighing on his mind. She also named **[[Louisa May]]**, a maid in the same household he had been close to. The party donated what food they had before leaving; Hildegarde listened at the door on the way out and heard only silence.

## Known Information
- Her son Gerd may still be alive — the morgue body does not match him
- **Gerd told her he was "helping out" the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]** about a week before he vanished, and it was troubling him
- Gerd was close to a maid named [[Louisa May]] in the same household
- Gerd was bookish and used the booksellers near the Temple District
- Her husband was a dock worker who taught Gerd to read; he died in the **Red Pox plague of '02**
