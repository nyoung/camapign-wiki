# Magistrate Gawinus

## Description
Known throughout Wolfenburg as "the hanging judge" — a stern official who presides with the air of a man who has already made up his mind.

## Role / Occupation
Presiding magistrate at the [[Wolfenburg Courthouse]]. Strictly enforces Ostland law, which makes no distinction between those who wield arms and those who merely support those who do.

## History with the Party
**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge]]:** Presided over [[Ganav]]'s hearing. Unmoved by Ganav's eloquent defense and marriage proposal to [[Hildegarde]], he sentenced him to death by hanging. When [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] invoked a rarely used statute to take personal legal custody of Ganav, Gawinus set bail at three gold crowns and accepted payment, releasing Ganav into Berin's custody.

## Known Information
- Strict, literal interpreter of Ostland law
- Can be worked around via a rarely used statute allowing a person of standing to take legal custody of a condemned prisoner (for a price and personal liability)
