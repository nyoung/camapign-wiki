# Anselm Geissmar

## Description
A merchant living just around the corner from [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s former townhouse, at [[Fazzini House]]. Not yet met directly.

## Role / Occupation
Employs the one manservant known to have been a close friend of [[Gerd Breytenbach]].

## Relationships
- Employer of Breytenbach's one known close friend (unnamed manservant)
- Possibly the employer of, or confused with, the servant "Anselm" the party met at [[Fazzini House]] in [[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]] — see Open Questions

## History with the Party
Named by neighbourhood grocers, bakers, and cheese sellers in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]] during the party's canvass around Schraeder's former townhouse, as the employer of Gerd Breytenbach's one close friend. The party did not visit him or his household that session.

**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** The party visited [[Fazzini House]] and spoke with a servant who gave his name as "Anselm" — confirming Schraeder's death and claiming (contrary to the party's own Session 33 conclusion) that Gerd Breytenbach had also died. It's not yet clear at the table whether this servant is Geissmar himself, his manservant sharing a first name with his employer, or something else — Geissmar has still never been directly, unambiguously identified.

## Known Information
- Lives around the corner from Schraeder's former townhouse, at [[Fazzini House]]
- His manservant is the most promising direct lead on the (probably still alive) [[Gerd Breytenbach]]
- A servant at his household, "Anselm," told the party Breytenbach is dead — contradicting the party's own findings

## Open Questions
- Does he or his manservant know where Breytenbach is now?
- Is the "Anselm" the party met at Fazzini House Geissmar's manservant (the actual lead), Geissmar himself, or a naming mix-up? And is his claim that Breytenbach is dead true, mistaken, or a deliberate lie?
