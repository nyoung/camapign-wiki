# Lucas Pollack

## Description
A man in his **early forties**, **graying hair**, **heavy stubble**, hung with **an air of detached disillusionment** that seems to drape him like a second coat. A witch hunter of the [[Fraternal Order of Witch-Hunters]], paired with [[Christina Asper]] in [[Ferlangen]]. First met in person over dinner at [[Frau Busch's Boarding House]] in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]].

## Role / Occupation
Per [[Christina Asper]], Pollack was **left behind in [[Ferlangen]]** to track [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s movements through the region while Asper came west to meet the incoming party at the [[Royal Wolf Inn]]. Per the [[Abbess of Verena (Ferlangen)|Abbess of Verena]], he has produced **no useful leads** in the city.

## Personality
Where [[Christina Asper|Asper]] is sharp, recording, exact, Pollack is the opposite: **mild, unfocused, only intermittently interested**. Listened to the party's news of the [[Wolfenburg]] disease outbreak with **mild, unfocused interest** while Asper recorded it precisely. Reads as a senior man going through the motions — a contrast that the party will likely find informative.

## Relationships
- **[[Christina Asper]]** — colleague; she answered "Hardly" when [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] asked how well she knew him — they are paired professionally, not personally close
- **[[Fraternal Order of Witch-Hunters]]** — his order

## History with the Party
Mentioned in [[Session 17 - Asper-ated|Session 17]] and [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]; **first met in person** in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]] at [[Frau Busch's Boarding House]], when [[Christina Asper|Asper]] brought him to dinner. Briefly introduced; listened to the party share the news of the [[Wolfenburg]] disease cluster but offered no observable reaction beyond mild interest.
