# Lucas Pollack

## Description
A man in his **early forties**, **graying hair**, **heavy stubble**, hung with **an air of detached disillusionment** that seems to drape him like a second coat. A witch hunter of the [[Fraternal Order of Witch-Hunters]], paired with [[Christina Asper]] in [[Ferlangen]]. First met in person over dinner at [[Frau Busch's Boarding House]] in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]].

## Role / Occupation
Per [[Christina Asper]], Pollack was **left behind in [[Ferlangen]]** to track [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s movements through the region while Asper came west to meet the incoming party at the [[Royal Wolf Inn]]. Per the [[Abbess of Verena (Ferlangen)|Abbess of Verena]], he has produced **no useful leads** in the city.

## Personality
Where [[Christina Asper|Asper]] is sharp, recording, exact, Pollack is the opposite: **mild, unfocused, only intermittently interested**. Listened to the party's news of the [[Wolfenburg]] disease outbreak with **mild, unfocused interest** while Asper recorded it precisely. Reads as a senior man going through the motions — a contrast that the party will likely find informative.

## Relationships
- **[[Christina Asper]]** — colleague; she answered "Hardly" when [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] asked how well she knew him — they are paired professionally, not personally close
- **[[Fraternal Order of Witch-Hunters]]** — his order

## History with the Party
Mentioned in [[Session 17 - Asper-ated|Session 17]] and [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]; **first met in person** in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]] at [[Frau Busch's Boarding House]], when [[Christina Asper|Asper]] brought him to dinner. Briefly introduced; listened to the party share the news of the [[Wolfenburg]] disease cluster but offered no observable reaction beyond mild interest.

## Session 25 — Reckless Charge
- **Charged the ambush with reckless aggression** as it broke on the slope between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]] — a marked contrast to his usual detached manner

## Session 27 — Two Fumbles, One Execution
- Charged into the fray and **fumbled his attack twice in a row** — the party started wondering if he had spent the night on the wine
- Executed a bandit who attempted to surrender, signalling plainly that **no quarter would be given**; a sharp contrast to his usual detached passivity
- The burst of reckless aggression first seen in Session 25 continues, now edged with something colder

## Session 28 — The Killing Blow
- Continued his run of reckless, cold aggression — drove his sword into the transformed werewolf's leg with enough force to shatter bone, delivering the **killing blow** that ended the fight
- Guided the party through [[Wolfenburg]]'s streets to the Temple of Verena the following session

## Session 36 — At the Docks
- Logged as present for the party's attempt to seize [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] at the [[Wolfenburg Docks]]; no specific action of his is recorded in the session notes
- **⚠️ Unattributed betrayal.** *"Some of the allies the party had been counting on had turned against them"* during the fight, and **the notes do not name who.** Pollack is one of three allies logged present, alongside [[Christina Asper]] and [[Gromril van Aelst]]. Worth weighing against his drift since Session 25 — the reckless charges, the execution of a surrendering bandit in [[Session 27 - Fight!|Session 27]], and the cold aggression that has replaced his earlier detachment. **Not established as his doing; awaiting the GM's confirmation.**
