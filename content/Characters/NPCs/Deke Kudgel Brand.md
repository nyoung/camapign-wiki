# Deke "Kudgel" Brand

## Description
A thin, gangly man in an orangish-red soft hat. Fast on his feet, quick with a sword when cornered, and quicker to be disarmed by absurdity.

## Role / Occupation
Unclear — fled toward "the Steel Ring" (likely the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]) after the encounter, suggesting an affiliation or at least a destination there.

## Personality
Wary but not without humor. Held a sword to a stranger's throat one moment and was laughing off his sword arm the next when Hildegarde returned his hat.

## Relationships
- Pursued by three [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] members, who accused someone of taking stolen lottery money
- Possibly connected to the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]

## History with the Party
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Burst out of a Wolfenburg side alley at a dead sprint, a crossbow bolt cracking into a post beside his head, three cloaked men close behind. Dove under a hay cart to escape; Hildegarde followed through the muck and came up under his sword. She returned his fallen hat with a smile ("I believe you dropped your hat, sir") — disarmed, he lowered his blade, gave his name, and declined the party's offer of protection, heading off toward the Steel Ring alone.

## Known Information
- Goes by "Deke," also known as "Kudgel Brand"
- Was being chased by three tattooed men matching the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]'s markings
- Headed toward "the Steel Ring" afterward — likely the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]

## Open Questions
- Is he a member of the Steel Fist Thief Ring, a target of both gangs, or something else?
- Did he actually take the stolen lottery money the Lurkers were hunting?
