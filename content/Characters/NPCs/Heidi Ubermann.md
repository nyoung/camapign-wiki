# Heidi Ubermann

## Description
A knight / Sigmarite agent of the [[Ordo Sancti Inquisita]], travelling as part of [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s contingent on the caravan north and then south toward [[Wolfenburg]]. Capable and level-headed, but passed over for advancement in favour of less able peers.

## Role / Occupation
Sigmarite agent / knight, subordinate to [[Danderich Flisshausten]]. More capable than her standing in the order reflects.

## Personality
Visibly resentful of [[Danderich Flisshausten]] and the institution that has overlooked her. More skilled and level-headed than [[Herman Wurt]] (a sycophant she has outpaced) yet repeatedly passed over. She does not share Danderich's arrogance — she is willing to acknowledge real threats and seek allies, and prefers to raise concerns privately rather than publicly.

## Relationships
- **[[Danderich Flisshausten]]** — superior; she considers him an overblown hothead, underqualified for his rank, and resents being passed over for him
- **[[Herman Wurt]]** — colleague; a sycophant she has outpaced in ability
- **[[Erika Sÿfrid von Drakkenheim]]** — dined together in [[Arenburg]]; Erika read her situation accurately
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** — she approached him privately on the road south; seems to view him as the most capable and reasonable member of the party

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 05 - The Road North]]:** Observed by [[Erika Sÿfrid von Drakkenheim]] at dinner in [[Arenburg]] — Erika identified her as far more capable than [[Herman Wurt]] but resentful at being overlooked; flagged as a potential point of contact within the Sigmarite contingent.
- **[[Session 24 - Hawkeye]]:** After [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] shared road intelligence with [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] (who dismissed it), Heidi pulled Berin aside privately and confided that she considers Danderich an **overblown hothead, underqualified for his rank**, and that she fears the caravan is walking into something far worse than the last ambush — a warning borne out at the choke point. This was the party's first substantial interaction with her.
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]:** Lent by [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] (along with [[Herman Wurt|Herman]]) to fill out the four-shift night watch at the [[Wayside Shrine]].

## Known Information
- Knight / agent of the Ordo Sancti Inquisita

- Resentful of Danderich and the current hierarchy; does not trust his judgment
- Correctly feared a worse ambush ahead before the choke point attack
- Has close insight into Danderich's capabilities and limitations from travelling with him
