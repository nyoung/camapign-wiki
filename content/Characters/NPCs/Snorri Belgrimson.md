# Snorri Belgrimson

## Description
A dwarven **sewer jack** of [[Wolfenburg]], assigned by clan elder [[Borin Anvilhart]] to escort the party through the **northern sewers**.

## Role / Occupation
Sewer jack — one of the dwarfs who works and maintains [[Wolfenburg Sewers|Wolfenburg's sewers]]. Assigned as the party's **guide** for the descent toward the catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]].

## Personality
Dry, blunt and competent. Nervous of the spirits but **sets his jaw and presses on** (*"let's keep going"*). Visibly **offended** by the party's talk of "filthy, corrupted channels" — he gave the GM's line *"book of grudges"* to Berin. Proud of the dwarves' engineering, and generous with the map but guarded: he lets the party **note** the relevant section, **not transcribe it all**.

## Relationships
- **[[Borin Anvilhart]]** — his clan elder, who assigned him to the party
- **The party** — their guide for the sewer expedition

## History with the Party
Assigned as their escort in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]] and **went underground with them in [[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]** as the fifth member of the party. Led the way down the catacombs, offered his dwarven lamp, **picked the lock** on the sewer gate, produced a **sewer map**, and fought the ghouls in [[The Ghoul Cavern]] — where his **hooked sewer spear** (a grate-clearing tool, not a weapon) got him into trouble: he **fumbled twice in a row and hit himself**. In [[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]] he redeemed himself by **killing the last ghoul**, then broke a **[[Forged Dwarf Key|forged copy of the dwarves' key]]** found on dead Lurkers, and guided the party through the main sewers to the Temple of Shallya's laundry drain and out a hidden street door.

## Known Information
- Knows the **northern sewer route** toward the catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], which [[Borin Anvilhart]] describes as treacherous — slippery platforms and winding passages
- It is not recorded whether he is the same sewer jack from whom the **four-tumbler key was stolen**
- **Has never been in the catacombs** and had **never seen a spirit before**; the catacombs are *"the old caves the manlings used"*
- The catacomb-side gate's lock is **not a dwarven one**; a **dwarven security gate should be on the far side** and **has been wrenched out** — *"nobody should be down here; no work has been done on this side in months"*
- The dwarves **rerouted a mountain stream** through the main sewer channel to keep it flowing to the river; the side tunnels are a **makeshift collector** they built
- From the catacomb exit, the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] is **half a mile or less** by the sewer branch
- **A [[Forged Dwarf Key|forgery]] of the dwarves' key exists**: skilled, accurate work. *"This isn't the one that we made"* *(Session 39)*
- Blames the **[[Screaming Mushrooms]]** on **goblins** (*"gobbos"*) on hallucinogenic mushrooms; goblin bands occasionally get into the sewers, never a full infestation *(Session 39)*
- The jacks have been **working a collapse on the other side of town** and have neglected this side, which ran without incident for months *(Session 39)*
- Knows the **laundry drain** into the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] and a **hidden street door** out of the sewers (dwarf key: *half a turn one way, a full turn back*) *(Session 39)*
- **The only way out of the city by sewer is the stream access**: a redirected tributary of the Wolfen behind **a large grate** that takes several dwarves several hours to remove. He doubts the dwarves would do it *"just to let you manlings out"* *(Session 39)*
- **Will not go back past the catacomb spirits** *(Session 39)*

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **First appearance.** Assigned by [[Borin Anvilhart]] to escort the party through the northern sewers, alongside the loan of a sewer key for 48 hours

## Session 38 — A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make
- **First time underground with the party.** Lit a dwarven lamp; led the descent through the catacombs; **kept going** when the spirits appeared
- Told them he had **never seen anything like** the spirits and **doesn't go into the catacombs**
- **Examined the sewer gate**: not a dwarven lock, and the **dwarven security gate on the far side was gone**. **Picked the lock** with a small set of tools
- Produced his **sewer map** and traced the route to the Temple of Shallya; let the party make notes of that section only
- Fought in the ghoul cavern with his **hooked spear** — **fumbled twice in a row and hit himself**

## Session 39 — Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently
- **Killed the last ghoul**: hooked its leg with his short spear, tripped it, and drove the blade through its throat, spraying blood across [[Hildegarde]]. He had been **poisoned** himself
- Seized the **[[Forged Dwarf Key]]** from the dead Lurkers, declared it a skilled forgery, **snapped it**, and swore to find the forgers: *"This is manling trash. We need to find these fiends and take our key back from them."* Took his **eight-shilling share** of the coins in the cavern
- **Refused to go back past the spirits**; led the party through the main sewers instead, stomping every [[Screaming Mushrooms|screaming mushroom]] in reach and blaming goblins
- Showed them the **laundry drain** into the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]], **spotted the Lurkers** at the junction first, and let the party out through a **hidden street door** with the dwarf key
- Explained the **stream-access grate**, the only sewer way out of the city, and doubted the dwarves would open it for manlings
- **Poisoned and wounded**, bade the party good night and went home to the dwarven quarter alone

## [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]
- **Collected from the Dwarf Longhouse** by [[Ganav]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] (a table of dwarves grumbled and laughed at them). Led them to the **same sewer door**; opened it with the **dwarf key**, *a half turn one way and a turn back*
- **Spotted red reflective eyes** in the dark: *"Just keep going. Ignore them. We need a bit of a rat hunt down here"* — hinted at worse than were-rats
- **Cursed the party** on learning nobody had fetched the priest of Mórr; led them back up
- Carried his **davich lamp** (candle, flares up and extinguishes itself in gas) and kept his **sewer pole-arm** ready: *a tunnel fighter*
- **Refused to let [[Ganav]] touch his lock tools** (*"you don't touch a dwarf's tools"*), but accepted assistance; **opened the human gate** into the catacombs after a minute or two
- Agreed to come back past the catacomb spirits: *"I guess I've come this far."* The party has reached out to the Sewerjacks Guild's **"noble wardens"**, and he snorted

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- Led the party up through the **winding catacombs** and **dropped back** as they neared the spirits' place, calling directions from behind. He **opened the human gate** at the start of the session
