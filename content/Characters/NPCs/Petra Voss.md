# Petra Voss

## Description
A woman in a black Lurker cloak with a **sling** she turned over in her hand as a nervous habit. Captured in [[Session 42 - I Almost Killed You All|Session 42]]. Willpower 32.

## Role / Occupation
A [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurker]] under [[Karel Vantz]]. Carried **knuckle dusters** (a 50% chance in the GM's NPC generator) and a dagger.

## Personality
Defiant at first ("shooting daggers from her eyes"), but not fanatical: she faltered visibly at news of dead companions and hardened her face again. Resents Azmus Beham for breaking the deal. Gave what she knew in pieces.

## Relationships
- Under [[Karel Vantz]]; friend of the vanished body-movers **Gunther** and **Fritz**
- Does not know [[Anselm Rathke]] beyond the gang
- Avoids the orphanage, like the rest of the Lurkers

## History with the Party
**[[Session 42 - I Almost Killed You All|Session 42]]:** Slung at [[Ganav]] and [[Hildegarde]], then fumbled into her own knuckle dusters twice when Hilde clubbed her and was stunned and tied up. Interrogated (Boris's weak Intimidate): Beham stopped paying and left that morning; the Lurkers dumped bodies in the Wolfen at 3 a.m.; no Lurker burned Gerd's house; she had never heard of the Medical Union; the orphanage swallows sick boys. Handed to the [[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]] for a **3-shilling bounty**.

## Known Information
- Gerd Breytenbach used to slip Karel tips about the Physicians Guild
- "Nobody leaves the Lurkers" when they know the operations, so [[Hilde Muntz]] is hunted
- Rumours of the Shallyan orphanage: boys escape and say new arrivals fall sick and vanish overnight
- **Not in the module**; confirmed by the GM
