# Louisa May

## Description
Jaie Schraeder's maid.

## Role / Occupation
Presumed to have died in the fire that gutted Schraeder's townhouse, alongside butler Gerd Breytenbach.

## Relationships
- Servant of [[Jaie Schraeder]]
- Died alongside fellow servant Gerd Breytenbach
- **Was close to [[Gerd Breytenbach]]** — per his mother, [[Madame Reitenbach]] ([[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]])

## History with the Party
Named by clerk Danel Koberger during the party's investigation of Jaie Schraeder's death in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]. Her body was too badly burned for formal identification by the City Watch.

## Known Information
- Presumed dead in the townhouse fire, alongside butler Gerd Breytenbach
- Body too badly burned for formal identification
- Killed by a clean, professional throat-slash ([[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]] autopsy) — used in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]] as the comparison that exposed the Breytenbach body as a mismatch
- **[[Gerd Breytenbach]] was close to her**, per his mother ([[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]])

## Open Questions
- Did Gerd's closeness to her make him a target, a witness, or a conspirator? She was killed cleanly and professionally; his death was staged with a substitute body
