# Abbess of Verena (Ferlangen)

## Description
A stern woman who peers over her spectacles from behind a great hardwood desk in the marble-fronted [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] in [[Ferlangen]]. Met by the party in [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]].

## Role / Occupation
Abbess of the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] in [[Ferlangen]]. Receives reports from agents in the field, holds the party's wages, and oversees the Verenan investigation arc in the city.

## Personality
Stern, unhurried, unmoved by emotional appeals. Heard out the party's defence against [[Christina Asper]]'s damning report with patience but visible scepticism. Pragmatic enough to clarify the actual chain of command in the party's favour without softening her tone.

## Relationships
- **[[Christina Asper]]** — sent her a full, unflattering report on the party
- **[[Lucas Pollack]]** — present in [[Ferlangen]]; per the Abbess, has not yet produced useful leads
- **The party** — formally the lead investigators on the [[Find Professor Stradovski|Stradovski]] mission
- **The Merchants Guild of [[Ferlangen]]** — several senior members are devoted patrons of the temple; she suggested the guild as the right avenue for the counterfeit-coin investigation

## History with the Party
**[[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]:** Greeted the party with [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s report already in front of her. The accusations: the party had withheld heretical artefacts, goaded Asper into acting as a judge in a matter at an inn, and spoken ill of her to soldiers. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] defended the group's conduct. The Abbess listened, then **clarified the chain of command**: the party holds the right of command over the investigation; Asper is a *resource*, not a director. She also noted with a pointed look at [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] that he alone had been named by Asper as a trustworthy ally.

She confirmed that **neither [[Christina Asper|Asper]] nor [[Lucas Pollack]] had located useful leads** in [[Ferlangen]], and that the **missing Ulrican knights had not yet arrived**. She suggested the **Merchants Guild** as the right avenue for the [[Beeckerhoven]] counterfeit-coin investigation. The party then collected their wages and were billeted at **Frau Busch's boarding house**.
