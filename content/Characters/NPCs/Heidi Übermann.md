# Heidi Übermann

## Description
A knight of the [[Ordo Sancti Inquisita]], travelling as part of [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s Sigmarite contingent. Previously mentioned in the existing NPC files as a subordinate of Danderich's who is "more capable and resentful of being passed over." Met properly in [[Session 24 - Hawkeye|Session 24]] when she pulled [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] aside privately.

## Role / Occupation
Knight of the [[Ordo Sancti Inquisita]], subordinate to [[Danderich Flisshausten]] on the road south to [[Wolfenburg]].

## Personality
More capable and level-headed than her superior. Does not share Danderich's arrogance — she is willing to acknowledge real threats and seek allies. Pulled [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] aside privately to confide her concerns rather than raise them publicly.

## Relationships
- **[[Danderich Flisshausten]]** — superior; she considers him an overblown hothead, underqualified for his rank
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** — she approached him privately; seems to view him as the most capable/reasonable member of the party

## History with the Party
**[[Session 24 - Hawkeye]]:** After [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] shared road intelligence with [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] (who dismissed it), Heidi pulled Berin aside privately and confided that she considers Danderich an **overblown hothead, underqualified for his rank**, and that she fears the caravan is walking into something far worse than the last ambush. This was the party's first substantial interaction with her.

## Known Information
- Knight of the Ordo Sancti Inquisita
- Does not trust Danderich's judgment
- Fears a worse ambush ahead (said before the choke point attack)
- Has insight into Danderich's capabilities and limitations from travelling closely with him
