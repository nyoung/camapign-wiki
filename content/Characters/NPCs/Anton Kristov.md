# Anton Kristov

## Description
A small-time import-exporter operating between [[Ferlangen]], [[Wolfenburg]], and [[Kislev]] — and, per **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**, running **people** along the same route as his goods. The party first heard the name spoken aloud as "**Anton Christoph**"; Gromril's correction landed somewhere in the middle of a brawl debrief, but the registered name appears to be **Anton Kristov**.

## Role / Occupation
Import-export agent. Trade route: **[[Ferlangen]] → [[Wolfenburg]] → [[Kislev]]**. Goods on the surface; people, per [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]], in practice.

## Relationships
- **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]** — co-named on the trafficking thread
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** — knows him by reputation; named him to the party
- **High-class brothel worker** ([[Ferlangen]]) — almost certainly on her list, by implication

## History with the Party
**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] mentioned the name (as "Anton Christoph") to [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]; the gnome confirmed him as a **small-time import-exporter** running goods and people on the [[Ferlangen]]→[[Wolfenburg]]→[[Kislev]] road.

## Known Information
