# Dr. von Bora

## Description
Weathered and tanned, with a well-trimmed beard and practical clothing that stands in sharp contrast to the opulence of the [[Aman Estate]] where he's currently staying. A surprise to meet in person — not the pampered academic the setting suggests.

## Role / Occupation
A doctor of spirituality (not medicine) from the University of Altdorf, seeking bodyguards and a secretary for a research expedition to the Kislev–Ostland border to conduct a spiritual ethnography of the peoples living there. Advertised via the town crier.

## Relationships
- **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]]** — describes him as "one of the good ones"; wrote the party's letter of introduction to him
- **[[Ganav]]** — Berin advocated for him as a potential expedition guide
- **[[Christina Asper]]** — Berin privately warned von Bora (in Classical) about her zealotry

## History with the Party
**[[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]]:** Named by the town crier as seeking secretarial guards; [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] wrote the party a letter of introduction to him.

**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]:** The party called on him at the [[Aman Estate]]. He agreed to accompany them to [[Ganav]]'s hearing and lend what influence he could, after Berin vouched for Ganav's skill as a healer and warned him privately about [[Christina Asper]]'s zealotry.

## Known Information
- Academic title, not medical — clarified this himself when the party assumed otherwise
- Planning an expedition to the Kislev–Ostland border

## Notes
- *Naming note (resolved):* GM Assistant's session export had introduced him as "Dr. Yuberos von Bora" in [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]], inconsistent with the town crier's "Dr. Hubertus von Bora" in [[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]]. Cross-checked against the source module (*A Private War*), his canonical name is **Doktor Hubertus von Bora** — confirmed via Find & Replace correction applied directly in GM Assistant.
