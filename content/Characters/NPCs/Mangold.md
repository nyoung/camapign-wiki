# Mangold

## Description
A dwarf **slayer** with an **orange mohawk** and **tattooed arms**, carrying himself with the bearing of someone who has never once considered backing down from anything. Walks with weapons close to hand — produced an **axe in one hand and hammer in the other** without breaking stride when he came charging back into the alley. Said almost nothing the entire encounter; charges thieves on sight; nearly took [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s head off before the gnome shouted him down.

## Role / Occupation
Bodyguard to the gnome merchant **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**.

## Personality
Wordless and direct. Will pursue a thief on instinct — even at the cost of leaving his charge undefended. Reacts first, identifies friends second; only Gromril's voice stopped him swinging at [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] after the fight. Returned the recovered purse without ceremony.

## Relationships
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** — his charge; the only voice he listens to
- **[[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]]** — fellow member of Gromril's small road party
- **The party** — owes them nothing yet; nearly killed [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] by reflex on his return

## History with the Party
**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** First seen escorting [[Gromril van Aelst]] down the street opposite [[Sam Bugman's Brewery]]. When a thief cut Gromril's purse, Mangold gave chase without a word — leaving the gnome to the waiting thugs. He returned moments after the brawl with the recovered purse, axe and hammer drawn, and very nearly struck [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] before Gromril called him off.

## Session 25 — Battle Frenzy on the Slope
- Entered a **thunderous battle frenzy** as the second ambush broke, clashing his axe and hammer together in a bone-rattling rhythm and charging up the slope
- One of only two (with [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]) **unaffected by the monstrous wolf's supernatural terror-howl**
- The treacherous rocky ground fought him as hard as the outlaws — he slipped and stumbled, but ground forward regardless, felling outlaws with both weapons
- When the fighting ended, his frenzy spent, he **collapsed and lay still for several minutes**, barely able to move

## Session 26 — A Coiled Spring
- Roused by [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] during the night harassment, he **woke half-strangling Boris by reflex** before recognising him — then signalled, in hand-signs, that **four or five scouts** were probing the perimeter, plainly itching to wade into the trees after them
- When the party settled on baiting a counter-ambush, he was **practically vibrating with anticipation** for the fight to come
