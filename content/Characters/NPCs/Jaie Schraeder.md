# Jaie Schraeder

## Description
The former first secretary of the Wolfenburg Physicians Guild, in that role for over a decade.

## Role / Occupation
Deceased. Recently killed under suspicious circumstances, allegedly involving another visiting physician; his home was burned to the ground, apparently to destroy evidence.

## History with the Party
Named by [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] at the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] in [[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]] as one of three troubling threads in the city. She confirmed he had little connection to Dr. von Bora beyond shared Physicians Guild membership.

In [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]] the party investigated in person: clerk Danel Koberger revealed Schraeder received a letter purporting to be from his long-dead, penniless father on his last day alive (31st Nachexen), then vanished; his townhouse burned the next day, killing his servants Gerd Breytenbach and Louisa May; his own stabbed body turned up the day after that on the road toward Kislev, staged to look like a bandit killing. A search of **[[Schraeder's House]]** turned up a hidden, uneducated Classical-language copy of **[[Professor Udo Stradovski]]**'s disease research, plus a crude blood-painted wolf clearly meant to frame the Howling Ones. The Watch's own evidence box held further Classical documents explicitly naming Stradovski.

In [[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]] the party examined his body directly at the [[Garden of Mórr]], alongside his two servants: he was killed by a single sword thrust straight through the center of his chest into his heart, with almost no defensive wounds — the work of a masterful assassin or supremely skilled warrior, not a bandit. All three bodies were confirmed dead *before* the fire was set; the fire was staged purely to hide the murders.

## Known Information
- Received a letter apparently from his dead, penniless father the morning he disappeared (31st Nachexen)
- His townhouse burned the next day, killing servants Gerd Breytenbach and Louisa May
- His body was found the day after that, stabbed and robbed, staged as a bandit killing, near the [[Forest Inn (Brein Kloff)|Forest Inn]] toward Kislev
- Directly linked to [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] via a hidden copy of his disease research, found at the townhouse
- Appointed to his post by Professor Melancthon, who retired abruptly to Altdorf five years ago
- **Autopsy ([[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]]):** killed by a single expert sword thrust through the heart, almost no defensive wounds; killed before the fire was set, not by it

## Open Questions
- Is Stradovski himself the "dead father" who wrote the letter? Berin theorizes Stradovski may use life-extension treatments on himself, which would explain why a letter from a supposedly dead man didn't surprise Schraeder
- Who searched the townhouse a second time, after the killers/arsonists? Nothing further was taken
- Why stage a Howling Ones frame-up specifically?
