# Reiner Kolber

## Description
A small, sharp-eyed street orphan who runs a network of informant children in Wolfenburg he calls the "Wolfen boys."

## Role / Occupation
Leader of a street-urchin intelligence network. Approached Berin directly in a plaza, unprompted, having apparently already been watching for the party — suggesting Berin's earlier arrangement to have street children search for [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] reached him.

## Personality
Business-like for a child — negotiated payment (4 silver shillings up front, 4 more on confirmation) before delivering anything, then led the party through a maze of back alleys with clear familiarity with the city's underside.

## Relationships
- **[[Berin Garibaldi]]** — approached him directly with the lead; likely the contact behind Berin's street-urchin search arrangement from [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]
- **Eyeball** — one of his boys, personally watched the boarding house for a full day

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth]]** — found the party in the plaza after their release from the City Watch. Led them to **[[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House]]**, where his boy Eyeball had been watching a raven-haired woman and a broad-shouldered bodyguard (likely [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]). Warned the party, in a low voice, to stay away from a local orphanage where children have been taken and never seen again. His network stayed on to watch the boarding house and reported movement that evening.

- **[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist]]** — waited for the party at the temple plaza by a statue of a mounted soldier (someone had skewered a gourd on the sword's tip) and delivered the session's most urgent intelligence: **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]] has made a deal with a barge captain docked at the far north end of the city and plans to flee at sundown**, just before the river nets are raised. He also confirmed that the **[[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]'s main territory is the southwest tenements along the city wall**, and warned that even priestly garb earns little trust there after dark. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] paid him and set him to keep watching Teuschel's residence and send word to the [[Tree and Rut Inn]] by end of day.

## Known Information
- Runs the "Wolfen boys" — a network of street-orphan informants across Wolfenburg
- Knows about the missing-children/orphanage thread and considers it dangerous enough to warn adults away from
- Tracked [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s escape arrangement: a barge captain at the far north end of the city, departure at **sundown**, ahead of the river nets going up
- Knows the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]] holds the southwest tenements along the city wall, and that the district is hostile to outsiders after dark
