# Hilde Muntz

## Description
A young woman, and a **close friend of [[Christina Asper]]** — the personal tie Asper finally admitted to at the **Altar of Solkan** in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]. **Met by the party in [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]].**

## Role / Occupation
A **former member of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** who has since **sought a calling with the Shallyans**. She is currently **trapped inside the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]** in [[Wolfenburg]], unable to leave.

## Personality
**Calm and practiced.** Accepted the plan at once on Asper's word, took her leave of Nina with a hug, and went down the laundry drain without much comment: *"you have a feeling she's done such as this before."* Protective of [[Marla Hengist|Marla]], whom she pushed back for the first time (*"Hush"*). She wouldn't want to lead the Steel Fists to the party. [[Lucas Pollack]] implied she takes the friendship with [[Christina Asper|Asper]] less seriously than Asper does, though this was said in the course of needling Asper.

## Relationships
- **[[Christina Asper]]** — close friend. Asper asked the party for help on her behalf, describing it as a **deeply personal matter** — the first time she has asked them for anything for herself
- **[[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** — her former gang, now hunting her
- **[[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]** — also hunting her, over the stolen lottery purse
- **[[Cult of Shallya]]** — the calling she has turned to; the temple currently shelters her

## History with the Party
**[[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]:** [[Hildegarde]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] reached her in the long-stay ward of the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] (she was tending burn victims with [[Marla Hengist|Marla]]). Hildegarde showed her the **silk handkerchief initialed "C.A."** and her look turned *from distrusting to more worried and interested*. In the linen closet she asked: *"You come from Asper, right? What's going on?"* and, hearing the plan, said *"If Asper is okay with your plan, then so am I"*; *she was ready to go at any time*. She took a **spare knife** from Hildegarde (*"in case we get separated"*), slid down the laundry drain first, and met the rest of the party and [[Ewalt Wrangel]] in the sewer: *"I guess you're the rest of the organization."* She moved up the tunnel with the party toward the catacombs, was nearly handed over to the **Steel Fist** thugs in a joke from [[Ganav]], and stayed behind the party during the ambush.

**Before [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]:** named for the first time in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]; the party agreed to help extract her.

## Known Information
- **Hunted by both gangs at once** — the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] and the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] — over **the stolen lottery purse**
- **Neither gang will violate the temple's sanctuary**, which is too important to their own people as a source of healing and charity — but **neither will let her walk free**, and **every exit is watched**
- [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]] are **themselves watched** whenever they move through the city, because the gangs suspect their connection to her
- The party's working plan: **smuggle her out with a protected merchant convoy on the route previously used to move people to Averland**
- **New plan** *(Session 39)*: out of the temple through its **laundry drain** (a 20-inch ceramic pipe to the sewers, grate already marked and loosened by [[Ganav]]), through the sewers to a **hidden street door**, then **away by boat** from the [[Wolfenburg Docks|docks]]. The [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] have agreed to stay clear of the sewer junction for **a week**

- [[Lucas Pollack]]'s reaction: *"The ice queen has a beating heart after all."*
- The party agreed to help, settling on the **Averland merchant-convoy route** as the extraction plan

## [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]
- **Out of the temple.** She left the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] by the **laundry drain**, on a knotted rope of bedsheets, and is now **with the party in the sewers**
- **Fears the Steel Fists are tracking her** and refused to meet the party at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]; three Steel Fist thugs found the party in the sewers and demanded *"the girl"*
- **Carries a knife** from [[Hildegarde]]; unarmed otherwise
- **Next:** the catacombs, then a funeral procession through the North Gate where [[Christina Asper]] waits

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- **Pressed to turn witness** in the catacombs. *"Don't blame me for their acts. I abandoned them some time ago… sometimes it's hard to extract yourself from such a gang."* She joined the Church of Shallya **over a year ago** but was **forced back** to work with the Lurkers on occasion
- **Admitted her part in the robbery.** She **helped plan the theft of the Steel Fists' lottery money**, because she knew the layout of their holdings from past work — the **repayment of a long-standing debt**, and *"the last I was involved with them"*
- **On Gerd (to [[Hildegarde]], in private):** it was **[[Nina Trielffen|Nina]]** who brought [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] to the temple; Hilde knew him from before and told no one. His household was burned and killed, another body put in his place; she called it *"a little bit crazy"*. **Not the Lurkers' style** — they are thieves, *"not into grand conspiracies"* — and she knew of no other group
- **Taught Hildegarde a sequence of hand-signs** for Nina: *Hilde is safe, these people are trustworthy*. Nina is a **follower of Ranald**
- **Rode out of the city in a coffin** — reluctantly, after checking there was no latch. She **stirred when the hearse stopped** for the man holding [[Marla Hengist|Marla]], and Berin talked her into staying: Asper's plan
- Now with [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Christina Asper|Asper]], heading for a **northern burial site**
