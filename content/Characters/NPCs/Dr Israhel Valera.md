# Dr Israhel Valera

## Description
Elderly lead doctor overseeing the Shallyan Orphanage in Wolfenburg. Met in person at the Physicians' Guild while the party sought treatment.

## Role / Occupation
Runs the orphanage's medical care, assisted by Dr. Imhot and Dr. Buchner. Treats poor patients — including the eastern-gate disease cases — who can't afford the Physicians' Guild.

## Relationships
- Assisted by [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]] and Dr. Buchner

## History with the Party
Met in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]] at the Physicians' Guild while the party had Hildegarde healed and Ganav's embedded arrowhead surgically removed. Asked about Professor Udo Stradovski and Jaie Schraeder — claimed to know neither.

## Known Information
- Oversees the Shallyan Orphanage, which is reportedly in difficult financial straits
- Treats the poor patients with the mysterious eastern-gate illnesses
- Says he doesn't know Stradovski or recognize Schraeder's name — unconfirmed whether this is true
