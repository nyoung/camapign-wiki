# Klaus von Kolditz

## Description
A name on two lists. Identified by the [[Ferlangen]] brothel worker as one of several **corrupt merchants involved in human trafficking**, then independently confirmed by **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** as an **antiques dealer with rumoured ties to human trafficking**. Not yet met in person.

## Role / Occupation
Antiques dealer based in [[Ferlangen]]. The antiques trade in [[Ferlangen]] is reputable on its surface; Von Kolditz's reputation among those who know better is rather different.

## Relationships
- **High-class brothel worker** ([[Ferlangen]]) — named him to [[Hildegarde]] as a trafficker
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** — confirmed his ties to trafficking when the party mentioned the name
- **[[Anton Kristov]]** — a fellow named-name on the trafficking thread

## History with the Party
**[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Almost certainly on the unwritten list of merchants the brothel worker passed to [[Hildegarde]] — though not specifically called out at the table.

**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] mentioned the name to [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]; the gnome's expression sharpened. Identified as an **antiques dealer with rumoured trafficking ties**.
