# Klaus von Kolditz

## Description
A name on multiple lists. Identified by the [[Ferlangen]] brothel worker as one of several **corrupt merchants involved in human trafficking**, independently confirmed by **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** as an antiques dealer with rumoured trafficking ties, and later named by Gromril (as "Augustus van Kolditz") as his prime suspect for funding the road attacks. Confirmed by GM ruling to be a single person — not a split identity — after the "Augustus" and "Klaus" leads coincided too neatly to be separate people.

## Role / Occupation
Antiques dealer based in [[Ferlangen]]. The trade is reputable on its surface; his reputation among those who know better is rather different. Also holds a debt relationship with [[Gromril van Aelst]]: owed him **over 500 crowns**, repaid only after being coerced.

## Relationships
- **High-class brothel worker** ([[Ferlangen]]) — named him to [[Hildegarde]] as a trafficker
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** — confirmed his trafficking ties when the party mentioned the name; separately, his debtor (500+ crowns, coerced repayment) and prime suspect for directing the road attacks
- **[[Anton Kristov]]** — a fellow named name on the trafficking thread
- **Hofbauer** — a rival [[Ferlangen]] merchant [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] proposed approaching for help against him

## History with the Party
**[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Almost certainly on the unwritten list of merchants the brothel worker passed to [[Hildegarde]] — though not specifically called out at the table.

**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] mentioned the name to [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]; the gnome's expression sharpened. Identified as an antiques dealer with rumoured trafficking ties.

**[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country|Session 26]]:** Named by Gromril (as "Augustus van Kolditz") at the [[Wayside Shrine]] camp as his best guess for who is behind the ambushes, after [[Astrid Hofhaimer]] demanded the promissory notes at parlay — reasoning that a merchant freshly dunned for a large debt, eager to see the evidence destroyed, has clear motive.

**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Independently corroborated — a bribed clerk at the [[Merchant's Office (Riverfront)]] confirmed Von Kolditz has been leaking caravan movements to the [[Howling Ones]], keeping his own shipments safe while rivals' cargo is plundered.

## Known Information
- Antiques dealer in [[Ferlangen]]
- Suspected of involvement in **human trafficking**
- Named by multiple independent sources: the brothel worker, [[Gromril van Aelst]] (twice, under both names), and a bribed merchant clerk in Wolfenburg
- His given name **begins with K** — directly relevant to the Stradovski-conspiracy "K" letter from earlier sessions
- Owed [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] 500+ crowns; coerced into repaying
- Leaking caravan movements to the [[Howling Ones]] so his own shipments arrive untouched while rivals are targeted ([[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]])
- Not yet met by the party in person

## Open Questions
