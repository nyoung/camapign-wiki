# Karel Vantz

## Description
An older man with a salt-and-pepper beard, in the black cloak of the Lurkers. Fast and strong with a sword; the players called him "a monster" in the fight.

## Role / Occupation
A senior [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurker]] who seized [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] and led the cell at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]. Spoken as "Carl" or "Carol" at the table; the GM confirmed **Karel Vantz**.

## Personality
Decisive and self-preserving: when his companions fell he spent Advantage and sprinted off, abandoning them. Cold under pressure and quick to use a child as leverage (*"Keep it up if you want this girl to die"*). Not a talker.

## Relationships
- Leads the cell of [[Petra Voss]] and [[Anselm Rathke]]
- Part of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]; not [[Anselm Rathsky]] (the gang's leader)
- By [[Petra Voss|Petra's]] account, [[Gerd Breytenbach]] passed "Karel" tips about the [[Physicians' Guild (Wolfenburg)|Physicians Guild]]
- Held [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] hostage; wants [[Hilde Muntz]] back

## History with the Party
**[[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]:** the older man in the road who held [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] at dagger-point and backed away down a side passage after Berin's denunciation.

**[[Session 42 - I Almost Killed You All|Session 42]]:** Led Marla, [[Petra Voss|Petra]] and [[Anselm Rathke|Anselm]] to the Temple of the All Gods "to see what Beham left for us." Spotted [[Ganav]] hiding there, charged [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and carved him up (left leg, about 12 damage in one round), then **fled**, spending Advantage to sprint away. Caught by Ganav half a block on, he rolled an 11-critical on Ganav's already broken arm and walked off after sheathing his sword.

## Known Information
- Carries a **sword** and fights like a veteran; the GM's autopilot ran him as a high-confidence melee type
- At large; he knows the party's faces and the Temple of the All Gods
- The Verena board posts the Lurkers at 2 shillings alive for questioning
- **Not in the module.** A campaign-original name, confirmed by the GM
