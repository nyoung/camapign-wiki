# Augustus van Kolditz

## Description
A powerful **antique dealer** in [[Ferlangen]], named by [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] — not yet met by the party in person. Gromril holds one of his debts: **over five hundred crowns** that Augustus had owed and been **coerced into repaying** when the gnome came collecting.

## Role / Occupation
Antique dealer of [[Ferlangen]]. [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s **prime suspect** for the man funding the [[Howling Ones]] attacks on the caravan — reasoning that a merchant freshly dunned for a large debt, and eager to see the evidence of it destroyed, has clear motive to want [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s **promissory notes** seized.

## Relationships
- **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]** — owed him 500+ crowns; was coerced into paying; Gromril names him as the likely paymaster behind the road attacks
- **[[Howling Ones]]** — *suspected* (by Gromril) of funding them; unconfirmed
- **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]** — shares the **Kolditz** family name (also a Ferlangen merchant / antiques dealer); any relationship is unestablished
- **Hofbauer** — a rival [[Ferlangen]] merchant [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] proposed approaching for help against Kolditz

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]** — named by [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] at the [[Wayside Shrine]] camp as his best guess for who is behind the ambushes, after [[Astrid Hofhaimer]] demanded the promissory notes at parlay. Gromril produced an actual banknote to show what the cult was after.

## Known Information
- Antique dealer in [[Ferlangen]]
- Owed [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] 500+ crowns; coerced into repaying
- Gromril's suspected paymaster behind the [[Howling Ones]] attacks — **a theory, not confirmed**
- Shares a surname with the trafficker [[Klaus von Kolditz]]

## Open Questions
- Gromril's accusation is **his own theory** — the party have no proof yet that Augustus van Kolditz is behind the attacks.
- The **Kolditz / von Kolditz** name overlap with the trafficker [[Klaus von Kolditz]] is unexplained: family, coincidence, or something more.
