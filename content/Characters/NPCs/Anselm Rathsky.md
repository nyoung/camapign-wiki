# Anselm Rathsky

## Description
Holds court from the absolute darkest corner of [[The Pig and Tack]], where only the glowing cherry of his cigar betrays that anyone is sitting there at all. Steps out of the dark on his own terms.

## Role / Occupation
**Leader of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** — not merely a frontman, as the party had been told. Reachable through the dwarf bartender at [[The Pig and Tack]].

## Personality
Watchful and unhurried — let the party work the room before revealing himself. Susceptible to genuine charm: [[Hildegarde]] drew him into what felt like candour, in which he laid out his organisation's position, including its weakness. Deals plainly and asks for something concrete in return.

**But he shades the truth.** His account of the gang war casts the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fists]] as thieves who robbed the Prince, making his own gang's raid a reclamation rather than a robbery — a framing [[Captain Crais]] flatly does not support. He also said nothing at all about the Lurkers' tattoo link to the Shallyan splinter sect while volunteering everything else. What reads as candour is a practised pitch.

## Relationships
- **Leads** the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]], including the three members who chased [[Deke Kudgel Brand|Deke "Kudgel" Brand]] in [[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]
- At war with the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]
- Shares the room at [[The Pig and Tack]] with the gambler [[Fiedler]]
- **Possible link to [[Gerd Breytenbach]]**, who told his mother he had been "helping out" the Lurkers

## History with the Party
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Named by one of three cloaked men Boris and Berin talked down during the alleyway chase, as their group's frontman — findable at [[The Pig and Tack]]. Not yet visited.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** **Met.** After [[Hildegarde]] ran a card game and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] accidentally bought the room's goodwill with a round of drinks, Rathsky emerged from the darkest corner and introduced himself as the Lurkers' leader. Charmed into apparent candour, he gave his account of the gang war: the Lurkers had **stolen the Prince's lottery takings from the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fists]], who he said had already stolen them** — the heist of the heist. *([[Captain Crais]] described it differently the same day; see Personality.)* The Steel Fists are hunting every Lurker they can find and his organisation is bleeding. He offered a deal — **stop the Steel Fists' pursuit and he can get someone out of the city** — left a standing invitation to make contact through the bartender, and wished the party luck in a way that comforted nobody.

## Known Information
- **Leader** of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]
- His gang stole the lottery takings **from the Steel Fists** — who, *he says*, had stolen them from the Prince first. **[[Captain Crais]] tells it differently**: the Steel Fists ran the lottery themselves and the Prince cracked down on it. Rathsky's framing turns his own gang's robbery into a reclamation
- The Steel Fists are hunting his people relentlessly; the Lurkers are losing
- Can arrange passage out of [[Wolfenburg]] for someone, if the pursuit is broken
- Contactable via the dwarf bartender at [[The Pig and Tack]]

## Open Questions
- **Does he know where [[Gerd Breytenbach]] is?** Gerd told his mother he was helping the Lurkers — the party spoke with Rathsky at length without either side raising the name
- What is the Lurkers' actual connection to the Shallyan splinter sect they share tattoos with? He volunteered nothing
- Is the offer of passage out of the city genuine, or leverage?
- **How much else was spin?** The party has one demonstrable discrepancy between his account and [[Captain Crais]]'s. If they catch it, everything else he said is worth re-testing — including that he's the leader, and that he can get anyone out of the city at all
