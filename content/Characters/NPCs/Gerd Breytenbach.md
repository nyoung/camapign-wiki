# Gerd Breytenbach

## Description
Jaie Schraeder's butler. Tall, healthy, close to six feet, with good teeth. A quiet, private man of voracious reading habits — worked through heroic tales, philosophy, medicine, and metallurgy at a local bookstore, exchanging books twice a week.

## Role / Occupation
Formerly presumed dead in the fire that gutted Schraeder's townhouse — now almost certainly alive. Has one known close friend: the manservant of merchant [[Anselm Geissmar]].

## Relationships
- Servant of [[Jaie Schraeder]]
- The body found alongside Louisa May's in the townhouse fire is **not** his
- Close friend: the manservant of [[Anselm Geissmar]]

## History with the Party
Named by clerk Danel Koberger during the party's investigation of Jaie Schraeder's death in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]. His body, along with Louisa May's, was too badly burned for formal identification by the City Watch.

**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]:** The Garden of Mórr body attributed to him turned out to be an older, malnourished man missing teeth, with wounds that didn't match the clean professional kills on Louisa May and Schraeder — likely a planted assassin killed in self-defense. [[Danel Koberger]] confirmed the real Breytenbach was tall, healthy, and well-toothed. A local bookseller confirmed he stopped exchanging books two weeks prior — exactly matching the fire's timing. The party's best lead on him now runs through [[Anselm Geissmar]]'s manservant, his one known close friend.

**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Muddying the trail — a servant at [[Fazzini House]] ("Anselm," possibly Geissmar's manservant himself) told the party Breytenbach had *also* died under suspicious circumstances at the Schraeder household, directly contradicting the party's own Session 33 conclusion. Still pursuing the lead, the party found and gently informed his mother, [[Madame Reitenbach]], of his supposed death; she was devastated but welcomed them in.

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** The morgue body was **confirmed** not to match his description, and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] reported to [[Captain Crais]] that it was likely a deliberate plant — its wounds suggesting a scuffle rather than the precise professional killing that took the other victims. His mother then supplied the single most important detail yet: on his **last visit, roughly a week before he vanished, Gerd mentioned "helping out" a questionable group he called "the Lurkers"** — the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] — and it had visibly been weighing on him. She also revealed he had been close to **[[Louisa May]]**, the maid who died in the fire, and that his father, a dock worker, had taught him to read before dying in the **Red Pox plague of '02**.

## Known Information
- The Garden of Mórr body is **not** him — it's an older, malnourished man missing teeth, with wounds inconsistent with the professional kills on Louisa May and Schraeder; likely a deliberate plant
- **He was involved with the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** — by his own account to his mother, about a week before he disappeared, and it was troubling him
- Was **close to [[Louisa May]]**, the maid killed in the townhouse fire
- Stopped exchanging books at his regular bookstore roughly two weeks before Session 33, in step with the townhouse fire; used the booksellers near the **Temple District**
- One close friend: the manservant of merchant [[Anselm Geissmar]], who lives around the corner from Schraeder's former townhouse
- His father was a dock worker who died in the Red Pox plague of '02
- A Fazzini House servant claims he's dead too — unconfirmed, and contradicted by the party's own evidence

## Open Questions
- If alive, where is he, and why does the household appear to have staged his death?
- Is he complicit in the murders, a witness in hiding, or a target himself?
- Why did Fazzini House's servant claim he's dead? Lie, error, or has something changed?
- **What was he doing for the Lurkers — and does [[Anselm Rathsky]] know where he is?** The party spoke with Rathsky at length in Session 35 without either side raising Gerd's name; the connection only surfaced afterwards
- Did his closeness to [[Louisa May]] make him a target, a witness, or a conspirator?
