# Hawkeye

## Description
A man whose age is difficult to place — weathered in the way that outdoor men get, neither young nor visibly old. He carries himself with the economy of movement that comes from years of staying still and invisible in forest cover. He has a hawk-and-eye patch somewhere on his person (shoulder area). He gave no name other than "Hawkeye" — chosen, he explained, because it commits him to nothing and reveals nothing; he has no friends, only people who know him.

## Role / Occupation
Formerly a member of the [[Howling Ones]] bandit cult, fighting on this stretch of road. Captured alive after the ambush on 8 Jahrdrung 2512 by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]. Currently travelling as **ward of Berin's honour** — his fate to be decided by proper courts in [[Wolfenburg]].

Demonstrated significant and apparently genuine field surgery skill — he directed the emergency procedure that saved [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s life when an arrow had driven through the agitator's mouth and jaw.

## Personality
Sardonic and precise with words. Refuses to incriminate himself further than necessary. Has a sharp tongue that cost him goodwill with [[Hildegarde]] during the surgery. Shows a surprising capacity for candour in private moments — he admitted to [[Hildegarde]] that stepping out of the forest to help a dying stranger rather than fleeing was the most selfless act of his life, and perhaps the first real choice he had ever made for himself.

He explicitly denies that he was the one who shot [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]].

## Relationships
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** — accepted oath of parole to him; Berin vouched for his skill and life; travels as his ward; Hawkeye noticed Berin's amulet (see below)
- **[[Hildegarde]]** — deeply distrustful of him; keeps her mace hand ready; suspects him of shooting Pilpoul; had an unexpectedly honest conversation with him during the night vigil
- **[[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]** — the man Hawkeye saved; the surgery was genuinely expert
- **[[Howling Ones]]** — former colleagues; may try to kill him to keep him quiet

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 24 - Hawkeye]]** — captured unconscious at the ambush site by [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]; gave his oath of parole; directed the field surgery that saved [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s life; named himself "Hawkeye" at crossbow-point; disputed shooting Pilpoul; scouted around him by Berin who found near-invisible camouflage and a wolf symbol on a dead bandit; noticed [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s bird-of-prey amulet during the night vigil and was visibly unsettled; present when second ambush began at the [[Mountain Pass Choke Point]]

## Known Information
- Calls himself Hawkeye — refuses to give a real name
- Has significant field surgery knowledge; familiar with treating arrow wounds
- Knows the Ferlangen–Wolfenburg road and its bandit operations well
- His former companions may try to kill him to keep him quiet
- Bears a hawk-and-eye device on his shoulder
- Denies shooting Pilpoul; the party has not resolved whether to believe this

## History with the Party (continued)
- **[[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf]]** — during the second ambush he tried to take a shot at his former [[Howling Ones]] associates from cover, but the distance and their rocky shelter made it near-impossible; an arrow from the ridge then **struck his shoulder and cracked his scapula**, stunning him and leaving him unable to draw. The arrow lodged too deep for field removal — it must wait for a surgeon in [[Wolfenburg]]. When the newly captured outlaw was brought down, he **spat at Hawkeye and branded him a traitor**, making plain how the Howling Ones regard those who break ranks. In this session's notes he is referred to as **"Ganav"** as often as "Hawkeye."
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]** — referred to throughout as **Ganav**. He raged about his broken clavicle, lamenting that he should have stayed with the bandits. Standing the **third watch**, he **recognised the cult's harassment as a deliberate exhaustion tactic** and countered it not by raising an alarm but by **stoking the fire to keep the sleeping party warm**. At the parlay he vouched that the [[Howling Ones]] would honour its sanctity. When the party resolved to bait a counter-ambush, he accepted a **crossbow pistol** from [[Hildegarde]] despite his sling, so he could fight.
