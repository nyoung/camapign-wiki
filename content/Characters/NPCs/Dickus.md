# Dickus

## Description
A [[Howling Ones]] bandit — distinguished among the cult's road crew by the fact that he can actually read. Approached the prisoner exchange point with practiced efficiency, removing [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s decoy satchel without bothering to untie the prisoner and rifling through its contents with quick, professional suspicion.

## Role / Occupation
Rank-and-file Howling Ones bandit, evidently the one trusted with document verification.

## Personality
Methodical and unhurried under pressure — he identified the fake papers and signalled his comrades without panic or display. Not easily rattled by bluffs.

## Relationships
- **[[Howling Ones]]** — member; took a field-command role at the prisoner exchange

## History with the Party
**[[Session 27 - Fight!]]** — Emerged from the tree line at the parlay point to inspect the prisoner and the satchel. Identified [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s decoy documents as worthless papers within moments; immediately alerted his comrades. [[Ganav]]'s shouted bluff — that Dickus had been planning to steal the notes for himself — failed to rattle him. After the exchange collapsed into fighting, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] shot him in the arm for a significant hit. His status at the end of the session is unknown — he may have fled, or may still be in the fight.

## Known Information
- Literate — able to identify forged documents on sight
- Shot in the arm by [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] during the ambush
