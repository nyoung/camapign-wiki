# Ansgar Knochenbrecher

## Description
A tall, heavily built man in his forties with the marks of a former pit boxer turned hired muscle: a flattened, crooked nose broken many times, old scars cutting through both eyebrows, a permanent split-line scar through the lower lip, and a left eye that droops slightly at the outer corner. Dark hair cropped to close stubble. Moves and stands like a man permanently on duty — arms often crossed, weight settled, immovable.

## Role / Occupation
[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s bodyguard and traveling companion since her flight from [[Beeckerhoven]]. Silent, professional muscle. For most of the pursuit he was only ever *observed* at a distance — that changed at the [[Wolfenburg Docks]] in [[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]], where the party engaged him hand-to-hand for the first time.

## Personality
Stoic and watchful. Everything observed of him so far is functional: driving, guarding doors, negotiating passage. No dialogue with the party on record.

## Relationships
- [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] — employer/protectee; has accompanied her the entire way from Beeckerhoven to Wolfenburg

## History with the Party

The party has tracked this "broad-shouldered companion" alongside Elfrieda for most of her flight north, without knowing his name until it surfaced independently of session play:

- **Session 12:** A black coach passed through the Hunter's Lodge the night before the party arrived, driven by a broad-shouldered man accompanying a raven-haired woman (Elfrieda) — continuing north despite road warnings.
- **Session 16:** Two women and a "brutish man" passed through Schoten (Ostland) three nights before — likely this group.
- **Session 18:** Confirmed at the [[Hemp Merchant]] by [[Jan Smuts]] — Elfrieda with "one large, burly male companion."
- **Session 32:** [[Reiner Kolber]]'s street-orphan network tracked Elfrieda and her "broad-shouldered/burly bodyguard" to [[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House]] in Wolfenburg. A barge captain separately reported the bodyguard tried (and failed) to book passage to Altdorf for two, paying four times the going rate.

**Session 36 (first direct engagement):** At the [[Wolfenburg Docks]], with [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] making for her boat, the party's ambush broke into an open brawl. [[Hildegarde]] was caught in a **crushing headlock by a massive brute she could barely budge** — logged in the session export under the name **"Bone Breaker,"** which renders his surname *Knochenbrecher* directly, alongside a separate "Ansgar" entry. She freed herself only by hurling her steel-capped hat into another thug's face to make an opening and then wrenching loose through sheer determination.

His performance matches his history exactly: a former pit boxer's grappling strength, used to hold rather than to kill. He did not stop Elfrieda's escape being disrupted, but he did neutralise the party's most mobile member at the critical moment.
