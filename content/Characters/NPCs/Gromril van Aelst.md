# Gromril van Aelst

## Description
A small figure in colourful clothing and a **pointy red hat**, instantly identifiable from a distance even in a crowded [[Ferlangen]] street. A gnome merchant of evident self-possession — happy to let his bodyguard cause the violence, then close the deal himself. Carries his apparent purse where a cutpurse can find it; carries his **real value in a hidden case tucked beneath his shirt**.

## Role / Occupation
Travelling merchant. Came to [[Ferlangen]] specifically to **collect on promissory notes** owed to him by local merchants — debts that, evidently, those same merchants would rather have stayed on paper. Currently in want of an **escort to [[Wolfenburg]]**.

## Personality
Calm under threat; quick to read a room. Patted his hidden case with quiet satisfaction in front of the party — a man who plans for the cutpurse, the brawl, *and* the next deal in the same breath. Offered a fair price (10 GC) for an escort job and shook on it within the minute.

## Relationships
- **[[Mangold]]** — dwarf slayer; bodyguard. Will chase a thief on instinct, even at the cost of leaving his charge undefended
- **[[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]]** — halfling driver; staffs his coach
- **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]** — knows by reputation as an **antiques dealer with rumoured ties to human trafficking**
- **[[Augustus van Kolditz]]** — antique dealer who **owed Gromril 500+ crowns** (coerced into paying); Gromril's **named suspect** for the man behind the road attacks
- **[[Anton Kristov]]** — knows as a **small-time import-exporter** running goods (and people) on the [[Ferlangen]] → [[Wolfenburg]] → [[Kislev]] route
- **Local [[Ferlangen]] merchants** — owe him money; have hired thugs to take it back
- **The party** — saved him from an alley ambush; hired to escort his coach to [[Wolfenburg]] for **10 GC**

## History with the Party
**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** First seen on the opposite side of the street from [[Sam Bugman's Brewery]] in [[Ferlangen]], walking alongside the unmistakable silhouette of the dwarf slayer [[Mangold]]. A thief cut his purse and ran; [[Mangold]] gave chase without a word, leaving Gromril to be herded by waiting thugs into a dead-end alley. The party intervened — [[Hildegarde]] called for calm, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] drew his sling and dagger, [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] came in with the flat of his blade, and a singing [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] left one thug catatonic with confusion — and beat the ambushers down. When [[Mangold]] returned with the recovered purse, Gromril introduced himself, named the merchants behind the attack, and confirmed two names from [[Hildegarde]]'s brothel intelligence: **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]** and **[[Anton Kristov]]**. He then hired the party to escort his coach to [[Wolfenburg]] for **10 GC**, with rendezvous at dawn outside the south gate.

## Session 25 — Second Ambush Survived
- His escort caravan **survived the second ambush** at the [[Mountain Pass Choke Point]], though battered and slowed; the monstrous wolf was driven off and a further outlaw captured. The captured outlaw's warning of **more ambushes ahead** keeps Gromril's promissory-note cargo (and his safe arrival in [[Wolfenburg]]) in jeopardy.

## Session 26 — The Notes Named
- After [[Astrid Hofhaimer]] demanded his **promissory notes** at parlay, Gromril explained what they were: **debts collected from [[Ferlangen]] merchants**, owed onward to [[Wolfenburg]] moneylenders
- Named his prime suspect for the paymaster: **[[Augustus van Kolditz]]**, an antique dealer who had owed him 500+ crowns and been coerced into repaying
- **Produced an actual banknote** — a printed document with a stamped family crest and hand-written amounts, near-impossible to forge convincingly
- Reluctant to engage in trickery, but his information seeded [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s decoy-and-ambush plan
