# Gammer Isolde Ulricht

## Description
A warm and thoughtful woman, finally met in person in [[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]] after being named as a contact all the way back in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]. Received the party in a private back room of the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] after reading their letter of introduction from the Shallyan priestess in [[Ferlangen]].

## Role / Occupation
Senior clergy — high priestess of the [[Cult of Shallya]] at the Wolfenburg temple. The Ferlangen priestess named her as the party's **best contact** for further information about the rare and exotic disease outbreaks reported in [[Wolfenburg]].

## Relationships
- **Cult of Shallya** — Wolfenburg temple
- **Ferlangen Shallyan priestess** — colleague who passed her name to the party
- **Dr. von Bora** — considers him "one of the good ones"; wrote the party's letter of introduction to him

## Known Information
- High priestess of Shallya in [[Wolfenburg]]
- Named contact for the party regarding the **simultaneous outbreaks of rare and exotic diseases** in Wolfenburg (rumours reached Ferlangen ~1 week before Session 20)

## Session 29 — The Meeting
- Received the party's full account of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s flight, experiments, and possible ties to illness and decay
- Shared three troubling leads: men have been collecting unwanted children under seven from local farms on behalf of the orphanage, and the children **simply vanish** — the law is powerless since children that young are not legally recognized as persons; Physicians Guild secretary **[[Jaie Schraeder]]** was recently murdered and his home burned; rare wasting-disease victims are arriving almost exclusively through the **eastern gate**
- Wrote a letter of introduction to **Dr. von Bora** and provided the party with protective talismans
- Named three physicians friendly to the temple who work at the orphanage: Israhel Valera, Hartmut Imhot, Mihai Buchner

## Appearances

- [[Session 29 - Wolfenburg]] *(met in person)*

## Notes
- Distinct from **[[High Priestess Isolde Begegnen]]** (the matron of the [[Temple of Shallya]] in [[Middenheim]] from [[Session 02 - The Trial of Klaus Grebel|Session 02]]); shared first name only.
- *Naming note (resolved):* GM Assistant's session exports had rendered her name inconsistently as "Mother Isoldi" ([[Session 29 - Wolfenburg|Session 29]]) and "Masilda Gamar" ([[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]). Cross-checked against the source module (*A Private War*), her canonical name is **Isolde** — confirmed via Find & Replace corrections applied directly in GM Assistant, and standardized here to the established campaign name **Gammer Isolde Ulricht** per [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]'s original naming.
