# Azmus Beham

## Description
Proprietor of the Temple of the All Gods. Broad smile, a slightly threadbare red velvet robe, and the relentless energy of a man who has never met a stranger.

## Role / Occupation
Runs the Temple of the All Gods, selling pre-paid burial plots with cheerful, relentless salesmanship — group rates, installment plans, the works. Claims a longtime acquaintance with Dr. von Bora.

## Personality
Jovial and sales-driven right up until the subject of missing bodies came up — then his composure broke entirely. Boris read it as genuine fear, not embarrassment. More interested in closing a sale than in theology; unbothered by the oddity of a heretical Shallya shrine in his own temple.

## Relationships
- Claims acquaintance with **Dr. von Bora**
- Sold a burial plot painting's commission to an unnamed "local artist" — details vague or evasive

## History with the Party
**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist]]:** Met the party at the Temple of the All Gods after they approached under cover of scholarly curiosity via Dr. von Bora. Sold Berin on conversation about the temple and its nature-spirit shrine while the rest examined the strange Shallya painting. Led the party into the catacombs below, showing off the ancient tunnels — until Hildegarde asked about the missing-bodies rumour, at which point he abruptly ended the visit, hustled the party out, and locked the gate behind them, disappearing around a corner.

## Known Information
- Claims the Shallya painting was donated by a local artist he "can't quite recall," commissioned by a group who see Shallya "in a different light"
- Has a large iron key to the catacombs below the temple
- Reacted with visible fear, not mere embarrassment, when asked about bodies missing from the temple

## Open Questions
- What does he actually know about the missing bodies and the river tunnel beneath the catacombs?
- Who is the "local artist" who painted the heretical Shallya shrine, and who commissioned it?
- Is his fear self-preservation, or is he complicit?
