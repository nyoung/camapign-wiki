# Azmus Beham

## Description
Proprietor of the Temple of the All Gods. Broad smile, a slightly threadbare red velvet robe, and the relentless energy of a man who has never met a stranger.

## Role / Occupation
Runs the Temple of the All Gods, selling pre-paid burial plots with cheerful, relentless salesmanship — group rates, installment plans, the works. Claims a longtime acquaintance with Dr. von Bora.

## Personality
Jovial and sales-driven right up until the subject of missing bodies came up — then his composure broke entirely. Boris read it as genuine fear, not embarrassment. More interested in closing a sale than in theology; unbothered by the oddity of a heretical Shallya shrine in his own temple.

## Relationships
- Claims acquaintance with **Dr. von Bora**
- Sold a burial plot painting's commission to an unnamed "local artist" — details vague or evasive

## History with the Party
**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist]]:** Met the party at the Temple of the All Gods after they approached under cover of scholarly curiosity via Dr. von Bora. Sold Berin on conversation about the temple and its nature-spirit shrine while the rest examined the strange Shallya painting. Led the party into the catacombs below, showing off the ancient tunnels — until Hildegarde asked about the missing-bodies rumour, at which point he abruptly ended the visit, hustled the party out, and locked the gate behind them, disappearing around a corner.

**[[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]:** Arrived to close the temple for the night, declaring it closing time — and went visibly pale at the sight of [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]]. **Hildegarde's Charm test to win him over failed**; Asper leaned on him, pointedly saying she would hate to see the party prevented from exploring the catacombs, and knocked past him on her way out for the [[Witch Hunters Guild]]. Left alone with the party, he reluctantly fished out his ring of keys, unlocked a padlock and lifted the iron bar on the catacomb gate, and let the five of them descend — then, once they were a short way in, **slammed the bar back, snapped the lock shut and hurried off across the temple floor**. Boris had half expected him to trail them and curse at the chalk marks.

## Known Information
- Claims the Shallya painting was donated by a local artist he "can't quite recall," commissioned by a group who see Shallya "in a different light"
- Has a large iron key to the catacombs below the temple
- Reacted with visible fear, not mere embarrassment, when asked about bodies missing from the temple
- **His ring of keys has no dwarven key on it** — only ordinary two- and three-notch iron keys *(Session 38)*
- **The party suspects him of writing the note signed "A.B."** found with two linen-wrapped corpses in the sewers — initials matching his own. A suspicion only *(Session 38)*
- **Locked the party into the catacombs** after letting them down, despite their promise to return within about an hour *(Session 38)*
- The cavern the bodies lead to is a **dead-end ghoul nest**; the bodies are being **fed to ghouls**, not stored *(Session 39)*
- The party has **not yet confronted him**. They are weighing taking the accusation to the **priests of Mórr** at the [[Garden of Mórr]], since he is **a powerful man** and their own standing with the law is shaky *(Session 39)*

## Open Questions
- **Is he "A.B."?** The note ("Lurkers … more bodies by Festag. — A.B.") sat under four old Ostland silver coins beside two well-kept corpses, dragged there by a barefoot adult — and he locked the party in right after
- Why did he **let them down at all**, and why did he **lock them in**?
- What does he actually know about the missing bodies and the river tunnel beneath the catacombs?
- Who is the "local artist" who painted the heretical Shallya shrine, and who commissioned it?
- Is his fear self-preservation, or is he complicit?

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- **The note is his — or signed as his.** At the gate between the catacombs and the temple, beside a burning candle, the party found a note signed **"A.B."**: *"You've made this town too warm for me. Keep the ring. I have no further use for it. Enjoy your victory while it lasts."* [[Ewalt Wrangel]]: **the same hand** as the note found with the corpses in the sewers ([[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]])
- **The ring he left** — a worn silver band with an amethyst — see [[Silver Amethyst Ring]]. The party's reading: it was **surrendered to a rival**, not left for them
- **The spirits were interred in his keeping.** Wrangel found that **all of the dozen spirits** he questioned were buried **within the last three years**, while Azmus ran the Temple of the All Gods; he suspects Azmus was the **instigator** of the body-moving, with **something deeper** behind the detaching
- **The temple was empty** and the front door **nailed shut** from outside; **the party reasons he has run**
- **Open:** is "A.B." the proprietor, or someone using the initials? Who was the note — and the ring — for?
