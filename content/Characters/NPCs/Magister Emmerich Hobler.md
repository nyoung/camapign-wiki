# Magister Emmerich Hobler

## Description
An ink-stained instructor in spectacles, academic gown and cap, who teaches at the [[Shallyan Orphanage]]. Heard as "Hodler" in the transcript; the vault uses the spelling from the GM's prep notes.

## Role / Occupation
Teaches the newer children letters, numbers, reading and arithmetic, and takes the most able as far as classical studies. He prepares them for apprenticeships.
