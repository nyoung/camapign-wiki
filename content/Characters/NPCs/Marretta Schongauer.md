# Marretta Schongauer

## Description
A pockmarked woman who keeps to a shadowed booth and smokes a long-stemmed pipe. Met at the [[Fist of Amber]] in [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]], where her subtle nod across the room — meant for someone else entirely — was misread by [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] as a personal invitation.

## Role / Occupation
Introduced herself to [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] as **headmistress of the local peddlars' guild**, coordinating goods for small settlements across the northern empire. The innkeeper at the [[Fist of Amber]] later confided to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] that she is in fact a **human trafficker**, smuggling desperate people from the north to cities further south for a fee.

## Personality
Cool, transactional, brisk. Pressed coins into Boris's hand and emphasised speed; offered no small talk and tolerated none. The kind of person who has had this conversation many times before.

## Relationships
- **[[Karl Helpmann]]** — recipient of her sealed letter, a warehouse operator in [[Ferlangen]]
- The two unnamed Ostlander travellers at the [[Fist of Amber]] — runaway serfs apparently in her trafficking pipeline
- **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]** — hired him to carry her sealed letter for **5 silver**
- **The innkeeper at the [[Fist of Amber]]** — knows her real trade and was willing to confide it to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]

## History with the Party
**[[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]:** Met at the [[Fist of Amber]]. Hired [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] to deliver a sealed letter to [[Karl Helpmann]] at a warehouse in [[Ferlangen]] for 5/-, with the seal and addressee marked clearly enough that the party concluded breaking it would be a mistake. Only [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] knows the innkeeper's confidence about her real trade — he kept it from the rest of the party.

**[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Letter delivered to [[Karl Helpmann]]. Her name **resurfaced in a far darker context** the same night: a high-class brothel worker in [[Ferlangen]], questioned by [[Hildegarde]] under condition of strict anonymity, named her in a list of **corrupt merchants involved in human trafficking** — men who had caused trouble for the working girls and their trade. The party (or at least [[Hildegarde]]) now hears Schongauer's name in the same breath as confirmed Ferlangen traffickers.

## Known Information
- Claims to head the local **Peddlars' Guild**
- Operates between the Ostland coast road and [[Ferlangen]]
- Has a sealed letter going to **[[Karl Helpmann]]**, a Ferlangen warehouse operator
- Per the [[Fist of Amber]] innkeeper (told to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] in confidence): **her real trade is human trafficking** — moving runaway serfs and other desperate people for a fee

## Session 22 — Intel at the Fist of Amber
On the evening of **6 Jahrdrung**, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] rode to meet her at the [[Fist of Amber]]. She expressed quiet satisfaction that the two Oeslander refugees had been safely delivered south. When the conversation turned to the Howling Ones, she glanced around the room before leaning in and revealing that the cult includes **werewolves who use transformation as a weapon**. She confirmed that **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]** is deeply involved in human trafficking — moving people in and out of [[Kislev]] — and has been systematically cutting off her trade contacts to strangle her business. She named **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** and **Fugger** as smaller rivals.

She then drafted and stamped a **letter of endorsement** for the party to use at [[Karl Helpmann's Peddler Store]]. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] paid for dinner for all three.

**Note:** Her trade (moving desperate people south for a fee) appears to be the same sympathetic serf-relocation pipeline as [[Karl Helpmann]]'s operation — quite different from [[Klaus von Kolditz]]'s trafficking network. The brothel worker who named her in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]] may have grouped all unconventional people-movers together.
