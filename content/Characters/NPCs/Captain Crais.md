# Captain Crais

## Description
Captain of the Wolfenburg City Watch, headquartered at the Courts in the central plaza — which doubles as the [[Wolfenburg Courthouse]]. **Remembers his friends and his enemies, and treats people accordingly.** Weary, blunt, and professional; he trades information rather than merely receiving it.

## Role / Occupation
Leads the Watch's investigation into [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s murder and holds the recovered evidence, including Classical-language documents naming [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]. He is also the party's **official point of contact under the consultancy status** they were granted after cashing in the [[The Howling Ones Bounty|Howling Ones bounty]].

## Personality
Professional and forthcoming when treated well, and genuinely dangerous to be on the wrong side of. He volunteered the corpses-in-the-river reports and a briefing on the city's two thief gangs unprompted — but he was also furious when the party overstepped, and blunt in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]] that whatever their warrants say, he cannot have bodies lining the streets of his city.

## Relationships
- Granted the party a gate-fee waiver and, separately, **consultancy status** for the Howling Ones bounty ([[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]])
- Receives investigation updates from [[Berin Garibaldi]] and the party
- Investigating [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s murder
- Tracks the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] and the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]] professionally
- [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]] carry enough weight with him to get the party released on their word

## History with the Party
**First encountered directly in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]]**, when Watch guards caught the party inside the sealed [[Schraeder's House]] crime scene and marched them to him. Furious that they had overstepped the gate-fee waiver he had issued, he relented after [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] reminded him of their Howling Ones service and offered to translate the Watch's Classical documents — a task the Watch could not do without relying on the despised witch hunters. He allowed them to examine the evidence box in exchange.

Since then the relationship has run **professional and reciprocal**, and survived the party being arrested in front of him.

## Known Information
- Both of [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s servants' bodies were **too badly burned for formal identification**
- Schraeder's own body was found **stabbed and robbed on the road toward Kislev**, staged to resemble a bandit attack
- Holds an **evidence box** of half-burned documents from Schraeder's townhouse, including Classical-language papers explicitly naming [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]
- **Embalmed and long-decayed corpses** are drifting downriver *from the direction of Wolfenburg* and collecting around the city's docks
- The [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]] **ran an illegal lottery** recently cracked down on by the Prince — **✅ confirmed correct** in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]
- The [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] specialise in thievery, lockpicking, and fencing stolen goods through untraceable channels
- Has **no knowledge of anyone named Astrid**, and no record of recent [[Howling Ones]] activity in the city
- A recent caravan led by [[Gromril van Aelst]] reported the roads entirely clear of Howling Ones

## Session 35 — The Heist of the Heist
- **Received the party's update** on the Schraeder investigation. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] reported that the body previously believed to be [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was **likely a plant** — its wounds suggesting a scuffle rather than the precise professional killing that took the other victims
- In exchange he shared that **embalmed bodies and long-decayed remains have been found floating downriver from the direction of Wolfenburg**, with corpses collecting around the city's docks, and gave the party a fuller picture of the two thief gangs

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **Presided over the party's hearing at the [[Wolfenburg Courthouse]]** after [[Hildegarde]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] were marched up from the docks over a dead stevedore
- **Weary and frustrated**, and blunt about it: whatever the party's warrants say, **he cannot have bodies lining the streets of his city**
- The surviving stevedore **dropped a freshly minted Nordland crown on his desk** as evidence of his payment. Crais pointed out that the **Imperial warrant covers [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]], not stolen coin** — [[Hildegarde]] argued the money and the fugitive were the same thread
- **Demanded corroborating names**; [[Hildegarde]] gave [[Hoffman Jannitzer]], [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]]. Asper and Pollack **arrived within half an hour**, spoke with him privately, and he **released the party** — with a firm warning not to take the law into their own hands again
- **✅ His account of the lottery was the correct one.** [[Lucas Pollack]] confirmed the same evening that the stolen money was the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]]'s illegal lottery take — **[[Anselm Rathsky]] had lied to the party**

## Notes
- **⚑ Naming (resolved 2026-09-05).** GM Assistant's transcription rendered this NPC three different ways across sessions: **"Crace"** (Session 31) and **"Kress"** (Sessions 35 and 37). The module names exactly one Watch captain — **Captain Crais** ⟨*A Private War*, p.51⟩ — and no second captain exists. The **"Kress"** spelling had been wrongly canonised as a separate campaign-original NPC with its own note; **the two have been merged into this file**, and all three sessions corrected at source in GM Assistant. All variants are the same man.
