# Danel Koberger

## Description
A clerk at the Wolfenburg Physicians' Guild who took over duties after Jaie Schraeder's death. Obsequious, an open book, eager not to give offense.

## Role / Occupation
Guild clerk. Felt passed over for promotion in favor of Schraeder, though he acknowledges Schraeder was reliable at the job.

## Relationships
- Colleague of the late [[Jaie Schraeder]]
- Reported to Professor Melancthon (former guildmaster, retired to Altdorf)

## History with the Party
Interviewed by the party in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]], after they had Hildegarde healed and Ganav's embedded arrowhead surgically removed at the Guild. Provided the full timeline of Schraeder's disappearance and death: the letter from Schraeder's supposedly dead father, the townhouse fire, and the discovery of Schraeder's body near the Forest Inn toward Kislev. Hildegarde read him as truthful and not withholding information. Directed the party to the Shallyan Orphanage for the eastern-gate disease pattern.

**[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]:** Re-interviewed about [[Gerd Breytenbach]]. Described him as a tall, healthy man with good teeth, close to six feet — flatly contradicting the malnourished, gap-toothed body held at the [[Garden of Mórr]], confirming the corpse is not actually Breytenbach.

## Known Information
- Schraeder received a letter the morning of the 31st of Nachexen claiming to be from his father — a man Koberger believed long dead and penniless
- Schraeder was appointed on Professor Melancthon's recommendation; Melancthon retired suddenly to Altdorf ~5 years ago
- Schraeder was usually reliable and never left the city — his disappearance was very uncharacteristic — and liked to brag about having come out of poverty
- Poor patients with the eastern illnesses are sent to the Shallyan Orphanage (overseen by [[Dr Israhel Valera]] and [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]]) rather than the Guild
- Schraeder's office and desk, searched by the party, held nothing tying him to Stradovski or Elfrieda Teuschel's network
- [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was tall, healthy, good-toothed, close to six feet — does not match the body held at the [[Garden of Mórr]]
