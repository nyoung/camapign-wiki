# Marla Hengist

## Description
A girl of perhaps **twelve or thirteen**, with **big brown eyes**, who shadows [[Hilde Muntz]] around the long-stay ward. Hildegarde could not stop smiling at her: *"she's way too cute."*

## Role / Occupation
An **orphan** the sisters of Shallya took in; she helps in the ward. Hilde: *"She's just an orphan who works here, taken on by the sisters. But we've grown very close."*

## Personality
Eager to help, attached to Hilde to the point of begging to leave with her. Stunned the first time Hilde rebuked her.

## Relationships
- **[[Hilde Muntz]]** — very close. *"You can't go… or if you do, I'm going with you."* Hilde pushed her back with a *"hush"* for the first time
- **[[Nina Trielffen]]** — works in the same temple
- **[[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]]** — she has seen him at night, wrapped in bandages, and thought he might be able to escape *"along with"* Hilde

## History with the Party
**[[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]:** Offered [[Hilde Muntz]] and [[Hildegarde]] the **linen closet** as a private place to talk (*"nobody goes there"*). Begged Hilde not to go. Then, with [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] busy asking [[Nina Trielffen|Nina]] about the laundry, **she told Hildegarde that the sisters are sheltering "Gerd" in the temple**, *"nobody's supposed to know"*: **brown hair, red tunic, wrapped in bandages**, seen when she was up in the night. Hildegarde posed as a **bounty hunter**, gave her a silver coin, and said she would come back.

## Known Information
- Believes **Gerd is "just another one who's trying to escape"**, not a bandit or burglar
- Cannot give a detailed description (she is a child)
- Has not been told that Hilde is leaving for good; she saw Hilde, Nina and the party go down to the basement

## Open Questions
- Will the party **go back for Gerd**?
- Will she tell anyone — the sisters, [[Nina Trielffen|Nina]], the watchers — what she has told Hildegarde?

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- **Taken hostage.** Two blocks from the Temple of the All Gods, an **older man in a black cloak with a salt-and-pepper beard** stood in the road holding her **by the back of her collar**, a **dagger loose in his hand** pointed at her side. *"Keep it up if you want this girl to die"*
- **She was not released.** Berin's denunciation failed; the man backed down a **side passage**, *"manhandling"* her, and [[Hildegarde]] set off after him. **Marla knows where [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] is**
- **Hilde heard it from the coffin** and was told by Berin to stay put
- *Inference, not stated:* a **black cloak** is the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]' mark. If so, she is **leverage for Hilde**

## [[Session 42 - I Almost Killed You All|Session 42]]
- **Recovered.** [[Hildegarde]] tracked her captor to a courtyard, then to the Temple of the All Gods, where Hilde knocked [[Anselm Rathke]] out and Marla ducked behind her. Marla later hid behind a statue and was kept out of the interrogation
- **Unhurt**, apart from bruising where her arm was gripped. The GM gave her no lines
- **Why she was taken:** per [[Petra Voss]], Marla wandered out looking for [[Hilde Muntz]], and [[Karel Vantz]] caught her first — *his* idea
- She went with the party to the [[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]]; the table intends to return her to the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]. She did **not** name [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] aloud

## [[Session 43 - A Mighty Gift|Session 43]]
- **Returned to the Temple of Shallya** with the party. She greeted Berin with a wave, took Hawkeye's sleeve to lead him in, and vouched for the party to Gammer: *"They saved me"* / *"They didn't kill anyone"*. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] praised her courage
- **Grounded** by [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht|Gammer]] until morning: quarters, no dinner or dessert
- **[[Hildegarde]] visited her** on the way out and gave her the **dagger** taken from the Lurkers, telling her that not following orders gets other people hurt (the example of Ganav) and to use it only as a last resort. The GM thought she would hide it somewhere secret
