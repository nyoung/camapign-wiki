# Lefty Longshanks

## Description
The halfling driver of **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**'s coach. Mentioned by name only — not yet met by the party in person.

## Role / Occupation
Coach driver in the employ of **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**.

## Relationships
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** — employer
- **[[Mangold]]** — fellow member of Gromril's road party

## History with the Party
**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** Named when [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] arranged to meet Gromril and his driver at the south gate the following morning to begin the [[Wolfenburg]] escort job.

## Known Information
- Halfling
- Drives [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s coach
- Will be at the south gate of [[Ferlangen]] at dawn on **7 Jahrdrung 2512**

## Session 22 — Met in Person; Coach Wreck
- Met in person at the south gate of [[Ferlangen]] on the morning of **7 Jahrdrung** when the caravan assembled
- Drove [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s large carriage south
- When the **Howling Ones ambush** severed the lead coach harness on 8 Jahrdrung, the coach careened into a stone wall and overturned — **Lefty rolled clear, shaken but unhurt**
- [[Mangold]] heaved the coach upright; Lefty recovered and continued driving

## Session 25 — Cooking for the Wounded
- At the [[Wayside Shrine]] night camp, he and [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] cooked [[Mathis Haack]]'s rabbits into a **restorative broth** for the wounded
