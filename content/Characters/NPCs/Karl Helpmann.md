# Karl Helpmann

## Description
A bespectacled man with a receding hairline and an irrepressible instinct to sell something to anyone in the room. Presides over **[[Helpmann's General Goods]]**, a cluttered little storefront watched over by a large orange cat asleep on a shelf. Inspects coins through a magnifying glass — and offers to sell the magnifying glass.

## Role / Occupation
General-goods peddlar in [[Ferlangen]]. The "warehouse operator" framing from [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]] now resolves to a small shopkeeper who also runs a **side trade in relocating serfs from the north to farms in [[Averland]]** — labour-supply work he characterises as a peddlar's smuggling, and which he openly distinguishes from "moving men of science and their equipment."

## Personality
Mercantile to the bone, cheerful and unhurried. Cannot resist trying to sell every item he handles — the letter opener used to slice the seal, the magnifying glass produced to inspect a coin. Talks freely about laborer opportunities heading toward Averland; quietly acknowledges that **guild rules make selling without a licence difficult** and so offers fractional prices for unlicensed goods.

## Relationships
- **[[Marretta Schongauer]]** — sender of the sealed letter Boris carried; her contact in [[Ferlangen]]
- **The orange shop-cat** — fixture of the shop
- **Party** — paid out roughly **a gold crown each** for sundries in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]; left a shilling retainer to send word of [[Elfrieda Teuschel|the dark-haired woman with the tall boxer]]
- Customers in [[Averland]] — the southern end of his serf-relocation pipeline

## History with the Party
**[[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]]:** Mentioned only — recipient of a sealed letter from [[Marretta Schongauer]] handed to [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] at the [[Fist of Amber]].

**[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Met in person at his shop. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] delivered the sealed letter, which Helpmann opened with a letter opener he immediately tried to sell them. The letter clearly created some modest work for him, and he mentioned **labourer opportunities heading toward [[Averland]]** while offering to buy odds and ends from the party at a fraction of value (guild-licence excuse). The party walked away with **roughly a gold crown each**; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] picked up a used set of **Averland sun-livery armour** tucked in a dusty corner. [[Hildegarde]] showed him a **freshly minted illegal penny** and asked whether he had seen others; he inspected it through a magnifying glass (and offered to sell her one), but **had not seen the counterfeits in peddlar circles**. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] gently probed whether his network had moved a certain physician; Helpmann **showed no recognition of the name [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]** and explained that his operation primarily moves **serfs to Averland farms**, not academics. [[Hildegarde]] left a shilling on the counter and asked him to send word if he heard of a young dark-haired woman traveling with a tall boxer.
