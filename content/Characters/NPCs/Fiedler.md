# Fiedler

## Description
A well-dressed man with a frilly collar, a neatly shaped goatee, and a hat worn at a sharp angle. Known to the party only as Fiedler. Keeps a crew who materialise out of the shadows of [[The Pig and Tack]] when he sits down to play.

## Role / Occupation
A gambler and fixer working out of [[The Pig and Tack]], with enough knowledge of the river trade to speak credibly about moving people out of [[Wolfenburg]] quietly.

## Personality
Game for a card table and willing to talk while playing, but candid about the limits of what he can arrange in the middle of a gang war.

## Relationships
- Runs with a crew at [[The Pig and Tack]]
- Operates in the orbit of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]], whose leader [[Anselm Rathsky]] holds court in the same room

## History with the Party
**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** Accepted [[Hildegarde]]'s invitation to a card game, stakes climbing to two shillings an ante. While she played deliberately badly to keep him talking, she probed him about smuggling someone out of the city discreetly. He warned that the gang war between the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] and the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fists]] had made leaving Wolfenburg nearly impossible, and that **barges caught in the crossfire had been known to burn on their way downriver**.

## Known Information
- Leaving the city quietly is currently very difficult because of the gang war
- Barges have burned downriver after being caught in the crossfire

## Open Questions
- Which gang, if either, does he actually answer to?
- Could he still be bought for a river passage if the Steel Fist pursuit were broken?
