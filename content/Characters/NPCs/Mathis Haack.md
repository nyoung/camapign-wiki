# Mathis Haack

## Description
A man dressed in roughly stitched hides and furs, emerging from within a [[Wayside Shrine]] on the road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]]. He carried a bag containing eight or nine large rabbits slung over his shoulder when the party first met him.

## Role / Occupation
Claims to be the **forest warden for Lord Jürgen Kimmelman**, whose lands he says stretch from the Shorts River down to the north spur near [[Dassel]].

## Personality
Affable on the surface — he offered to share his catch with the travelers without prompting. [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] and [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]] used the rabbits to cook a restorative broth for the party's wounded.

## Relationships
- **Lord Jürgen Kimmelman** — claims to serve as his forest warden
- **Party** — met at the shrine; fed them; under quiet suspicion from [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf]]** — appeared from inside the [[Wayside Shrine]] where the battered caravan made camp for the night, ~10 miles short of [[Dassel]]. Introduced himself and shared a bag of rabbits, which [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] and [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]] cooked into a broth for the wounded.
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]** — [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] woke him on the first watch to ask whether the shrine held any significance that might deter the bandits; he answered that it was just an old temple the [[Howling Ones]] would think nothing of. By morning he had **slipped away from camp without a word.**

## Known Information
- Wears stitched hides and furs; carries a bag of rabbits
- Claims to be forest warden for Lord Jürgen Kimmelman
- Appeared at the wayside shrine the party chose for a night camp; **vanished during the night of harassment**

## Open Questions
- **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] never trusted him.** A genuine forest warden would have no reason to poach rabbits from his own lord's lands — yet here he was, with a full bag and a story "with just enough gaps to be suspicious."
- He **disappeared overnight**, on the very night the [[Howling Ones]] harassed the camp — which does little to settle Boris's doubts about whose side he was really on.
