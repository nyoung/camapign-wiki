# Frau Busch

## Description
A matronly woman with neat gray hair, met at the door of her freshly whitewashed boarding house in [[Ferlangen]]. Stands like a small fortress of propriety amid a rough neighbourhood that has fallen apart around her over the years. Widowed; the house was left to her by her late husband, and she has no intention of leaving it.

## Role / Occupation
Keeps **[[Frau Busch's Boarding House]]** in [[Ferlangen]] — the establishment used by the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] to billet visiting investigators. Her daughter **Gertrude** assists with the practical work of running the house (baths, laundry, lice-checks).

## Personality
Strict, devout, unflappable. Lays out her rules without preamble and expects them obeyed. Tolerated [[Hildegarde]]'s unusual late-night request only after a frank explanation, and only on the condition the door was returned locked and the key returned by morning. Frosts visibly when guests linger past their welcome at the dinner table.

## House Rules (laid out on arrival)
- Wash before bed
- Be present for meals at **eight in the morning and five in the evening**
- **No drinking** on the premises
- No arriving in a drunken state
- Each guest receives a **single beeswax candle, to last the week**

## Relationships
- **Gertrude** — daughter; bathes the guests, checks for lice, handles the laundry
- **Late husband** — left her the house
- **[[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] (Ferlangen)** — sends her boarders, including the party
- **[[Hoffman Jannitzer]]** — guided the party to the house
- **Party** — current guests; granted [[Hildegarde]] a one-night key exception

## History with the Party
**[[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]:** Met the party at the door of her house, laid down her rules, and had Gertrude prepare baths and launder their clothes overnight. Issued each a beeswax candle. Later that night quietly granted [[Hildegarde]] a key to the front door for an unusual after-hours errand, on her insistence that it concerned the investigation rather than indiscretion.
