# Ewalt Wrangel

## Description
A priest of the Garden of Mórr who met the party as if he had been expecting their arrival. Has tended the bodies there for thirty years without complaint.

## Role / Occupation
Cleric overseeing the Garden of Mórr, Wolfenburg's cemetery and catacombs. Currently holding the three bodies from the Schraeder townhouse fire pending burial (plots not yet determined).

## Personality
Calm and accommodating — led the party through the cool, lily-scented interior to a viewing room, provided a small printed prayer book, and offered to remain just outside during the examination without pressing them.

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth]]** — granted the party access to examine the three bodies (the two Schraeder servants and [[Jaie Schraeder]] himself) at the Garden of Mórr. The party intends to question him further about what else the Garden might know.
- **[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]** — questioned further about the fire-victim bodies. Admitted the body attributed to [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was troubling: older, malnourished, missing teeth — not the picture of a well-kept servant. Confirmed the [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]] rumour of bodies gone missing from the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], and named its proprietor **[[Azmus Beham]]** as worth speaking to.

## Known Information
- Currently holding [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s body and his two servants', burials pending
- The body identified as [[Gerd Breytenbach]] is older, malnourished, and missing teeth — inconsistent with the real Breytenbach's description
- The Garden of Mórr itself remains secure, but bodies have gone missing from the nearby [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]; the City Watch has made no progress explaining it

## [[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]]
- **Recruited.** [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] told him the dead beneath the Temple of the All Gods *"do not sleep quietly"* and that the Keeper there is paying gangs to move corpses. Wrangel: *"I knew that Azmus was somehow a charlatan, cheating us, but I couldn't quite put my finger on the extent of his shenanigans… I'm willing to help in whatever way I can, within the laws of Mórr"*
- He **balked at going into a sewer** (*he looked green*). A group-assisted **challenging Charm** persuaded him; he **changed out of his robes** into **travelling boots and old trousers** and came along
- **Explained the Sigmarite crowd** at the cathedral as a local holy day, a *"throng and a training day"* in the plaza
- **Inspected the corpses** from the [[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]] discovery (rodent marks, otherwise untouched) and judged the burials **two to three months old**: *"That villain, he'll be brought to justice."* He has been told of [[Hilde Muntz]], an informant who must be got out of the city
- [[Christina Asper]]'s view of him: *"a bit of a toady… mostly harmless"*, related to more dangerous people. He is to lead the **funeral procession** through the North Gate

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- **Led the way and took charge in the catacombs.** At the place of the spirits he drew a **chalk circle** with letters, numbers and *yes*/*no*, set a **cloth-wrapped glass** in the centre, and communed with the dead. First name: **Melchior Pellen**, interred under three months; he *"performed the service myself"*
- **Found the niche empty** and diagnosed a **weakness between the planes**: the catacomb was **closed thirty years ago** for a link between the hedge and the terrestrial plane, and [[Azmus Beham]] **reopened it**. The spirits were **detached from their bodies but not moved on** — *"it is, as viewed by the Church of Mórr, a torture"*
- **Questioned about a dozen spirits**, all interred **within three years** of Azmus's keeping, and then performed the **last rites** (twenty minutes; the party repeated lines). Afterwards he was shaking and drenched; he asked the party **not to discuss the details**
- **Believes more than greed is involved:** *"some other… necromancy"* to disconnect the spirits from the bodies. *"I need to consult my library."* Ghouls are **not** a necromancer's work. He pledged support to *"bring justice to this and to these spirits, to this crypt, and to the Church of Mórr"*
- **Arranged the funeral procession** (gravedigger, hearse, six mourners from the Mourners' Guild) and **rode with it** to the North Gate in priestly robes
