# Ewalt Wrangel

## Description
A priest of the Garden of Mórr who met the party as if he had been expecting their arrival. Has tended the bodies there for thirty years without complaint.

## Role / Occupation
Cleric overseeing the Garden of Mórr, Wolfenburg's cemetery and catacombs. Currently holding the three bodies from the Schraeder townhouse fire pending burial (plots not yet determined).

## Personality
Calm and accommodating — led the party through the cool, lily-scented interior to a viewing room, provided a small printed prayer book, and offered to remain just outside during the examination without pressing them.

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth]]** — granted the party access to examine the three bodies (the two Schraeder servants and [[Jaie Schraeder]] himself) at the Garden of Mórr. The party intends to question him further about what else the Garden might know.
- **[[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]** — questioned further about the fire-victim bodies. Admitted the body attributed to [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was troubling: older, malnourished, missing teeth — not the picture of a well-kept servant. Confirmed the [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]] rumour of bodies gone missing from the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], and named its proprietor **[[Azmus Beham]]** as worth speaking to.

## Known Information
- Currently holding [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s body and his two servants', burials pending
- The body identified as [[Gerd Breytenbach]] is older, malnourished, and missing teeth — inconsistent with the real Breytenbach's description
- The Garden of Mórr itself remains secure, but bodies have gone missing from the nearby [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]; the City Watch has made no progress explaining it
