# Watch Captain (Wolfenburg)

## Description
A large, heavily built man with a scar on his chin. Pragmatic and fair.

## Role / Occupation
Oversees the Wolfenburg City Watch.

## History with the Party
**[[Session 30 - Hanging Judge]]:** Personally accompanied the party to [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s wagon outside the North Gate to verify the ~20 severed [[Howling Ones]] heads, then had them counted and paid out five gold crowns. Accepted the bandits' coded notes and the werewolf's teeth as evidence, confirmed Wolfenburg has dealt with werewolves for centuries (the Wolf's Bane at the gates is no decoration), and granted the party official consultancy status with free passage through the city gates.

## Known Information
- Unsurprised by the werewolf evidence — the city has a long institutional memory of the threat
- Not to be confused with **[[Watch Captain Tringen Melber]]**, a different Watch Captain posted in [[Middenheim]]
