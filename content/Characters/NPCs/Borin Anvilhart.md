# Borin Anvilhart

## Description
Clan elder of the dwarfs of [[Wolfenburg]], who presides over the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]] — a hall of dwarfs drinking malt beer that **fell entirely silent** when the party walked in. Direct to the point of bluntness: he demanded to know their business before anything else.

## Role / Occupation
Clan elder. He has authority over the city's **dwarven sewer jacks** — the dwarfs who work and maintain [[Wolfenburg Sewers|Wolfenburg's sewers]] — and over the **four-tumbler sewer keys** that grant access to them. He can loan a key, and he can assign a jack as an escort.

## Personality
Gruff and formal. He responded well to being treated with respect — [[Hildegarde]] offered a **curtsy** before the party explained themselves, and he heard them out and helped. He deals in **oaths**: the key he loaned came with a 48-hour term and a sworn undertaking to return it.

## Relationships
- **[[Snorri Belgrimson]]** — a sewer jack under his authority, whom he assigned as the party's guide
- **The party** — helped them on a first meeting, on terms of respect and an oath

## History with the Party
Met once, in [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]].

## Known Information
- **A four-tumbler dwarven sewer key was stolen in broad daylight** from one of his sewer jacks — he believes almost certainly by one of [[Wolfenburg]]'s criminal gangs
- **A younger dwarf of his hall saw bodies wrapped in linen and sewn shut** being moved surreptitiously through the tunnels
- He warned that the route to the **catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]** is treacherous — slippery platforms and winding passages
