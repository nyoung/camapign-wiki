# Derich Kölderer

## Description
A name only — a physician newly registered with the **[[Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)|Physicians' Guild]]** in [[Ferlangen]] roughly a month before [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]. The Guild Master mentioned him in passing when the party asked after [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]: a separate practitioner, by the Guild's reckoning, who happened to register around the time the party would expect [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] (or someone using his methods) to begin operating.

The party heard the name spoken aloud and noted it down phonetically as "**Derek Coldberg**"; the registered guild name is **Derich Kölderer**.

## Role / Occupation
Practising physician in [[Ferlangen]] — registered with the [[Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)|Physicians' Guild]].

## Relationships

## History with the Party
**[[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]:** Named by the [[Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)|Physicians' Guild]] administrator when the party presented [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s warrant — offered as the only recently-arrived physician of note. The Guild Master did **not** suggest Kölderer was the man on the warrant.
