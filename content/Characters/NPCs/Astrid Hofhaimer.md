# Astrid Hofhaimer

## Description
A woman with **striking orange eyes and pinpoint pupils** that seem to look straight through whoever she addresses. When the party met her at parlay, her **right arm was wrapped in a bandage soaked dark with blood**. She introduced herself through her demands rather than her name, riding out alone and unafraid to face an armed escort.

## Role / Occupation
The local leader of the **[[Howling Ones]]** on the road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]] — the figure directing the ambushes and the night-long harassment of [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s caravan. The captured prisoner named her, in a whispered warning, simply as **"Astrid" — someone the party very much did not want to meet.**

## Personality
Cold, contemptuous, and entirely in control. She dismissed every overture [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] offered — an alliance against a rival merchant, appeals to her sense of leadership — without a flicker of interest, gave the party a hard deadline, and rode away. She trades in ultimatums, not negotiation.

## Relationships
- **[[Howling Ones]]** — leads the band harassing the caravan; her bowmen obey her
- **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]** — wants his **promissory notes**; will not be bought off with anything else
- **The party** — issued them a noon ultimatum; treats them with cold contempt

## History with the Party
- **[[Session 26 - A Night in the Country]]** — after a night of harassment at the [[Wayside Shrine]], she answered the morning with a **parlay**. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] rode out to meet her in a roadside clearing. She demanded [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s **promissory notes** by **noon** or her bowmen would harry the caravan all the way to [[Dassel]], dismissed every counter-offer, and rode back into the trees. The party resolved to bait a counter-ambush rather than comply.

## Known Information
- Named "Astrid Hofhaimer"; the prisoner's whispered warning was simply "Astrid"
- Orange eyes with pinpoint pupils; **wounded right arm** at the parlay
- Leads the [[Howling Ones]] band on this stretch of road
- Wants only [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s promissory notes; unmoved by alliance or appeal
- **Confirmed operating within [[Wolfenburg]] itself, in full human form** — cornered [[Hildegarde]] near the Myrmidia compound, reclaimed a wolf's fang item by force, and passed a wolf's mane with no reaction while leaving through the city gates ([[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]])
- Explicitly named herself as leader of the Howling Ones to Hildegarde's face

## Open Questions
- Her **bandaged, bloodied right arm** was on the same side as the wound [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] dealt the monstrous wolf at the [[Mountain Pass Choke Point]] in [[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf|Session 25]] — something the party noticed but has not yet remarked on aloud.

## Session 27 — The Wolf Returns
- The wolf entered the battle on the road between [[Hasselhund]] and [[Dassel]] — partially healed (the same leg wound from [[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf|Session 25]]), but functional
- Its howl caused **terror across the entire battlefield**: every horse present bolted except [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s [[Orazio]], who stood firm alone; [[Herman Wurt|Herman]]'s horse [[Vortex]] was already down from a critical arrow and could not run
- Lunged directly at [[Herman Wurt|Herman]], who was on foot in the mud after his horse was struck; the session ended with the wolf attacking him
- The party still has not said aloud what Astrid is — but the clues now include a second shared wounding and an identical howl

## Session 28 — Transformation and Escape
- The wolf **killed [[Herman Wurt]]**, tearing out his throat, then **transformed on the spot into a hybrid humanoid-wolf form** — direct on-screen confirmation that this creature is a true werewolf
- Entered a frenzy, savaging the party's horses, before [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] (permanently burning a point of resilience) and **[[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]]** (killing blow to the leg) brought it down, apparently lifeless, in its hybrid form
- When the party returned to deal with the body, it had **vanished** — only a blood trail and rapidly lengthening tracks leading into the forest, healing and outpacing pursuit
- The party has still not spoken **Astrid's** name aloud in connection to the wolf, but the identification (matching wound, matching howl, now a witnessed transformation) is essentially certain

## Session 32 — A Tooth For a Tooth
- Cornered [[Hildegarde]] alone near the Myrmidia compound in Wolfenburg, in full human form, and demanded back a wrapped wolf's fang the party had been carrying — "You have something that belongs to me"
- Caught Hildegarde's knife hand with inhuman speed when she tried to pickpocket her instead, wrenched the fang free, and pressed it into her own jaw — it visibly shrank into a human canine and was absorbed by tendrils of flesh within seconds
- Told Hildegarde she was "on my list to kill... but not today," then walked out through the closing city gates; the guards dismissed Hildegarde's warnings after Astrid accused her of harassment
- Passed a wolf's mane at the gate with **no reaction whatsoever**, remaining entirely human throughout the whole encounter
- Item significance: the wolf's fang appears able to regrow into one of her own teeth — worth tracking what else the item can do or reveal
