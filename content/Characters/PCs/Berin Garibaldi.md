# Berin "Orsetto" Garibaldi

## Description
Berin is a lean, pockmarked southerner with darker skin and the careful bearing of someone trained in both discipline and observation. He wears frayed but clearly quality clothing bearing the golden sunburst of the [[Knights of the Blazing Sun]], the fraying an honest record of real hardship. He is accompanied everywhere by his horse [[Orazio]] — a large, friendly dark brown animal who noses at people who smell of carrots and responds to Berin's voice across a chaotic courtyard.

## Personality
Berin is measured where others are reactive, philosophical where others are blunt. He ordered Lonningbruck Ruby wine for the table and observed that happiness is found in life's simple pleasures. He is not soft: he charged [[Orazio]] through the Forest Inn gate in a thundering assault, pinned Melmoth's skilled swordsman to the wall with lethal force, and severed Melmoth's head and mounted it on the gatepost. He then tended to [[Hildegarde]]'s near-fatal neck wound and [[Orazio]]'s injuries with the same unhurried competence.

His faith is practical and present. He set up a makeshift altar at the Forest Inn stable. He poured a libation to Myrmidia rather than drink the spiced cider Gunter brought him — the gesture quiet but absolute. He offered alms readily: six shillings to Manfred Teuschel over Elfrieda's objection; healing to farmers on the road. He argued the party couldn't serve as unbiased judges for Torben, measured and careful where others react on instinct.

He is the only party member who can read classical texts. He read Stradovski's patient notes and identified the wasting disease symptoms. He noticed and interpreted Elfrieda Teuschel's micro-expressions, assessed she was hiding the true nature of her cure, and said so. He gave the Seuchenbuch a "knowledge is fire" assessment — useful, dangerous, and not to be treated lightly.

His bond with [[Orazio]] is genuine and particular. He calls to the horse in the language of their bond, and [[Orazio]] answers.

## Background
Berin's background spans two careers that sit in unusual harmony: horse trader and Myrmidian novitiate. The first gave him practical knowledge of animals, travel, and commerce; the second gave him discipline, formal bearing, medical training, and the capacity to read classical texts. He was introduced to the party in [[Beeckerhoven]] by a priestess of Verena, brought in as a reliable replacement for mercenaries who had fled with their payment. He joined on the party's warrant for [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] and has remained.

## Campaign History (Sessions 09–26)
- **Session 09:** As the only member literate in classical texts, examined the [[Seuchenbuch|Plague Book]] and concluded it concerned *weaponising* disease, not curing it — comparing it to controversial texts in the [[Knights Panther]] libraries and to fire ("useful in skilled hands, catastrophic if misused"). Ordered Lonningbruck Ruby wine and mused that happiness lies in life's simple pleasures. Stayed overnight at the [[City Stables (Beeckerhoven)|stables]] guarding [[Orazio]]; his intuition flagged the stable boy as withholding. Paid a West Gate guard "five shillings for each of [[Hildegarde]]'s children."
- **Session 10:** Estimated from military knowledge that 8–10 men would be needed to hold the [[Medical Union]] compound. When the melee broke out, charged [[Orazio]] at the gate, climbed the wall, and opened the gate from above.
- **Session 11:** Ended the compound fight by **invoking a writ of arrest in the name of the [[Knights of the Blazing Sun]]**, cowing the guards into surrender. [[Orazio]] reared and critically struck [[Sebald Brenz]]. Exposed the [[Church of Morr]] paperwork as a crude forgery (signed with an "X"); stabilised the gravely wounded [[Hugo (Medical Union)|Hugo]]; examined a dissected corpse; showed [[Manfred Teuschel]] the minted coin.
- **Session 12:** Read the wrecked coach correctly (no ambush — it had simply mired in mud ruts). Grew uneasy studying the [[Seuchenbuch|Plague Book]] — its formulas require plague materials, pointing toward [[Seuchenshof]]. Examined the [[Haleth Shrine]]; judged the wife sale "more customary than commercial."
- **Session 13:** Studied the carvings at the [[Temple to Liadriel|Liadriel inn-temple]] (not genuine elvish script). Explained mutation to [[Hildegarde]] as dark humours falling out of balance — "the soul is a fortress." Gave farmers minor healing and learned three riders had bypassed the toll. At the [[Forest Inn]], inspected its defences, noted the **total absence of animals**, and — handed a spiced cider by [[Gunter]] — poured it out as an offering to [[Myrmidia]] rather than drink it. Kept watch all night in the stable with [[Orazio]].
- **Session 14:** Stood a meditative vigil (sword across his knees, plate helm on). Descended first into the **bone cellar**, confirming the stripped bones were cattle and fowl. When the abduction erupted, called [[Orazio]] to him "in the language of their bond," mounted under fire, and charged the kidnappers.
- **Session 15:** Drove [[Orazio]] through the gate in a thundering charge and **pinned [[Melmoth]]'s skilled swordsman to the wall with lethal force**, then **slew and beheaded Melmoth**, mounting the head on the gatepost. **Cut a strip from his own cloak to staunch [[Hildegarde]]'s near-fatal neck wound, saving her life**, then tended [[Orazio]]. His firm interrogation broke [[Torben Pieknich]], exposing Stradovski's betrayal.
- **Session 16:** Argued the party could not be Torben's unbiased judges. Defused the [[Skjaldberg]] militia's suspicion by recognising the "Drakwald Hunters" as a sports team; took a gut-punch trying to break up the inn brawl with knightly authority. Took the night watch and discovered every corpse — and Melmoth's head — had been reclaimed by the ghouls overnight.
- **Session 17:** Surrendered the Khaine tome to the Royal Wolf militia; laid [[Melmoth]]'s dark sword and the tome before [[Christina Asper]]. Sparred the militia captain (his sword against a cavalry hammer).
- **Session 18:** Offered to stand as innkeeper [[Anton Petz]]'s champion in a judicial duel; confronted [[Christina Asper|Asper]] over neglecting her Solkanite duty (comparing her unfavourably to [[Condradine]]); loosened his sword to cow the Grunwald farmer into freeing [[Herga]]; taught [[Hildegarde]] basic combat drills on the road.
- **Session 19:** Noticed the odd feed-storage at the [[Fist of Amber]]; verified [[Marretta Schongauer]]'s sealed letter was genuine; researched the cult symbols in the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Verenan library]] and identified **[[Khaine]], god of murder**; saluted [[Myrmidia]] at the Sigmar gate-altar before complying.
- **Session 20:** Proposed surrendering the [[Stradovski Vellum Papers|treatise]] to the temple (overruled by Pilpoul); formally apologised to [[Christina Asper|Asper]]; donated to the [[Temple of Shallya (Ferlangen)|Temple of Shallya]] and asked about the [[Wolfenburg]] disease cluster; bought a set of Averland-sun armour; trained [[Orazio]] to come when called.
- **Session 21:** Wisely declined the dwarven ale (asked for wine, met with a blank stare); extracted the drunken party from [[Sam Bugman's Brewery]] (collecting a hangover remedy); was pelted with vegetables seeking help; charged into the alley brawl with the flat of his blade; was nearly beheaded by [[Mangold]] before [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] stopped him; introduced himself formally as a Knight of the Blazing Sun and arranged the [[Wolfenburg]] escort.
- **Session 22:** Rode with [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] to the [[Fist of Amber]] and bought wine while gathering intelligence from [[Marretta Schongauer]] (Howling Ones werewolves; von Kolditz's trafficking).
- **Session 23:** Read the cut-through-hills terrain as perfect ambush ground and **warned [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] directly — who dismissed it moments before the first volley fell**. [[Orazio]] refused the slope, sensing the hidden archers. Cut down a fleeing bandit, ran down and captured the last one alive, then tended the wounded draft horses with his farrier's kit and prayed to [[Myrmidia]].
- **Session 24:** With the surrendering outlaw [[Ganav|Hawkeye]], **performed the field surgery that saved [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s life** (cloak as a clean surface, sterile linens, farrier's pliers to draw the arrow), praying to [[Myrmidia]] throughout and carrying real guilt for failing to protect him. **Vouched for Hawkeye, accepted his oath of parole**, and won the argument to keep him alive as a ward of his honour. Scouted the hillside (near-invisible camouflage, a wolf symbol), treated a draft horse, and drew from [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] the truth of the **promissory notes** — concluding someone with inside knowledge was directing the bandits.
- **Session 25:** Led the advance on foot behind his shield, urging the party to spread out; pressed up the arrow-swept slope alone, bluffing that the bandits' leader was already taken; struck down an outlaw. Afterward **stabilised [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s broken jaw and torn cheek**, judged [[Ganav]]'s arrow too deep to remove without a [[Wolfenburg]] surgeon, captured and intimidated the last outlaw (threat of the Sigmarite Templars), deflected Danderich's demand to hang the prisoners, and chose the defensible [[Wayside Shrine]] for the night.
- **Session 26:** Built branch barricades around the wagons; "interrogated" the prisoner by reading the books of [[Myrmidia]] at him for hours; steadied [[Herman Wurt|Herman]] on watch in Battle Tongue; healed [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s wounded horse on Myrmidia's holy day. Rode out with [[Hildegarde]] to **parlay with [[Astrid Hofhaimer]]** (met only contempt); floated allying with the rival merchant **Hofbauer**; and devised the **decoy-and-counter-ambush plan** the party rallied behind.
