# Screaming Mushrooms

## Description
Tiny **walking fungi** found growing in the dirt along the walkways of the main [[Wolfenburg Sewers|sewer channels]] beneath [[Wolfenburg]]. They look like a mix of **red-capped and white mushrooms**, **3–4 inches tall**, no taller than a boot. The tendrils at their base have grown into **tiny legs**. When light or people approach they **squeal**, detach from the ground and **skitter away** at a snail's pace, often tripping, falling into the channel, or toppling over and kicking helplessly.

- Apparently **sentient**, or at least aware: they peek around corners to see whether intruders are still there

## Encounter Notes

### Wolfenburg sewers ([[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]])
- Found in the **eastern** main channel, the one reached from [[The Ghoul Cavern]]'s side tunnel; **none** in the western channel toward the junction
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] **kicked one** screaming across the channel into the water (and briefly wondered about using them as sling ammunition; too light). [[Ganav]] warned it was a bad omen to needlessly hurt lesser beings. [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] **stomped and sliced** every one that came near
- **[[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]]'s explanation:** goblins. *"Only a messed-up gobbo on some kind of mushroom hallucinogen would decide to bring fungus to life."* Goblin bands have occasionally found their way into the sewers, never a full infestation
- By their size, they had been growing **at least a week, maybe a fortnight**, so **nobody had come that way recently** (or had found a way past without setting them off)

## Weaknesses / Tactics
- Harmless in themselves. The danger is **the noise**: anyone in the tunnels will know the party is coming
