# Ghoul

## Description
Grey-skinned, red-eyed, sharp-toothed humanoids that hang from ceilings and walls and feed on the dead. The party has met them twice: the **degenerate, ex-human ghouls of the [[Forest Inn]]** ([[Session 15 - Stradovski's Guide to Selling Out Your Friends|Session 15]]) — townsfolk turned by [[Melmoth]] — and a **new colony under Wolfenburg** ([[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]).

## Traits
- Climb walls and ceilings (**"parkouring"** off cavern walls, swinging from stalactites)
- **Poison** — lesser ghouls: basic poison, resisted by an Average test; the **elder**: a paralysing venom (a **Difficult Endurance** test — fail and you are paralysed but conscious, which the GM noted would let ghouls "devour you alive")
- Fight with sharpened bones (a **sharpened tibia** in the case of the Wolfenburg elder)

## Encounter Notes

### Wolfenburg — the elder ghoul and pack ([[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]])
- Found in **[[The Ghoul Cavern]]**, a natural cavern reached through a **hand-punched three-foot hole** at the end of a side tunnel off the [[Wolfenburg Sewers]] — with **linen-wrapped corpses** and **piles of bones** nearby
- The fight was **fought in the dark, cramped, at range and in melee** at once; **long weapons were impractical** (the party's longbow and Snorri's spear both hampered)
- **At least four enemies** were in the fight: a ceiling ghoul, a "drunk"/"insecure" ghoul softened by [[Ganav]] and shot in the shoulder by [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], further lesser ghouls, and the **elder** — a larger, meaner ghoul **adorned with spikes, bones and scraps of skin**
- **The elder** swung down from a stalactite onto [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]], attacked with a **sharpened tibia and a paralysing bite**, took a **cutting critical to the left arm** (dropping one tibia), was **stunned by [[Hildegarde]]'s scream**, dropped **prone by Boris's sling crit**, took **a sword through the eye from Berin**, and a **crushing critical to the head from Hildegarde's mace** that burst its eyes
- **At session's end the elder was alive**: stunned ×3, blinded ×2, prone, bleeding — *"nearly dead"*. **One other ghoul "went down"**; one was **called dead** in Round 1
- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]] — the fight finished.** The elder was **killed by [[Hildegarde]]'s free coup de grace**. [[Ganav]] killed a lesser ghoul with a leg critical; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] crippled another's leg; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] finished (probably) the hopping **"cruel" ghoul**; [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] killed the last, the **"grieving"** one, spear through the throat. The lesser ghouls' poison **stacks penalties on its own resistance test**; **Resolve can clear it** (Ganav spent his last)
- The cavern held a **ghoul nest** of old bones, a body being eaten from an opened ribcage, and **two dead Lurkers**. The GM: *ghouls don't reproduce*
- See [[Butcher's Bill]]

### The Forest Inn ([[Session 15 - Stradovski's Guide to Selling Out Your Friends|Session 15]]–[[Session 16 - Plus ça change|16]])
- Ex-human ghouls run by [[Melmoth]]; **[[Rolf]] decapitated one** in a single bite; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] crumpled another with a critical sling stone
- The ghoul network **reclaimed every body overnight** — including Melmoth's severed head

## Weaknesses / Tactics
- **Open ground and light** help the party; the cavern's **cramped, dark, uneven space** hurt them
- **Stunning and blinding** work: Hildegarde's scream and Boris's sling crit neutralised the elder for several rounds
- Poison is **applied by the hit itself** — the party can only resist the damage, not the application (GM ruling)
