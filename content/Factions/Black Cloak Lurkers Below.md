# Black Cloak Lurkers Below

## Overview
A gang operating out of Wolfenburg's sewers and catacombs, identifiable by black cloaks and tribal tattoos. Their name and the tattoos were independently confirmed by a Myrmidian priest during [[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]], matching tattoos Hildegarde had already spotted on a Shallyan novitiate connected to the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]'s splinter-sect thread.

## Goals
Survive the war they started. Having robbed the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]'s lottery takings, they are now being hunted and are losing people. [[Anselm Rathsky]] frames the robbery as taking back money the Steel Fists had themselves stolen from the Prince; [[Captain Crais]] says the Steel Fists simply ran the lottery — which would make it a straightforward robbery dressed up as a principle.

## Structure / Leadership
- **[[Anselm Rathsky]]** — **the gang's leader**, not merely a frontman as the party was first told. Holds court from the darkest corner of [[The Pig and Tack]]; reachable through the tavern's dwarf bartender

## Notable Members
- [[Anselm Rathsky]] — leader
- Three unnamed members encountered chasing [[Deke Kudgel Brand|Deke "Kudgel" Brand]] in [[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]
- **[[Gerd Breytenbach]]** — by his own account to his mother, had been "helping out" the gang about a week before he vanished

## Resources & Influence
Specialists in thievery, lockpicking, and fencing stolen goods through channels that leave no trace ([[Captain Crais]]). Operate out of the city's sewers and catacombs. Tied, via tattoo, to a secretive splinter sect operating out of the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] — a connection the party hasn't yet pulled on, and one Rathsky volunteered nothing about. Rathsky claims he can get a person out of the city, gang war notwithstanding.

## Relationship with the Party
Cordial and transactional. The members encountered in Session 34 backed down once they realized the party wasn't the City Watch. In Session 35 Rathsky spoke at length with the party and offered them a deal (see [[Stop the Steel Fists' Pursuit]]), leaving a standing line of contact — though his account of the war's origin is contradicted by [[Captain Crais]].

## Known Activities
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Three members chased [[Deke Kudgel Brand|Deke "Kudgel" Brand]] through a Wolfenburg alley, hunting stolen lottery money tied to "the Prince's reconstruction fund." Boris and Berin talked them down; the party caught a clear look at their tribal tattoos, matching those on the Shallyan novitiate seen in [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]].

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** [[Anselm Rathsky]] gave his account of the war — **the Lurkers stole the lottery takings from the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fists]], who he said had already stolen them from the Prince.** The heist of the heist. The Steel Fists are now hunting every Lurker they can find and the gang is bleeding. Rathsky offered the party passage out of the city for someone, in exchange for breaking that pursuit. **[[Captain Crais]] contradicted the framing the same day**, describing the Steel Fists as *running* the illegal lottery rather than having stolen it — which would make the Lurkers' raid a plain robbery rather than a reclamation.

## Session 37 — Especially the Hugs
- **🔴🔴 THE LURKERS LIED TO THE PARTY.** [[Lucas Pollack]] revealed at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] that the real stolen money belonged to **the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]]'s illegal lottery, not the Prince's** — and that **the Lurkers misdirected the party to obscure their own involvement** in the theft
- [[Anselm Rathsky]]'s "heist of the heist" account in [[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]] was **a fabrication**. [[Captain Crais]]'s version was correct all along
- **🔴 They are hunting one of their own former members.** [[Hilde Muntz]], an **ex-Lurker** who has since sought a calling with the Shallyans, is **trapped inside the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]** with the Lurkers watching the exits alongside the Steel Fists
- **They will not violate the temple's sanctuary** — it is too important a source of healing and charity for their own people — **but they will not let her leave either**
- They are also **watching [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]]** whenever those two move through the city, suspecting their connection to Hilde
- **A gang stole a four-tumbler dwarven sewer key** in broad daylight from a [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarf Hall]] sewer jack ([[Borin Anvilhart]]'s report). Given the Lurkers' name and their use of the undercity, they are an obvious suspect — **but this is not established**

## Session 39 — Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently
- **Two dead Lurkers** in the ghoul nest of [[The Ghoul Cavern]]: black hooded cloaks, swords, and a **[[Forged Dwarf Key]]**. This is the first physical link between the Lurkers and **copies of the dwarves' sewer key**
- The party took **two Lurker cloaks**, now worn by [[Ganav]] and [[Hildegarde]]
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s read:** if [[Azmus Beham]] pays the Lurkers to collect bodies, **nobody has been collecting them lately**. Bodies are left in the tunnels, and the [[Screaming Mushrooms]] show a stretch of sewer unused for a week or more. *"Something seems to have happened to the Lurkers."*
- **At the sewer junction**, at least two Lurkers challenged the party (*"Hail, Lurker! Karl Franz!"*, possibly a password). A prisoner ruse failed, but **[[Ganav]]'s threat** (*vacate it for a week, or permanently*) worked: *"We were leaving anyway, but we won't be back this way for another week."* They withdrew **east**

## Session 42 — I Almost Killed You All
- **A cell of three fought and broken.** [[Karel Vantz]] (older, bearded, swordsman), [[Petra Voss]] (slinger) and [[Anselm Rathke]] (young footpad) took [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] hostage ([[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]) and came to the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] for whatever [[Azmus Beham]] had left them. Anselm was knocked out, Petra stunned and captured, and Karel fled
- **Their grievance with Azmus:** he paid them to dump bodies in the Wolfen at 3 a.m., **stopped paying** and vanished. Two of their body-movers, **Gunther** and **Fritz**, disappeared around Hexenstag; Petra faltered to hear two Lurkers had been found half-eaten by ghouls
- **They claim not to know** about the spirits, the ghouls (those sewers are not theirs), the Medical Union, or the burning of [[Gerd Breytenbach]]'s household. They **avoid the Shallyan orphanage**
- **"Nobody leaves the Lurkers"** — [[Hilde Muntz]] knows where all the operations are, so they will not let her go; that is why Marla was seized
- **Two Lurkers handed to Verena:** the board posts the Lurkers at **2 shillings, alive for questioning**; Hilde was paid **3 shillings** for Petra and Anselm
- [[Gerd Breytenbach]] had been passing tips about the Physicians Guild to "Karel" (so the gang could reach doctors); Petra had not seen him in months
