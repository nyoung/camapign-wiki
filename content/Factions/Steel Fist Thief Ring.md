# Steel Fist Thief Ring

## Overview
One of [[Wolfenburg]]'s two major thief gangs, at open war with the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]. Per [[Captain Crais]], they **run an illegal lottery** of their own — a going concern the Prince recently cracked down on, not a theft — and the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] robbed their takings. They are now hunting every Lurker they can find. **Not yet directly encountered by the party.**

> **Two conflicting accounts.** [[Anselm Rathsky]] told the party the Steel Fists had *stolen* the lottery money from the Prince before the Lurkers took it from them ("the heist of the heist"). [[Captain Crais]] said the Steel Fists simply ran the lottery and the Prince suppressed it. Crais's version is disinterested; Rathsky's was delivered mid-recruitment and makes his own gang's robbery sound like justice. Treat Crais as the reliable account until the party proves otherwise.

## Goals
Recover their stolen lottery takings and punish the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] for taking them. Their pursuit is relentless enough that [[Anselm Rathsky]] describes his own organisation as bleeding. Note that on Crais's account the money was **theirs** — earned running the lottery — which makes the retaliation a good deal more understandable, and the Lurkers a good deal less heroic, than Rathsky implied.

## Resources & Influence
Hold the **southwest tenements along the city wall** as their main territory ([[Reiner Kolber]]), a district where even priestly garb earns little trust after dark. Identify their own by a **flat cap in a distinctive red-orange**; wearing one by accident in the wrong neighbourhood is reportedly very dangerous. Their reach extends to the river — barges caught in the crossfire have burned on their way downriver ([[Fiedler]]).

## Relationship with the Party
None yet. [[Hildegarde]] has bought a pale flat cap to dye red-orange, intending either to draw them out or to use as an opener when approaching them.
