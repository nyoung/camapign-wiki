- **🔴 [[Find Professor Stradovski]] — THE BIGGEST BREAK IN THE HUNT.** [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] was cornered at the [[Wolfenburg Docks]], **surrendered, and was interrogated** ([[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]).
