# Butcher's Bill

A running tally of everything the party and its allies have killed, wounded, or taken
alive. Covers **Sessions 01–44** (17 Nachexen – 18 Jahrdrung, 2512). *Sessions 43 and 44 had no kills.*

> [!note] Two tiers of sourcing
> **Sessions 23, 25, 27, 28, 36, 37, 38, 39, and 40** — the densest road-war, docks and
> catacomb fighting — are now **transcript-verified** *(S39–S40 from local Whisper transcriptions of the
> Craig recordings, since GM Assistant had no credits left)*: pulled from GM Assistant's speaker-labelled session
> recordings via its API and read line by line for actual dice/damage/kill call-outs,
> not just the narrative recap. Everything else is still read from the processed session
> notes (narrative summaries), marked accordingly. A **confirmed** kill has a named killer
> in the source; **probable** describes a decisive blow without confirming death;
> **indirect** means death followed from a party action without a killing blow.

## What the transcripts changed

The first pass (narrative-summary only) got several things wrong. In order of how much
they matter:

- **[[Session 40 - A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan|Session 40]] added the first living enemy since the road war.** In the sewer ambush near the catacomb gate, **one [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] thug** died and **two fled**. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s sling critical to the head dropped one off the platform (*"effectively out of combat for good… I think he was pretty dead"*), and [[Hildegarde]]'s crossbow critical (*"brain damage — blinded, stunned, fatigued"*) is recorded on the same man; the table says one died. Credited **jointly**

- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|S39]] settled the ghoul fight.** At the start of the session the GM confirmed the elder was
  still down but alive (*"any further attacks will kill him"*) with **three other ghouls** up. The kills:
  **[[Hildegarde]]** took the elder with a free coup de grace (her player was absent; EndyShrike ran her);
  **[[Ganav]]** killed one with a leg critical (*"we'll call it dead. Ganav kills the ghoul"*);
  **[[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]]** killed the last (*"Snorri slays the last ghoul"*, spear through the throat);
  and **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** probably finished the hopping "cruel" ghoul (*"not going to be doing
  much more… we won't even bother to roll"*), after [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] crippled a ghoul's leg with a
  critical. All four are counted below; Berin's as probable
- **[[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|S38]]'s "two ghouls down" is wrong — and no
  ghoul has a confirmed killer.** GM Assistant's recap says [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s
  enchanted sword through the eye killed the elder ghoul, and that [[Hildegarde]]'s mace
  then crushed a second ghoul's skull "beside its elder." The transcript shows **both
  criticals landing on the same elder ghoul**: Berin's sword-through-the-eye (*"one to the
  donglers and one to the eye… he wishes he was dead"*) and, a few beats later, Hildegarde's
  crushing critical that burst its remaining eye. The GM's own state at the end of the
  recording: *"stunned and blind and prone and bleeding… nearly dead… three stun conditions,
  two blind conditions… more over than overkill."* **The elder was alive when the session
  ended mid-round**, with the fight to be "mopped up" next time. The only kill call in the
  whole session is one line in Round 1, right after a critical roll — *"we will call him
  dead"* — and the transcript does **not** say whose blow it was or which ghoul. A separate
  late hit made one ghoul **"go down"** (status unconfirmed). Berin's and [[Ganav]]'s own
  dialogue is missing from the labelled transcript (only the GM, Hildegarde and Boris are
  labelled), so their actions here rest on the GM's narration
- **[[Christina Asper]] has killed someone.** [[Session 28 - A Knight to Dismember|S28]]'s
  transcript has it in four words — *"she killed him dead"* — during the werewolf ambush.
  The earlier claim that the self-described executioner had killed nothing on screen was
  wrong.
- **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s S23 "devastating shot to a bandit's head" was not a kill.**
  The transcript shows the arrow landing (a genuine 18-damage hit, after he'd already
  fumbled his whole quiver onto the road once that session) — but the target then
  **got away**: *"I'm gonna say this guy gets away, the one that Boris shot."* Boris
  himself objected on the recording ("No, come back, I want to claim my kill") and lost
  the argument.
- **[[Danderich Flisshausten|Sir Danderich]]'s kill count was undercounted, not
  overcounted.** [[Session 27 - Fight!|S27]]'s transcript shows a single mounted charge
  chaining **death blows against up to five targets**, with at least three explicitly
  confirmed as kills in the GM's dice-narration ("that would also be a kill" ×2, on top of
  the opening arm-through kill), plus a **separate, later** kill-and-chain-finish in the
  same session. He also killed at least one bandit *after* that bandit had surrendered to
  him — Danderich was the one they surrendered to, not [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]].
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s S37 "cut a man in the groin" was a kill, and the victim has
  a name.** Under City Watch questioning, a surviving stevedore testifies: *"I think he's
  the one who killed Gunther, cut him bad in the groin."* **Gunther** — otherwise unnamed
  anywhere in the vault — is the first named casualty on the party's side of the ledger
  from the docks fight.
- **The [[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|S37]] docks toll was undercounted.** The
  processed note recorded "one stevedore bled out." The transcript has the GM confirming
  at least **two, likely three**, dead in quick succession during Boris's shooting: *"that
  guy that you cut... dead," "the other one, the cynical one, that's dead now,"* then a
  third *"next guy... he's dead."* Notably, the crew had been **pulling their punches**
  against Boris and Berin the whole fight — *"they weren't trying to kill"* — which makes
  the one-sidedness of the beating even starker.
- **[[Gromril van Aelst]] has a kill.** The gnome merchant, not previously credited with
  landing a blow all campaign, put a crossbow bolt through a bandit's leg artery in S27:
  *"your arrow pierces his left leg, hitting the left leg artery, and he bleeds out."*
- **The S27–28 road battle has a hard body count, stated by the GM at the table:**
  *"There's about, I want to say, 10 to 12 bodies of outlaws."* That's the single most
  precise number in the whole campaign, and it accounts for roughly half the 20-head
  bounty on its own.
- **Horse casualties run higher than recorded**, and the table itself lost count. Beyond
  [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s horse (spine severed, [[Session 25 - Asper and
  the Wolf|S25]]), a **second horse bled out and died** later the same session, and
  [[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]]'s horse died at some point in S27–28 ("the other horse that
  died here"). By the end of S28 the table argued over whether one or two horse bodies
  remained on the field and settled on one — the true toll across the whole arc is
  genuinely unclear even to the people who played it.

Sessions 01, 07, 10–11, and 14–15 (the Sewer Horror, Were-Rabbit, Medical Union, and
Forest Inn fights — source of [[Rolf]], [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], [[Hildegarde]], and
[[Falconius Thorncobble|Connie]]'s credited kills) have **not** been transcript-checked
yet. Those entries below are still narrative-summary only.

## Attribution vs. the payout

Two different questions, two different numbers:

**How many actually died in the road war?** The GM's own stated count for the S27–28
fight — the largest single engagement — was *"10 to 12 bodies of outlaws."* Add S23's 1
confirmed kill and whatever S25 contributed, and the real total is somewhere in that
neighbourhood, independent of whatever number got narrated later at the payout.

**Of that, how many have a named killer?** Roughly **12–14**:
[[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] 6+, [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] 2, [[Mangold]] ~2–3,
[[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]] 1, [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] 1. That's a healthy majority of
the S27–28 fight's own 10–12, even before counting S23 and S25 — attribution here is
actually in good shape. The gap isn't the count, it's naming the rest of the hands.

**The "20 heads, 5 gold crowns"** from [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]] is a
separate fact: it's what got paid, advertised in [[Ferlangen]] at **ten shillings a
head**. Treat it as the in-fiction payout, not as the denominator for the tallies above.
