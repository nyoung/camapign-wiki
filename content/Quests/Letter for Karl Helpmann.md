# Letter for Karl Helpmann

## Objective
Deliver a sealed letter from [[Marretta Schongauer]] (encountered at the [[Fist of Amber]]) to **[[Karl Helpmann]]**, a warehouse operator in [[Ferlangen]], with emphasis on speed.

## Background
At the [[Fist of Amber]] in [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]], [[Marretta Schongauer]] presented herself to [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] as the **headmistress of the local peddlars' guild** and hired him on the spot to carry a sealed letter to **[[Karl Helpmann]]** in [[Ferlangen]]. She paid **5 silver** in advance and stressed the need for speed.

The innkeeper at the [[Fist of Amber]] later confided to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] that Schongauer is in fact a **human trafficker**, not a guildmistress — though the rest of the party still believes her cover.

## Known Information
- The letter bears a **distinctive wax seal**
- **[[Karl Helpmann]]**'s name is printed clearly on the front
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] examined it and confirmed that the seal would make tampering obvious
- Helpmann operates a warehouse in [[Ferlangen]]
- Two refugee Ostlanders accompanying the party from the [[Fist of Amber]] parted ways at a warehouse near the city gate — possibly the same establishment

- [x] Travel to [[Ferlangen]] without breaking the seal
- [x] Locate [[Karl Helpmann]]'s shop in [[Ferlangen]] (turned out to be **[[Helpmann's General Goods]]** — a peddlar's storefront, not a warehouse)
- [x] Deliver the letter ([[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]])

- If Schongauer's true trade matters to its content, the letter may be coordinating a trafficking shipment
- Only [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] knows what the [[Fist of Amber]] innkeeper said about Schongauer

## Resolution
Letter delivered in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]. Helpmann opened it with a letter opener he immediately tried to sell, and the contents created modest work for him (he mentioned **labourer opportunities heading toward [[Averland]]** — consistent with a serf-relocation pipeline). He bought sundries from the party at fractional prices (≈1 gold crown each); [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] picked up Averland sun-livery armour as part of the shop's stock; [[Hildegarde]] showed him a counterfeit penny (he had not seen others); [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] probed about [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] (Helpmann showed no recognition of the name).
