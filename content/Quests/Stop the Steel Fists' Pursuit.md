# Stop the Steel Fists' Pursuit

## Summary
[[Anselm Rathsky]], leader of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]], will smuggle someone out of [[Wolfenburg]] if the party can do something to stop the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]'s relentless hunt for his people.

## Quest Giver
[[Anselm Rathsky]], at [[The Pig and Tack]]. Contact runs through the tavern's dwarf bartender.

## Objectives
- [ ] Find a way to break, blunt, or end the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring]]'s pursuit of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]
- [ ] Collect on Rathsky's offer: passage out of the city for one person

## Background
The gang war has a specific cause — but the party has **two incompatible accounts of it** from [[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]].

- **[[Anselm Rathsky|Rathsky]]'s version:** the Lurkers didn't simply steal the Prince's lottery takings — they **stole them from the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fists]], who had already stolen them**. The heist of the heist.
- **[[Captain Crais]]'s version:** the Steel Fists **ran** the illegal lottery themselves, and the Prince recently cracked down on it.

If Crais is right, the Lurkers robbed a criminal business rather than reclaiming stolen public money, and Rathsky sold the party a considerably more flattering story than the facts support. Either way the Steel Fists are now hunting every Lurker they can find, and Rathsky's organisation is bleeding.

The Steel Fists' main territory is the **southwest tenements along the city wall**, per [[Reiner Kolber]], who warns that even priestly garb earns little trust there after dark. They identify their own by a **flat cap in a distinctive red-orange**; [[Hildegarde]] has bought a pale cap to dye to match, either to draw them out or as an opener.

## Progress
**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** Offer made and accepted in principle. No action taken against the Steel Fists yet.

## Complications
- The party has an unrelated hard deadline the same day — [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] flees by barge at **sundown**
- Barges caught in the gang war's crossfire have burned downriver ([[Fiedler]]), so river escape is dangerous for anyone, including whoever Rathsky would smuggle out
- **The party is taking this job on the word of the man who started the war**, and his account is already contradicted by the City Watch. Verifying it before acting would cost them a morning they may not have
- Taking a side risks making an enemy of a second criminal organisation in a city where the party has no standing army
- The Lurkers are tied by tattoo to the Shallyan splinter sect the party is already investigating — helping them may entangle the party in that thread

## Rewards
- Passage out of [[Wolfenburg]] for one person, arranged by the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]
