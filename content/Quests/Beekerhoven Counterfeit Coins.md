# Beekerhoven Counterfeit Coins

## Objective
Trace the **counterfeit coins from [[Beekerhoven]]** that have been circulating along the party's route, and identify who is moving them through eastern [[Ostland]].

## Background
The party has been encountering distinctive **freshly minted coins** along [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s trail since [[Beekerhoven]] — both legitimate-looking gold crowns and false pennies. In [[Session 19 - Ferlangen|Session 19]], [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] took the [[Fist of Amber]] innkeeper aside and warned him about the false pennies, showing him an example.

On arrival in [[Ferlangen]], the [[Abbess of Verena (Ferlangen)|Abbess]] of the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] explicitly recommended approaching the **Merchants Guild** as the right avenue for an organised investigation, noting that several senior guild members are devoted patrons of the temple.

## Known Information
- The counterfeits originate from [[Beekerhoven]] (Elson's mint and the Medical Union network exposed in [[Session 11 - Rolf's Rampage and the Writ of Arrest|Session 11]])
- Both **freshly minted gold crowns** and **false pennies** are in circulation
- [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]'s group has been paying with freshly minted crowns — visible all along the eastern Ostland route
- The Ferlangen **Merchants Guild** is the recommended channel for an organised approach
- Pilpoul is currently giving informal warnings to innkeepers as they travel
