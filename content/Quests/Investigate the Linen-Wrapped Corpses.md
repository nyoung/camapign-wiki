# Investigate the Linen-Wrapped Corpses

## Objective
Find who is moving **linen-wrapped, sewn-shut corpses** through [[Wolfenburg]]'s sewers, recover the **stolen four-tumbler dwarven sewer key** — and return the **borrowed key within 48 hours** under oath.

## Background
At the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg]], clan elder [[Borin Anvilhart]] told the party that **a four-tumbler dwarven sewer key had been stolen in broad daylight** from one of his sewer jacks, almost certainly by a city gang, and that a younger dwarf had seen **linen-wrapped, sewn-shut bodies** being moved through the tunnels. He **loaned the party a key for 48 hours under oath of return** and assigned [[Snorri Belgrimson]] to guide them ([[Session 37 - Especially the Hugs|Session 37]]).

## Known Information
- **The northern route ends in the catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]** ([[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]]) — through **a haunted stretch of lower catacombs** and a **short iron gate** into the sewers
- **The gate is not a dwarven lock** — it belongs to *"the people who control the catacombs"* — and **the dwarven security gate on the far side has been wrenched out**, hinges torn apart. The gate is **freshly oiled and heavily used**
- **Two linen-wrapped corpses** lay in a side tunnel, **one over 70 and one middle-aged**, **well-cared-for with good teeth**, **stripped of clothes and jewellery**, **dead more than a week and less than two months**, **cause of death unknown** without unwrapping
- **A note under four old Ostland silver coins**: *"Lurkers … more bodies by Festag. — A.B."* The party suspects [[Azmus Beham]]
- **Tracks:** a **barefoot adult human** has been **dragging bodies uphill** — footprints go down empty and come back up dragging
- **The trail leads to [[The Ghoul Cavern]]**, where more wrapped corpses and **bones** lie, and a **ghoul colony** including an **elder ghoul** ([[Ghoul]]) attacked
- **[[Session 39 - Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently|Session 39]]:** the cavern is a **dead end** where the bodies are **fed to ghouls**: a nest of old bones, burial wrappings, **slips of paper**, **40 shillings** of coin that came with the bodies, and a body being eaten from the ribcage. Lore (Medicine): **no common cause** among the dead. **Two dead [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]** lay there with a **[[Forged Dwarf Key|forged copy of the dwarves' key]]**
- **[[Snorri Belgrimson]]:** no bodies had been seen for *"several weeks"*; the dwarves **rerouted a mountain stream** through the main channel; the side tunnels are a **makeshift collector**

## [[Session 41 - Too Warm for Me|Session 41]]
- **The stranded spirits are laid to rest.** [[Ewalt Wrangel]] used a spirit board, found them **detached from their bodies**, and performed the **last rites** (about twenty minutes). First name: **Melchior Pellen**, **less than three months** in the ground; his **niche was empty**. All questioned spirits were interred **within three years**, under [[Azmus Beham]]
- **The second note.** At the temple gate: **"You've made this town too warm for me… — A.B."**, **in the same hand** (per Wrangel) as the note under the silver coins. With it, a **silver amethyst ring** and a **certificate from the Vermillion Pawn** in Middenheim — see [[Silver Amethyst Ring]]
- **Wrangel's theory:** **necromancy or something deeper** cut the spirits loose; he **needs his library**. Ghouls, he says, are *not* a necromancer's work
- **Still open:** who is **"A.B."** and where has he gone; **who dragged the bodies uphill** and where they came from; **who is meant to collect the ring**
