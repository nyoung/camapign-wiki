# Investigate the Black Cloak Lurkers Below

## Summary
Run down the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]] — the sewer-and-catacomb thief gang whose tribal tattoos match those [[Hildegarde]] saw on a Shallyan novitiate — and find out what ties them to the Shallyan splinter sect, and now to the missing [[Gerd Breytenbach]].

## Quest Giver
Self-directed, following the tattoo identification made by a Myrmidian priest in [[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]].

## Objectives
- [x] Identify the gang behind the tattoos
- [x] Find and speak with [[Anselm Rathsky]] at [[The Pig and Tack]]
- [ ] Establish what the Lurkers' connection to the Shallyan splinter sect actually is
- [ ] Find out what [[Gerd Breytenbach]] was doing for them, and whether Rathsky knows where he is

## Background
A Myrmidian priest identified the tribal tattoos Hildegarde had seen on a Shallyan novitiate ([[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]) as belonging to the Black Cloak Lurkers Below, and connected the strange androgynous Shallya icon at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] to a secretive splinter sect. Minutes later the party watched three tattooed Lurkers chase [[Deke Kudgel Brand|Deke "Kudgel" Brand]] through an alley over stolen lottery money, and got the name of their frontman.

## Progress
**[[Session 34 - I Believe You Dropped Your Hat|Session 34]]:** Gang identified by tattoo; three members encountered and talked down; [[Anselm Rathsky]] named as their frontman, findable at [[The Pig and Tack]].

**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** Two major advances.
- **[[Madame Reitenbach]] revealed that [[Gerd Breytenbach]] had mentioned "helping out" the Lurkers** about a week before his disappearance — tying the party's central missing-person lead directly to this gang
- The party found Rathsky at [[The Pig and Tack]] and learned he is not a frontman but the **gang's leader**. He explained the gang war (see [[Stop the Steel Fists' Pursuit]]) and opened a standing line of contact through the tavern's bartender

## Complications
- The party spoke with Rathsky at length **without either side raising Gerd Breytenbach's name** — the connection was only established afterwards, via his mother
- The Lurkers' tattoo link to the Shallyan splinter sect remains unexplained; Rathsky volunteered nothing about it
- Rathsky wants something from the party first (see [[Stop the Steel Fists' Pursuit]])

## Rewards
- Potential line on [[Gerd Breytenbach]]'s whereabouts
- Potential inroad to the Shallyan splinter sect and the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] thread
