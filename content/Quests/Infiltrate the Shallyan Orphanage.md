# Infiltrate the Shallyan Orphanage

## Summary
Get inside the [[Shallyan Orphanage]] — the fortress-like charity hospital tied to both the missing-children thread and the eastern-gate disease pattern — by invitation, since it cannot be entered by force or stealth.

## Quest Giver
Self-directed. [[Reiner Kolber]] independently warned the party away from the orphanage in [[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]], saying children have been taken there and never seen again.

## Objectives
- [x] Scout the building for a way in
- [x] Get inside — [[Hildegarde]] and [[Ganav]] toured the ground floor as a couple wishing to adopt ([[Session 44 - Not Today, Unfortunately|Session 44]]); the adoption was refused
- [ ] Get back in for a proper look: the stair beside the infirmary, the locked door under the main stair
- [ ] Establish what happens to the children who don't come back out
- [ ] Follow up the eastern-gate disease patients reportedly sent here

## Background
Named by clerk [[Danel Koberger]] in [[Session 31 - Surgical Theater|Session 31]] as where the eastern-gate disease cases are sent, and independently flagged as dangerous by [[Reiner Kolber]] in [[Session 32 - A Tooth For a Tooth|Session 32]]. [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]] personally invited [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] to visit — an invitation the party has not yet used.

## Progress
**[[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]:** Scouted from the outside for the first time. The building is effectively a fortress: three storeys of solid granite, every window barred, ringed by an eight-foot spiked wrought-iron fence anchored directly into the stone road. [[Hildegarde]] walked the perimeter and found **no gaps and no climbable sections**. The party concluded that entry must be by invitation and settled on Hildegarde's cover — a woman of a certain age, childless, with motherly instincts, seeking to volunteer or adopt. Ganav proposed it; Hildegarde conceded with exasperation that it wasn't a bad plan.

## Complications
- No forced or stealthy entry is available — the cover story is the only route in
- [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]]'s standing invitation to Berin is a second, unused way in that may be faster
- Reiner Kolber's warning suggests whatever is inside is willing to make children disappear

## Rewards
- Access to the missing-children thread and the eastern-gate disease pattern, both feeding [[Find Professor Stradovski]]

## Notes
- Formally *The Hospital for the Maintenance and Education of Exposed and Deserted Children*
