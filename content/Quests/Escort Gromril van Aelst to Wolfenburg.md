# Escort Gromril van Aelst to Wolfenburg

## Objective
Escort the gnome merchant **[[Gromril van Aelst]]** and his coach safely from [[Ferlangen]] to **[[Wolfenburg]]**, along the road currently plagued by the **[[Howling Ones]]**.

## Background
In [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]], the party stumbled out of [[Sam Bugman's Brewery]] in time to witness an ambush on **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**. Local merchants — angered by the gnome's success in collecting **promissory notes** they had hoped to repudiate — hired thugs to intercept him in an alley while a thief lured his bodyguard ([[Mangold]]) away. The party intervened and beat the ambushers down. Gromril, in gratitude and out of evident need, offered the party a job: he had been **unable to find anyone willing to escort his coach to [[Wolfenburg]]**, the road being so dangerous of late.

[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] sealed the deal for **10 GC** before anyone else could draw breath. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] introduced himself formally as a **[[Knights of the Blazing Sun|Knight of the Blazing Sun]]** and arranged the rendezvous at the **south gate, dawn the following morning** — **7 Jahrdrung 2512**.

## Known Information
- Reward: **10 GC**, sealed by handshake
- Departure: **dawn, 7 Jahrdrung 2512**, **outside the south gate** of [[Ferlangen]]
- Coach driver: **[[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]]** (halfling, mentioned only)
- Bodyguard: **[[Mangold]]** (dwarf slayer; will leave his charge to chase a thief on instinct)
- Gromril carries his **real value in a hidden case under his shirt**, not in his belt-purse
- Gromril is freshly **a target of [[Ferlangen]] merchants** he has dunned for debts
- The road to [[Wolfenburg]] is the named theatre of operation for the **[[Howling Ones]]** — both a highwayman threat and a cult bounty (10/- per head)

## Complications
- The **[[Howling Ones]]** are active on this exact stretch of road — escort jobs through here have been hard to fill for a reason
- [[Mangold]]'s reflex to chase thieves can leave the party without a heavy hitter at the wrong moment
- [[Anton Kristov]] is a small-time import-exporter on this same road — encountering his operation is plausible
- The party have their own reasons to be on this road (the [[Find Professor Stradovski]] trail, the [[Matron Isolde Uricht]] meeting, the [[Wolfenburg]] disease cluster), so the job aligns rather than competes with their main objectives

## Notes
- The escort job is the most lucrative side-job the party have taken since [[Beekerhoven]]
- It also functionally **makes the party's planned departure for [[Wolfenburg]] paid work** — a happy alignment
- Gromril's promissory notes are the real target — the Bear Claws street attack in [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]] and the Howling Ones road attacks are almost certainly connected through a merchant who hired both
- The escort goal is now **directly entangled with the [[The Howling Ones Bounty|Howling Ones bounty]]**: the cult harassing the caravan and the bounty target are the same band, led by [[Astrid Hofhaimer]]. The party are no longer just defending the coach — they are turning to **hunt the hunters**.
