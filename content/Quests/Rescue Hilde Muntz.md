# Rescue Hilde Muntz

## Objective
Get **[[Hilde Muntz]]** safely out of the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] in [[Wolfenburg]] and out of the city, without breaching the temple's sanctuary and without either gang taking her.

## Background
Asked of the party by **[[Christina Asper]]** at the **Altar of Solkan** in the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] at twilight on 16 Jahrdrung 2512. After a long pause, Asper admitted she needed help with a **deeply personal matter** — the first time in the campaign she has asked the party for anything for herself.

[[Hilde Muntz]] is a young woman, a **former member of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** who has since sought a calling with the Shallyans, and **Asper's close friend**. She is sheltering inside the Temple of Shallya and cannot leave: **both the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] and the Lurkers are hunting her** over the stolen lottery purse.

## Known Information
- **Neither gang will violate the temple's sanctuary.** Per [[Lucas Pollack]], it is too important a source of healing and charity for their own people to be worth breaching
- **But neither will let her walk free**, and **all exits are being watched**
- **[[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]] are themselves watched** whenever they move through the city, because the gangs suspect their connection to Hilde — which limits how directly either can act
- The stolen money at the root of it was **the Steel Fist's illegal lottery take**, and **the Lurkers took it** — the deception [[Anselm Rathsky]] sold the party in [[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]]
- The party has **not yet met Hilde**
