# Session 34 — I Believe You Dropped Your Hat

## Summary
With the hunt for [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] gone cold, the party regrouped in **[[Wolfenburg]]** and resolved to find out who in the city was sheltering him. At **[[Fazzini House]]**, a servant introducing himself as **Anselm** confirmed **[[Jaie Schraeder]]**'s death and — muddying the trail the party opened in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]] — claimed **[[Gerd Breytenbach]]** had also died under suspicious circumstances at the Schraeder household, before pointing them toward Gerd's elderly mother in the city's ethnic quarter. At the **[[Merchant's Office (Riverfront)]]**, a bribed clerk confirmed two Ferlangen shipments handled by the **[[Hofbauer Bodelstein]]** company included goods from **[[Klaus von Kolditz|Von Kolditz]]** — and whispered that Von Kolditz had been leaking caravan movements to the **[[Howling Ones]]**, keeping his own shipments safe while his rivals were plundered. Research at the **[[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]]** turned up little beyond the catacombs predating the city, but Vespers at the **[[Temple of Myrmidia (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Myrmidia]]** paid off: a priest identified tribal tattoos Hildegarde had seen on a Shallyan novitiate as belonging to the **[[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]**, a thief gang working the sewers and catacombs. That connection turned real minutes later, when a fleeing man in a red hat — **[[Deke Kudgel Brand|Deke "Kudgel" Brand]]** — burst out of a side alley chased by three cloaked, tattooed men. Hildegarde dove under a hay cart after him and disarmed the tension by returning his dropped hat; Boris and Berin talked down the pursuers, who let slip they were hunting stolen lottery money and named their frontman, **[[Anselm Rathsky]]**, before fleeing — flashing the same tattoos as the Lurkers. The party pressed on into the ethnic district and, trading on Gromril van Aelst's name, found their way to Gerd's mother, **[[Madame Reitenbach]]**, who — devastated by news of her son's death — invited them in for tea.

## Events
- War council in Wolfenburg: the party refocused on finding who is sheltering [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] within the city, and where he's gone to ground. Berin shared his own goal of reconsecrating a desecrated section of the catacombs beneath the city as part of his growing commitment to Myrmidia
- Visited **[[Fazzini House]]**; a servant giving his name as **Anselm** confirmed Jaie Schraeder's death and, pressed further, claimed Gerd Breytenbach had also died at the Schraeder household — contradicting the party's own [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]] conclusion that Breytenbach is alive. He clarified Gerd was a local hire and manservant, not privy to Schraeder's business, and pointed the party to Gerd's elderly mother in the city's ethnic neighbourhoods
- Reached the **[[Merchant's Office (Riverfront)]]** before closing; Berin posed as a Wissenland merchant house representative investigating missing medical supplies and amber tied to [[Marretta Schongauer]]. A clerk confirmed two recent Ferlangen shipments via the [[Hofbauer Bodelstein]] company included goods from **[[Klaus von Kolditz|Von Kolditz]]**; a bribe of silver shillings from Boris loosened his tongue further — Von Kolditz has been leaking caravan movements to the [[Howling Ones]] so his own shipments arrive untouched while rivals' cargo is plundered. Stolen goods reportedly move on through forged papers via boat captains bound for Nuln or Altdorf
- Researched the catacombs at the **[[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]]**; an over-enthusiastic librarian buried the table in architectural histories instead. Learned the catacombs predate much of the city and the sewers used advanced dwarven engineering. Hildegarde tried to draw Shallya gossip out of the Verenan clergy and got cold indifference
- Attended Vespers at the **[[Temple of Myrmidia (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Myrmidia]]**; Berin introduced the party as pilgrims under the goddess's protection, earning loaned Myrmidian robes and vestments. A Myrmidian priest identified the tribal tattoos Hildegarde had seen on a Shallyan novitiate (see [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]]) as belonging to the **[[Black Cloak Lurkers Below]]** — a thief gang working the sewers and catacombs — and placed the strange androgynous Shallya icon at the Temple of the All Gods with a secretive splinter sect
- Left alms at the temple with the door ward for a street urchin, **Kulber**, who wants to meet at the plaza statue in the morning — likely [[Reiner Kolber]]
- On the way south, a chase erupted: a man in a red hat sprinted from a side alley under crossbow fire, chased by three black-cloaked men (crossbow, stiletto, dagger). He dove under a hay cart to escape; **Hildegarde** followed through the muck in her brand-new borrowed robes and came up under the point of his sword. She picked up his fallen hat, handed it back, and introduced herself — he lowered the blade, gave his name as **Deke, "Kudgel Brand,"** and declined protection, heading off toward the **Steel Ring** on his own
- On the other side of the cart, **Boris and Berin** talked down the three pursuers once they realized the party wasn't the City Watch. One let slip they were after stolen lottery money before trying to walk it back; Berin claimed to represent an organization with missing goods and name-dropped Von Kolditz, which put them at ease. They named their frontman, **[[Anselm Rathsky]]**, findable at **[[The Pig and Tack]]**, and slipped away — but not before the party caught the same tribal tattoos on one man's arm as they'd seen on the Shallyan novitiate, tying the Lurkers directly to the splinter cult
- Continued south into the ethnic district; a local dwarf, swayed by the names [[Gromril van Aelst]] and [[Mangold]], pointed the party to a leaning tenement as the likely home of a woman living alone — and mentioned, almost in passing, that van Aelst has an estate by the river on the east wall
- Talked past the tenement's landlord with a cover story about a Temple of Mórr bequest; found **Madame Reitenbach**'s door on the second level. Hildegarde disarmed and approached alone; delivered news of Gerd's death gently. Grief-stricken, Madame Reitenbach invited the party in anyway, setting water to boil for tea
