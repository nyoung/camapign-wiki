# Session 30 — Hanging Judge

**Played:** 11 July 2026
**In-game date:** 13 Jahrdrung 2512 (evening) – 14 Jahrdrung 2512 (afternoon)

## Summary

The party opened by recapping their tangled leads in Wolfenburg — and reconfirmed, via the city's guest register, that **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]] is somewhere within the city walls**. Leaving the Temple of Shallya at day's end, Boris and Hildegarde caught a Shallyan initiate with tribal tattoos making **discreet hand signals at [[Christina Asper]]** — signs Boris recognized as thieves'-cant, not battle-signals. Asper flatly denied it when confronted. **[[Hildegarde]] shadowed her that night** and watched Asper meet the same initiate again in an alley between the Temple of Myrmidia and the Temple of Shallya, then disappear into the back of the **Witch Hunters Guild**. The party said nothing openly, but the suspicion is now fully seeded.

The next morning brought a midday hearing for a captured **[[Howling Ones]]** member — **[[Ganav]]**. Before that, the party called on **Dr. von Bora** at the wealthy **Amman Estate**, securing his tentative goodwill and his agreement to attend the hearing. In court, **Magistrate Gawinus**, Wolfenburg's "hanging judge," heard Ganav's remarkable, unexpectedly moving defense — capped by an on-the-spot marriage proposal to Hildegarde — and sentenced him to hang anyway. **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] invoked a rare legal statute**, paid three gold crowns, and took personal custody of Ganav, saving him from the gallows. Hildegarde greeted her newly free almost-fiancé with a punch to the jaw.

The party then cashed in the **bounty on the Howling Ones' heads** (five gold crowns) with the city Watch, handed over the bandits' coded notes and the werewolf's teeth as evidence, and were granted official **consultancy status** and free passage through the gates. The day closed on a strange note: a wild-eyed madman burst from a side street, seized [[Ganav]], and hissed a warning about an "**influencing machine**" and a "**world plan**" before fleeing toward the waterfront — leaving the party with more questions than answers as they turned toward the Physicians Guild.

## Events

### Arrival in Wolfenburg Recap
- The party recaps their leads: rumors of a deserting prince, an imprisoned witch hunter cell, disturbed corpses beneath the Church/Temple of the All Gods, and [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s murder
- The city register reconfirms **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** is somewhere within Wolfenburg's walls
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** mentions he's arranged street urchins to search the city for her

### Departure from the Temple of Shallya
- Boris and Hildegarde notice a tattooed Shallyan initiate making discreet hand gestures at **[[Christina Asper]]**
- Asper acknowledges the signal and follows the group out; when confronted directly, she flatly denies it happened
- Boris identifies the gestures as thieves'/smugglers' cant, not military signals

### Shadowing the Witch Hunter
- **[[Hildegarde]]** discreetly shadows Asper that night
- Asper meets the tattooed initiate again in an alley between the Temple of Myrmidia and the Temple of Shallya for an animated, gestural conversation
- Asper is tracked to the back entrance of the **Witch Hunters Guild**, where she disappears inside

### A Night of Tales and Toasts
- The party lodges at a well-regarded inn on the south side of the city, recommended by Asper
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]**, deep in his cups, mourns the road behind them and loudly regales the tavern with an exaggerated account of the Howling Ones fight (boasting of thirty heads, not twenty)
- Financial pressure mounts — the party is nearly out of coin pending the bounty payout

### Negotiations at the Amman Estate
- The party calls on **Dr. von Bora** at the wealthy Amman Estate in the Estates District
- He explains his research into old religious beliefs and his planned expedition to the Kislev–Ostland border
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** switches to Classical to warn him privately about [[Christina Asper]]'s zealotry, and to advocate for **[[Ganav]]** as a skilled healer and potential guide
- Dr. von Bora agrees to attend Ganav's hearing and lend what influence he can

### The Trial of Ganav
- **Magistrate Gawinus**, known as the "hanging judge," presides over Ganav's hearing
- **[[Ganav]]** delivers an unexpectedly eloquent defense, arguing he was a healer forced into service, never a raider — and pivots into a genuine on-the-spot marriage proposal to **[[Hildegarde]]**
- Gawinus is unmoved by the speech and sentences Ganav to death by hanging
- **[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]** invokes a rarely used statute to take personal legal custody of a condemned prisoner, paying **three gold crowns** bail; Ganav walks free
- **[[Hildegarde]]** punches Ganav in the jaw the moment he's outside, then sets terms: five children, minimum, if he's serious about marrying her

### The Bounty of the Howling Ones
- The Watch Captain personally verifies the ~20 severed heads at Mungo's wagon outside the North Gate
- The paymaster counts out **five gold crowns**
- The party hands over the bandits' coded notes (evidence of a Ferlangen informant) and the werewolf's teeth taken from Astrid's beast form
- The Watch Captain confirms Wolfenburg has dealt with werewolves for centuries (the Wolf's Bane at the gates is no decoration) and grants the party **official consultancy status**, with free passage through the city gates

### The Madman's Warning
- A wild-eyed stranger bursts from a side street, grabs **[[Ganav]]** by the lapels, and warns him he is "under the influence of the influencing machine" and that "the world plan has come for you," then flees toward the waterfront with unnatural speed
- Ganav gives chase but loses him amid the docks
- The party turns toward the Physicians Guild to investigate [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s murder and seek care for their unhealed wounds
