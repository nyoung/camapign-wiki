# Session 43 — A Mighty Gift

## Summary
The party left the bounty hall of the [[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]] with [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] and, with [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] rejoining them on the way, chose the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] over the [[Physicians' Guild (Wolfenburg)|Physicians' Guild]]. [[Nina Trielffen]] met them in the courtyard; [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] heard Marla vouch for them, and then personally washed [[Ganav]]'s ruined arm with water and lily petals. The miracle of **[[Shallya's Tears]]** took hold: Ganav's bones, elbow and muscle were restored and the blessing spread to heal the whole party, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s infection included. *"You have received a mighty gift today; you need to earn this with your future actions."*

Nina then led the party to the man they had been seeking since Session 31: **[[Gerd Breytenbach]]**, alive, bandaged and hiding in an isolation room. Led by [[Hildegarde]], the party heard how [[Professor Udo Stradovski]] stayed at [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s townhouse waiting for a messenger from the manor at **[[Levudaldorf]]**; how [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]], a doctor attached to the orphanage, treated Gerd's withered arm with a concoction, a cut and a balm until it was whole and monstrous; how Gerd fled when rough men entered the house and [[Louisa May|Louisa]] screamed; and what Stradovski was like in the flesh. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and Ganav examined the arm and read the signs of a flesh-eating disease, a fungal fever, and the touch of the Unclean One. Berin warned Nina in secret and blessed her conscience.

## Events

### Choosing Shallya
- The ring and the provenance document from the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] bait were recorded as being in [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s keeping. The ring is the **Ring of Quiet**, also called **Zoltar's Ring** (see [[Silver Amethyst Ring]]); its provenance document was produced by the **Vermillion Pawn**, a business in Middenheim
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] had left [[Hilde Muntz]] with [[Christina Asper]] at the burial pits, come back through the gates and crossed the plaza by the [[Temple of Myrmidia (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Myrmidia]] when he met his battered friends; [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] waved
- The party weighed the **Physicians' Guild** (private practice; a chance to question one of the three doctors) against the **Temple of Shallya** (an overcrowded "emergency room", but Marla had to go there anyway, as did the lead on [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]]). They chose Shallya

### Gammer Isolde's mercy
- [[Nina Trielffen]] hugged Marla in the courtyard, then took the group to [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]]. Marla vouched for them: *"They didn't kill anyone, they used minimum force."* Hildegarde gave Nina the sign [[Hilde Muntz]] had taught her; Gammer, unsurprised, asked for the procedure room
- A condescending young initiate (**[[Hermann Roeging]]**) objected; Gammer glared him quiet. She cut away Ganav's sleeve with silver shears, poured spring water into a brass basin, scattered lily petals on it and washed the wound while praying, tears falling on her hands
- On the GM's roll (a **99**), **Shallya's Tears** worked: skin closed, shattered bones slid into place, the elbow reformed and the muscle and tendon regrew; the glow spread and healed **Boris** (his cut and its infection) and **Hildegarde** as well. Gammer: *"For how you acted today, Shallya has shined upon you."* The party must **earn** the gift by acting in Shallya's grace
- Gammer told them the orphanage takes in unwanted children from outlying villages, many with village diseases; the doctors there **quarantine them for some weeks**; the party is free to speak to anyone there. She had never heard of the **Medical Union**

### Nina leads the way
- Marla was grounded until morning, but not before Boris spoke up for her courage
- Nina led the party through the temple's corridors, checking for followers. She does **not** trust the initiate **Hermann Roeging**: *"I think he isn't just working for the Shallyans"*
- She brought them to a private isolation room, where [[Gerd Breytenbach]] lay bandaged in a hospital gown, his face wrapped in a removable mask of linen

### Gerd's story
- **[[Professor Udo Stradovski]]** had stayed at [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s house for several days, awaiting someone from outside. A **servant from the manor at [[Levudaldorf]]** arrived to take them; Stradovski had Gerd quietly summon Schraeder from the Physicians' Guild, and the three left for Levudaldorf **the next day, about six days ago**
- Gerd returned from household errands to find **rough men in the house**, heard **Louisa** scream and be cut off, and fled. Later the house burned. He suspects the killers were the same people Stradovski worked with, and that they wanted to remove every sign of where the Professor had stayed
- **[[Dr Hartmut Imhot]]**, who works with the orphanage, visited with a case of clinking jars and spent hours behind closed doors with Stradovski and Schraeder. Gerd's left arm had been **withered since a dog bit him as a boy**, some 25 years ago. Imhot and Stradovski poked his fingers with a concoction, cut his upper arm and applied a balm. He passed out for about a day and a half and woke with a **burlap bag of maggots** over the arm. The arm is now **whole, scarred, veined and half again larger than the other**, and strong
- Since then he has **nightmares of flies, maggots and rot**
- **Stradovski:** looks **20–25 years older** than the painting; hair receded and cut short, beard grey; certainly the same man. **Smug**, a man who seemed to know exactly what would happen and to have planned for it; looked down on everyone; stayed sequestered in his quarters. Gerd's arm was used as a **test** to verify the "product" worked. Schraeder acted like a nervous student and Imhot like a man trying to please him
- **Schraeder** was **copying Stradovski's documents symbol by symbol**, in a hurry, and was angry when Gerd walked in; Gerd thinks this may be why he was killed. He identified the notes the party carried as Schraeder's handwriting, with a poor command of the Classical script
- Schraeder worshipped at the **Sigmarite cathedral** and visited the Temple of the All Gods; he belonged to the **Order of Occidental Fellows**, which meets every two weeks at the tavern **The Occidental Gentleman**. A private courier was summoned for him once the Professor arrived; Gerd gave the courier firm's location (across the street from the Physicians' Guild)
- **[[Karel Vantz]]** was vying for power in the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]: he thought they weren't fighting the Steel Fists hard enough, while the main leader wanted to lie low and wait out the storm. Gerd had been treating wounded Lurkers when his household was taken
- **Louisa** was about **seven months pregnant** by Schraeder when she died, and, Gerd believes, was never treated. The baby is presumed to have died with her
- Gerd gave **seven crowns** for his mother ([[Madame Reitenbach]]), asking that she be told only that they came from a benefactor; he handed them to **Berin** rather than Boris

### The examination
- **Berin (Theology, challenging):** the nightmares are classic imagery of rot, flies, maggots and death. Berin is convinced Gerd has been **touched by the Unclean One** (Nurgle)
- **Berin (Medicine):** the fingertip depressions match a **flesh-eating disease**; the lightning-bolt scars on the upper arm match a **fungal-spore infection** that causes a deadly fever
- **Ganav (Heal, difficult):** Gerd had been in excellent health all his life, other than the arm, until the treatment
- Hildegarde asked whether any of the men in the house had bright orange eyes; they did not. She showed Gerd the portrait of Stradovski

### A warning for Nina
- Berin prayed for a **Blessing of Conscience** on Nina (a Fortune point spent) and told her, in confidence, that Gerd bore a Nurgle's taint; he feared a servant of the **Fly Lord** had reached the temple through him and urged her to take it to Gammer. Nina, who said Gerd had been a friend to many thieves and had been tending wounded Lurkers, steeled herself to do so

### Wrapping the day
- It was about **half past four**. The party agreed to repair gear and decide whether to pursue the orphanage; Ganav suggested hiring someone for the road
- Hildegarde looked in on Marla and gave her the **dagger** taken from the Lurkers, telling her to follow orders in danger and to use the dagger only as a last resort
