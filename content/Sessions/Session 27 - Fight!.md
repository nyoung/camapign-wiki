# Session 27 — Fight!

**Played:** 20 June 2026
**In-game date:** 10 Jahrdrung 2512 (noon)

## Summary

The decoy plan fell apart almost immediately. [[Ganav]] and [[Hildegarde]]'s satchel of worthless papers — tied around the neck of the gagged prisoner and sent shuffling toward the parlay point at midday — was exposed within moments by a literate [[Howling Ones]] bandit named [[Dickus]], who rifled through the contents, announced the fake, and drew his comrades' bows. [[Ganav]]'s shouted bluff (that Dickus had been planning to steal the notes himself) landed on deaf ears.

What followed was a grinding, chaotic road skirmish through muddy terrain. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] charged [[Orazio]] across a stream toward archers in the tree line, shouting to [[Myrmidia]]; the horse struggled at the water but pressed on. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], retreating to the wagon for archery, put an arrow through [[Dickus]]'s arm and later shredded a bandit's jerkin so thoroughly [[Ganav]] remarked it could no longer be sold. [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] proved the day's most destructive force — crushing one bandit's arm outright and later charging across the stream to cut down two more in quick succession. [[Lucas Pollack]] fumbled his attack twice in a row and then executed a bandit who tried to surrender, making his absence of mercy plain. [[Mangold]] charged toward the fight and went face-first into the mud. [[Christina Asper|Asper]], jaw still wired, held her fire rather than risk allies in the crush.

Then [[Astrid Hofhaimer|the wolf]] arrived — not fully healed from [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s arrow in [[Session 25 - Asper and the Wolf|Session 25]], but functional enough. It lunged for [[Herman Wurt|Herman]] directly, and before it reached him it let out a howl that emptied the battlefield of horses and rattled every warrior present. Every animal fled — every one except [[Orazio]], who stood his ground alone. [[Herman Wurt|Herman]]'s horse [[Vortex]] had already been struck by a critical arrow and was stuck in the mud; Herman dismounted and faced the wolf on foot. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], battered but sharp, helped [[Hildegarde]] reload her crossbow (bolt tips dipped in ink), then let out a near-perfect mimicry of the bandits' own retreat signal — and confusion rippled through the nearest outlaws.

The session ended mid-battle, with the wolf engaged, [[Herman Wurt|Herman]] on foot, and the escape bridge the stated goal.

- [[Herman Wurt|Herman]] performed a rapid combat dismount; faced the wolf on foot
