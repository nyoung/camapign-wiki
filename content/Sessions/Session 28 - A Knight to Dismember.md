# Session 28 — A Knight to Dismember

**Played:** 27 June 2026
**In-game date:** 10 Jahrdrung 2512 (afternoon–evening) – 11 Jahrdrung 2512 (morning)

## Summary

The road skirmish from [[Session 27 - Fight!|Session 27]] continued in driving rain. [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] crushed the bandit line in the northeast; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] fought close with outlaws near the river while [[Orazio]] sensed something far worse than bandits in the northern woods. [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s horse bolted, and while [[Ganav]] and [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] fought to bring the wagon back under control, [[Herman Wurt]] tried to prove himself against the giant wolf alone — and was knocked face-first into the river.

[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] called on [[Myrmidia]] and spent a point of resolve to master his terror, spurring [[Orazio]] directly at the wolf. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] shifted his aim to the bandits at the treeline and took an arrow to the leg for it, fired straight back by a wounded outlaw. Then the wolf struck: it tore out **[[Herman Wurt]]'s throat**, killing him instantly — and as it stood over the body, it rose onto its hind legs and **transformed into a hybrid humanoid-wolf form**, plunging into a frenzy that savaged the party's horses.

[[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] permanently burned a point of resilience to land a blow that cleaved the werewolf's hand to the wrist; **[[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]]** finished it with a leg strike that shattered bone and dropped it, unresponsive, in the mud. The surviving bandits broke and fled. [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] mourned Herman and paid for his grave, which [[Ganav]] dug with Sigmarite rites spoken over it; [[Hildegarde]] took a fang from the fallen creature as a trophy. When the party looked back, the **werewolf's body had vanished** — only a blood trail and rapidly lengthening tracks leading into the forest, healing and outpacing them as they gave chase.

A coded scrap of paper found on the literate bandit near the parlay point turned out, once [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] deciphered it, to be a **scout's tally of the caravan's strength** — confirmation that someone had been feeding the [[Howling Ones]] information about them, likely from [[Ferlangen]]. The party spent an uneventful defensive night in a roadside cave and reached **[[Dassel]]** by sunset the next day, where [[Hildegarde]]'s werewolf fang and [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s armour convinced the skeptical locals that the road was clear — earning the party free drinks and a discount room after Hildegarde and Ganav's impromptu tavern performance.

## Events

### The Ambush Continues
- [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] crushes bandit resistance in the northeast; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] fights close-quarters near the river while [[Orazio]] senses something far worse than bandits from the northern woods
- [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s horse panics and bolts; [[Ganav]] and [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] work to bring the wagon back under control
- [[Herman Wurt]] attempts to fight the giant wolf alone and is knocked face-first into the shallow river

### The Wolf Transforms
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] spends a resolve point invoking [[Myrmidia]] and spurs [[Orazio]] directly at the wolf; barely lands a strike
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] shifts fire to bandits at the treeline; a wounded outlaw pulls Boris's own arrow from a companion and fires it back, striking Boris in the leg
- The wolf **tears out [[Herman Wurt]]'s throat**, killing him instantly
- The wolf's corpse rises onto its hind legs and transforms into a **hybrid humanoid-wolf form** — "form two of the boss," per [[Hildegarde]]
- The transformed werewolf enters a frenzy, launching hammer blows and savaging the party's horses, targeting [[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]]'s mount

### The Fall of the Beast
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] permanently burns a point of resilience to land a critical strike, cleaving the werewolf's hand to the wrist
- **[[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]]** delivers the killing blow, shattering the creature's leg and dropping it unresponsive in the mud
- Surviving bandits break and flee into the woods

### Aftermath of the Ambush
- [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] confirms the dead, mourns [[Herman Wurt]], and pays ten shillings for a grave
- [[Ganav]] digs the grave and speaks Sigmarite condolences over [[Herman Wurt]]'s body
- [[Hildegarde]] extracts a fang from the fallen werewolf as a trophy
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] searches the dead: battered blades, makeshift shields, ~60 arrows
- [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] reluctantly agrees to transport the ~20 severed bandit heads (for bounty), wrapped in canvas, for a cut of [[Hildegarde]]'s share
- The werewolf's body is found **gone** — only a blood trail and drag marks leading into the forest

### The Coded Message and the Vanishing Werewolf
- A search of the literate bandit near the parlay point turns up a coded scrap of paper
- **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]** deciphers it as a **scout's tally** of the caravan's horses, coaches, wagons, and men — confirmation of an informant feeding the [[Howling Ones]] intelligence, likely out of [[Ferlangen]]
- The party tracks the werewolf's blood trail into the forest; the trail thins, then vanishes, as the creature heals and outpaces them

### Shelter and the Road to Dassel
- The party fortifies a shallow **roadside cave** for the night; the watch passes without incident
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] hunts along the road the next day, supplementing dwindling supplies
- The caravan reaches **[[Dassel]]** at sunset

### Arrival in Dassel
- [[Ganav]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] announce the [[Howling Ones]] defeated and the road clear; skeptical farmers are convinced by [[Hildegarde]]'s werewolf fang and [[Sir Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]]'s armour
- The grateful locals buy the party a round of drinks
- [[Hildegarde]] and [[Ganav]] perform a song and dance at the inn, earning a discounted large room
