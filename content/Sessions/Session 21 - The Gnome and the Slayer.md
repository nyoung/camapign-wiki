# Session 21 — The Gnome and the Slayer

**Played:** 9 May 2026
**In-game date:** 6 Jahrdrung 2512

## Summary

The morning after her clandestine visit to the brothel, [[Hildegarde]] slipped back into [[Frau Busch's Boarding House]] in time for breakfast and shared what she had learned with the rest of the party: a list of missing merchants and women, and the grim confirmation that [[Ferlangen]]'s kidnappings happen not on the streets but out in the countryside, where raiders sweep through in the night. Several merchants had been named as involved in the trafficking, though the precise workings of the network remained shrouded in mystery. With the morning's intelligence in hand, the party set out to follow their strongest leads.

Their first stop was the **[[Physicians' Guild (Ferlangen)|Physicians' Guild]]**, a well-appointed building near the main plaza, its Guild Master's office lined floor-to-ceiling with medical texts in a dozen languages. [[Hildegarde]] presented the warrant for [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Professor Stradovski]] — upside down, as was her habit — while [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] spun an elaborate tale of the professor's miraculous youth-restoring cures to jog the Guild Master's memory. The administrator was cooperative but unhelpful, claiming no knowledge of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]], though he did mention that one new practitioner — a man calling himself **[[Derich Kölderer]]** — had registered with the guild roughly a month prior. He also noted that the road to [[Wolfenburg]] had grown treacherous of late, plagued by the **[[Howling Ones]]**, with a bounty on their heads for anyone brave enough to stop them. To maintain their cover, [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] fabricated a story about needing ointment for an embarrassing case of chafing, purchasing a healing poultice and leaving the guild with their dignity only partially intact.

Back on the street the party fell into a heated debate. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] connected the **letter signed only with "K"** sent from [[Ferlangen]] to the now-dismantled [[Medical Union]], suggesting a network of prepared contacts might be guiding [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s movements. [[Hildegarde]] theorised that [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] was using stolen gold to buy influence among high-society merchants in the trafficking trade. The group weighed rushing to [[Wolfenburg]] against spending more time in [[Ferlangen]] untangling the web — until a sandwich-board man strolled past announcing **[[Sam Bugman's Brewery]]** on the corner of First Strasse and Wallstrasse, and the matter was settled without another word.

The brewery was a cavernous, active establishment, three-quarters full of rough-looking mining dwarves bearing the evidence of their trade in crushed limbs and missing eyes. The waitress offered two choices: **Beardtangle** and **Bugman's Best**, both described with the quiet confidence of someone who had watched many a human make a terrible decision. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] ordered the strongest available, [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] ordered a round for the group, and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] — wisely — declined entirely after his request for wine was met with a blank stare. [[Hildegarde]] proudly produced her newly acquired **[[Bounty Hunter's Guild License]]** and passed it around the table, beaming, while [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] interrogated her about the woman she had visited the night before. The dwarven ale, however, proved to be a force unto itself: [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], whose constitution was simply not built for it, became thoroughly and catastrophically drunk, and before long he was on his feet belting out rowdy dwarven tavern songs to an audience of actual dwarves who were decidedly not amused. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] ushered the group out before fists could replace vegetables as the preferred form of audience feedback, pausing only to ask the barmaid for a hangover remedy on the way out. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] dealt with the aftermath by dunking his head into a water barrel outside, with [[Hildegarde]] gripping his collar to prevent him from drowning in three inches of water.

As the party stumbled back into the afternoon light, they caught sight of an unusual pair making their way down the opposite side of the street: a **dwarf slayer** with an orange mohawk, tattooed arms, and the bearing of someone who had never once considered backing down from anything, escorting a small figure in colourful clothing and a pointy red hat. Before anyone could remark on the oddity, a shadowy figure slipped from an alley, cut the small figure's purse clean from his belt, and bolted. The slayer gave chase without a word, leaving his charge entirely undefended — and within moments several hard-looking men materialised from the shadows, slamming fists into palms and grabbing boards from the rubbish as they herded the small figure toward a dead-end alley.

[[Hildegarde]] moved first, stepping into the street and calling for calm, but the thugs paid her no mind. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] ducked back into the brewery and shouted for help from the dwarves inside, only to be pelted with rotten vegetables for his trouble. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] drew his sling and whistled sharply, demanding the men stand down, while [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] — still magnificently drunk — charged after the slayer, singing. When diplomacy failed and the thugs closed in, the brawl that followed was as chaotic as the state of its participants. [[Hildegarde]] took blow after blow as two men double-teamed her in the alley, her attempts at intimidation undermined by the fact that she could barely lift her arm. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], meanwhile, launched into a nonsensical drunken ballad about goat-filled rooms, gnome cooks, and South Road trains, and somehow — through sheer verbal chaos — left one of the thugs standing completely dumbfounded and unable to act. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] sprinted across the street and charged in with the flat of his blade, while [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] drew a dagger from his boot and manoeuvred behind the nearest attacker, delivering a precise strike to the man's knee that dropped him to the cobblestones. One thug broke and ran; the rest were beaten into submission.

The slayer came charging back moments later, axe in one hand and hammer in the other, and very nearly took [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s head off before the small figure shouted him down. The figure introduced himself as **[[Gromril van Aelst]]**, a gnome merchant who had been in [[Ferlangen]] collecting promissory notes owed to him by local merchants — notes that those same merchants had apparently hired the thugs to steal back. The slayer, **[[Mangold]]**, returned the stolen purse, which he had recovered from the thief, and Gromril patted the hidden case tucked beneath his shirt with evident satisfaction. When [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] mentioned the names **Von Kolditz** and **Anton Christoph**, Gromril's expression sharpened: **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]**, he said, was an antiques dealer with rumoured ties to human trafficking, while **[[Anton Kristov]]** was a small-time import-exporter running goods — and apparently people — along the route from [[Ferlangen]] to [[Wolfenburg]] and into [[Kislev]].

Gromril explained that he was looking for passage to [[Wolfenburg]] and had been unable to find anyone willing to escort his coach through the dangerous roads. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], eyes lighting up at the mention of payment, extended his hand and sealed a deal for **ten gold crowns** before anyone else could draw breath. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] introduced himself formally as a **[[Knights of the Blazing Sun|Knight of the Blazing Sun]]** and arranged to meet Gromril and his driver, **[[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]]**, outside the city gates the following morning. As the gnome and his stoic guardian made their way back toward the inn outside town, the party was left to tend to their wounds and reflect on a day that had begun with a visit to a guild and ended with a drunken brawl, a new employer, and **two more names to add to their growing list of suspects**.
