# Session 32 — A Tooth For a Tooth

## Summary
Released by the City Watch, the party hired street-orphan informant [[Reiner Kolber]] to track down a raven-haired woman and her bodyguard — almost certainly **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** — to a riverfront boarding house, [[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House|Megrita's Anchor]]. While the boarding house was under watch, Hildegarde was ambushed near the Myrmidia compound by **[[Astrid Hofhaimer]]** herself, in full human form, who reclaimed a wolf's fang the party had been carrying by force and walked out the city gates unchallenged — passing a wolf's mane with no reaction. The next morning, the party examined the three bodies from the Schraeder townhouse fire at the [[Garden of Mórr]], confirming all three were killed before the fire was set, including [[Jaie Schraeder]] himself, felled by a single expert sword thrust.

## Events
- Released from City Watch Headquarters after Berin finished transcribing the charred Classical-language documents; the Watch Commander shared that the Howling Ones are internally divided (a violent northern faction vs. an eastern faction pursuing an unclear "revolution")
- [[Reiner Kolber]], a street-orphan informant running a network of "Wolfen boys," approached the party in the plaza and — for 4 silver shillings up front, 4 more on confirmation — led them to **[[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House]]**, where his boy Eyeball had been watching a raven-haired woman and her bodyguard come and go for a day
- Reiner warned the party to stay away from a local orphanage where children have been taken and never seen again (ties to the existing missing-children thread)
- A barge captain confirmed a "man built like a bear" had tried to book passage to Altdorf for two, offering four times the going rate — refused
- Ganav and Boris watched the boarding house and confirmed the raven-haired woman and her broad-shouldered companion went inside and didn't re-emerge — description matches **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** exactly (raven-haired, broad-shouldered/burly companion)
- Hildegarde struck out at the Bounty Hunter Guild — an old, vague wanted poster, nothing on Elfrieda or Stradovski
- Ambushed near the Myrmidia compound by **[[Astrid Hofhaimer]]**, fully human, who demanded back the wrapped wolf's fang the party carried. Hildegarde's pickpocket attempt failed against Astrid's inhuman reflexes; Astrid wrenched the fang free, pressed it into her own jaw, and it visibly regrew into a human canine, absorbed by living flesh in seconds. She said Hildegarde was "on my list to kill... but not today," then walked through the closing city gates — the guards dismissed Hildegarde's warnings, and Astrid showed no reaction passing a wolf's mane, remaining fully human throughout
- Party regrouped at the **[[Tree and Rut Inn]]**; a runner reported the raven-haired woman dined at the **Trout and Pike** before returning to Megrita's Anchor for the night. Hildegarde's wound treated overnight
- Next morning: visited the **[[Garden of Mórr]]**, met by cleric **[[Ewalt Wrangel]]**, and examined the three bodies from the Schraeder fire. Berin's findings: the female servant's throat was slashed (quick, clean kill); the male servant showed signs of prolonged torture/suffering before a slow death; **[[Jaie Schraeder]]** was killed by a single sword thrust straight through the heart, almost no defensive wounds — a highly skilled killer. All three were dead *before* the fire was set — the fire was staged to hide the murders, not the cause of death
