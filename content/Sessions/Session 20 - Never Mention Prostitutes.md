# Session 20 — Never Mention Prostitutes

**Played:** 2 May 2026
**In-game date:** 5 Jahrdrung 2512

## Summary

Word reached the party outside the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] that **[[Falconius Thorncobble|Connie]]** had been called away by family trouble and would not be returning — a quiet, painful loss. With the [[Stradovski Vellum Papers|Stradovski medical treatise]] still in their possession and [[Christina Asper|Asper]] likely to seize and burn it on sight, the party debated where to keep it; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] won custody (and earned a hard slap from [[Hildegarde]] for a misogynistic aside). They delivered [[Marretta Schongauer]]'s sealed letter to **[[Karl Helpmann]]** — a bespectacled, irrepressibly mercantile peddlar with a large orange shop-cat — selling off odds and ends for a gold crown apiece, learning that Helpmann's "smuggling" runs serfs to Averland farms (he claimed never to have heard of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]), and walking away with a pawned set of Averland sun-livery armour for [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]. At the [[Temple of Shallya (Ferlangen)|Temple of Shallya]] they finally delivered [[Herga]] into safe care, and learned in passing that **rare and exotic diseases have begun to appear simultaneously in [[Wolfenburg]]** — confirmed by Shallyan colleagues arriving from the city only the past week. The matron pointed them at **[[Matron Isolde Uricht]]**, high priestess of the Shallyan temple in Wolfenburg, as their best contact. [[Hoffman Jannitzer]] then led them through a rough quarter — taverns, balconies, a red-lamped house — to **[[Frau Busch's Boarding House]]**, an island of stern propriety where [[Frau Busch]] laid down her rules (wash, eight and five, no drinking, one beeswax candle a week) and her daughter Gertrude bathed and deloused the party with mechanical efficiency. Over dinner [[Christina Asper|Asper]] arrived with **[[Lucas Pollack]]** — early forties, graying, stubbled, hung with a kind of detached disillusionment — and the party shared the Wolfenburg disease news, which Asper recorded; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] formally apologised for his earlier conduct and Asper waved it off. Late at night [[Hildegarde]] secured a key from [[Frau Busch]] and visited the nearby brothel — not for service, but for information — paying two and a half crowns to a high-class worker for the night, and walking away with a list of corrupt merchants involved in human trafficking, the name **[[Marretta Schongauer]]** among them, and a plea to investigate girls recently gone missing from the trade. **[[Wolfenburg]]** is now the unmistakable next destination.

## Events

### Outside the Temple of Verena — Connie Departs

- The party gathered outside the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] under a heavy mood; word had come from **[[Falconius Thorncobble|Connie]]** that family troubles had forced him to step away from the company, perhaps for good
- The others spoke of him fondly — a singular halfling, his vicious dog [[Rolf]] as much a fixture as he was — and resolved to keep in touch and hold his place open
- The debate then turned to the **[[Stradovski Vellum Papers|Stradovski medical treatise]]** the party had been carrying since the road north
  - [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] proposed surrendering it to the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] for safekeeping, fearing [[Christina Asper|Asper]] would destroy it if she found it
  - [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] argued fiercely against — the book is the party's best tool to prove [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s innocence or guilt
  - [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] dismissed [[Hildegarde]]'s objection with a cutting remark about *women and complaining*; she answered with a firm slap; he conceded, theatrically, to mind his words
  - [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] volunteered to carry the book himself — ostensibly to keep [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] clear of suspicion; in practice no one believed he meant to hand it over to anyone

### Helpmann's General Goods — Letter Delivered

- The party found **[[Helpmann's General Goods]]**, a cluttered little storefront with a large orange cat asleep on a shelf, presided over by **[[Karl Helpmann]]** — bespectacled, receding hairline, an irrepressible instinct to sell something to anyone in the room
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] slid the sealed letter from [[Marretta Schongauer]] across the counter; Helpmann opened it with a letter opener he immediately tried to sell them
- The letter visibly **created modest work** for Helpmann; he mentioned **opportunities for laborers heading toward [[Averland]]** and offered to **buy goods from the party at a fraction of their value**, quietly noting the guild rules made selling without a licence difficult
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] unloaded a pouch of valuables; each member walked away with **roughly a gold crown** for their trouble
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] quietly discovered a **used set of armour bearing the Averland sun** tucked into a dusty corner of the shop and bought it
- [[Hildegarde]] showed Helpmann a **freshly minted illegal penny** and asked if he had seen others circulating; he inspected it carefully through a magnifying glass (and offered to sell her one) but admitted he had seen nothing of the sort in peddlar circles
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] gently probed whether Helpmann's smuggling network might have moved a certain physician; **Helpmann showed no recognition of the name [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]** and explained that his operation primarily relocates **serfs from the north to farms in [[Averland]]**, not men of science and their equipment
- [[Hildegarde]] left a shilling on the counter and asked him to send word if he heard anything of a **young dark-haired woman traveling with a tall boxer** ([[Elfrieda Teuschel]] and her companion)
- Before leaving, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] sold his old hunting bow — sentimentally claimed as a were-rabbit slayer — to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] for **one gold crown** after a spirited round of haggling

### Temple of Shallya — Herga Delivered, Word from Wolfenburg

- The party went to the **[[Temple of Shallya (Ferlangen)|Temple of Shallya]]** to fulfil their long-standing promise to see **[[Herga]]** safely delivered
- A **harried priestess with wild hair escaping her habit** met them; [[Hildegarde]] explained the girl's ordeal — kidnapped by reavers, nearly sold by opportunistic farmers, carried south across dangerous roads
- The priestess accepted Herga and promised to find a trusted worshipper to escort her home to [[Nordland]]
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] donated generously
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] asked the priestess about rumours of unusual illness in the region:
  - Colleagues arriving from **[[Wolfenburg]]** had brought word of **rare and exotic diseases appearing simultaneously** in the provincial capital — ailments uncommon enough that **even one would be alarming, let alone several at once**
  - The rumours had reached [[Ferlangen]] only **about a week prior** — the outbreaks themselves are recent
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] quietly confided that the party were pursuing a physician who might be the source
- The priestess provided the name of **[[Matron Isolde Uricht]]**, **high priestess of the Shallyan temple in [[Wolfenburg]]**, as their best contact for further information
- [[Hildegarde]] pressed a **dagger into Herga's hands** and told her to keep it close; [[Herga]] gave tearful hugs to those who had been kind to her on the long road south

### Frau Busch's Boarding House — Settling In

- **[[Hoffman Jannitzer]]** led the party through a rough stretch of [[Ferlangen]] — past rowdy taverns and a brothel where women called down from balconies — to **[[Frau Busch's Boarding House]]**, a pristine, freshly whitewashed building standing like a small fortress of propriety amid the disorder
- **[[Frau Busch]]** met them at the door and laid out her rules:
  - Wash before bed
  - Be present for meals at **eight in the morning and five in the evening**
  - **No drinking** on the premises, and no arriving in a drunken state
- She remarked that the neighbourhood had fallen apart around her over the years, but that she had no intention of leaving the house her late husband had left her
- Her daughter **Gertrude** prepared baths in turn, mechanically washing hair and checking for lice with the detached efficiency of someone who had done it a thousand times
- Clothes were taken to be laundered overnight
- Each guest was issued a **single beeswax candle**, with the stern instruction that it had to last the week

### Dinner — Lucas Pollack Arrives

- Over a plain but well-prepared dinner of boiled vegetables and unseasoned meat, **[[Christina Asper]]** arrived with her fellow witch hunter, **[[Lucas Pollack]]** — a man in his early forties, graying hair, heavy stubble, and an air of detached disillusionment hanging around him like a second coat
- [[Christina Asper|Asper]] introduced him briefly; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] **formally apologised** for his earlier misunderstandings about her order, which she dismissed with a wave and a request to move forward with the investigation
- The party shared the news from the [[Temple of Shallya (Ferlangen)|Shallyan temple]] about the **[[Wolfenburg]] disease outbreaks**, which [[Christina Asper|Asper]] recorded with characteristic precision in her silk-lined notebook
- [[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]] listened with an expression of **mild, unfocused interest**
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] dragged a chair loudly across the floor, sat down across from [[Christina Asper|Asper]], crossed his arms, and asked how well she knew Pollack; she looked at him for a long moment before she understood what he was implying, and answered with a single word: **"Hardly."**

### Hildegarde at the Brothel — Information, Not Service

- After [[Frau Busch]] began casting pointed looks, [[Hildegarde]] quietly pulled the matron aside and asked for a **late-night exception** — not for any improper reason, but because the women working at the nearby brothel might have information pertinent to the investigation
- [[Frau Busch]] reluctantly agreed, lending a key on the condition it was returned by morning and the door locked securely behind her
- [[Hildegarde]] left her armour in her room, tucked her **mace under her arm**, and made her way down the street to the red-lamped house
- A dozen working girls moved through a well-appointed meet-and-greet room with practiced ease
- [[Hildegarde]] identified a **well-dressed, high-class woman** and negotiated a price of **two and a half crowns for the entire night**
- [[Hildegarde]] wanted information, not services. The woman:
  - Had **seen no sign of the illegal coins**
  - Knew the city's underworld well, and on condition of strict anonymity provided **names of merchants involved in human trafficking** — men who had caused trouble for the girls and their trade
  - Among the names was one already familiar: **[[Marretta Schongauer]]** — the "guildmistress" from the [[Fist of Amber]], now resurfacing in a far darker context
- [[Hildegarde]] also asked about **girls who had recently gone missing**, suspecting they might be connected to [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s experiments, and promised to look into their disappearances
- [[Hildegarde]] spent the rest of the night simply talking — offering the woman a rare evening of rest and something closer to friendship than transaction

### Berin and Orazio

- In the days since arrival in [[Ferlangen]], [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] had been quietly working with his warhorse **[[Orazio]]**
- Orazio had finally **learned to come when called** — a small but meaningful victory in a journey that had offered precious few
