# Session 42 — I Almost Killed You All

## Summary
[[Hildegarde]] followed the older black-cloaked man who had taken [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] through the city's thief routes to a courtyard where two more [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]] waited, then trailed all three to the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]], where [[Ganav]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] had spent an hour setting an ambush around the bait ring (caltrops on the path to the table, Boris watching both doors, Ganav posing as a statue). The ambush slipped: the leader, **[[Karel Vantz]]**, spotted Ganav at once. Hilde crushed the skull of **[[Anselm Rathke]]**, the young man holding Marla; Karel then cut Boris to pieces, fled when his woman, **[[Petra Voss]]**, was incapacitated, and — chased by Ganav — shattered Ganav's already-broken arm a second time before walking away.

The party kept Petra and the unconscious Anselm, kept Marla out of the room, and questioned them. The Lurkers had come for whatever [[Azmus Beham]] had "left for us": he had stopped paying their body-movers, **left town that morning through the East Gate** on a handcart of chests, and told the gang a magical trinket was waiting. Petra knew nothing of the Medical Union, said the Lurkers did **not** burn Gerd's household, and gave rumours of the **Shallyan orphanage** — boys brought in from outlying towns who "fall sick and disappear overnight." Hilde and Boris disagreed over the prisoners' fate; Hilde took them to the **Temple of Verena** and was paid **3 shillings**.

## Events

### Following the Lurker
- Hildegarde trailed the older cloaked man and Marla through back alleys and over a fence to a tucked-away courtyard, chalking an **H** on a wall at each turn in case her party came looking
- Two more Lurkers met them: a **thin young man** and a **woman with a sling**. The older man: a Myrmidian had stirred the crowd against him so he ran, but they still had the girl and **Muntz would come back for her**. Next stop: *"the temple, to see what Beham left for us"*
- They approached the temple's single door; Hildegarde followed at a distance

### The temple, prepared
- The GM mapped the temple (fixed statues as cover). Ganav and Boris had about an hour; both the main door and the catacomb door were **unlocked**
- Caltrops covered one **two-by-two square** between the door and the table with the ring and certificate. Boris took a hiding spot watching both entrances; Ganav tried to pass as a statue. [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] had gone home: no purpose, and he disliked the ghosts
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] (his player away), [[Ewalt Wrangel]], [[Hilde Muntz]] and [[Christina Asper]] were off-screen at the burial site

### The ambush goes wrong
- The Lurkers entered: a **salt-and-pepper-bearded leader**, the woman, and the young man leading Marla by the arm. *"Beham did leave it open… but that sign's gone. Somebody's been in here. Be on guard"*
- The leader spotted Ganav; Ganav's bluff (*"you don't think I'd come alone"*) drew one turn. Boris, aimed in, loosed a **called shot at the head** of the young man holding Marla — it **parted his hair and missed**
- [[Hildegarde]], unseen at the door, **charged** the young man with Strike to Stun and a +20 surprise bonus, rolled a critical and **caved his skull in**. He dropped unconscious; Marla hid behind her. Petra's first sling shot at Ganav ricocheted off a statue's head

### The sword fight
- The leader whipped out his sword and **charged Boris**, cutting his left leg (a bleeding condition, later removed with Resolve) and, over several rounds, carving him badly — a 12-damage hit even after three Advantage spent on defence
- Ganav's off-hand **pistol crossbow** shot (−50: shooting into melee, off-hand, move) *"ping-pinged"* around the ceiling; his later sword attacks with outnumbering and flanking bonuses kept missing. Petra's sling shots slid past Hilde, one cutting her **leggings**
- Hilde tried an Intimidate and a Guile bluff (*reinforcements are coming*) on the leader and failed, then bonked Petra: Petra fumbled and **knuckle-dustered herself in the face twice**, stunned, then incapacitated
- Calling *"let's get out of here,"* the leader spent two Advantage and **sprinted from the temple, abandoning both companions**

### The chase
- Ganav gave chase under the *Up in Arms* pursuit rules (p.127), caught the man **half a city block away**, and tried a non-lethal, no-critical attack from behind. The leader turned and rolled an **11-critical on Ganav's arm** — the one already broken three weeks — leaving a **major broken bone and a ruined elbow**, Ganav stunned and bleeding
- Stunned and with no Resolve, Ganav could only stagger as the man **sheathed his sword and trotted off into an alley**

### Aftermath
- Ganav staunched his bleeding (Fortune re-roll and a bandage) and is **fatigued** (−10 to everything) until he sleeps; by his reckoning about two months of added healing, and he will need surgery
- He healed [[Anselm Rathke|Anselm]] (one wound back) and then Boris — a **fumble gave Boris a minor infection**, which sets in tomorrow (leg 20% larger, throbbing). Boris kept his last wound
- The party stripped the prisoners of weapons and split the coin four ways: Ganav kept the **knuckle dusters** as a "story item," Hilde took the dagger; Boris objected on principle to pilfering the living. Hilde briefly took Petra's jerkin, returned it when Boris pointed out how wrong it was

### The interrogation
- Marla was kept out of the room (*"I'm definitely not interrogating people in front of a child"*). The party agreed not to use violence. Boris, the best Intimidator, rolled **poorly**; the GM let Petra give only part of the story (she had a Cool of 32)
- **Why they came:** to get *"our just due"* — the "fat man" ([[Azmus Beham]]) had promised something of value would be left behind. He had **stopped paying them to move bodies** and **left town first thing that morning on a handcart through the East Gate**, loaded with luggage and chests, giving them money on the way out
- **The bodies:** the Lurkers took the corpses down to the **Wolfen** at **3 a.m.** and tossed them in. **Gunther** and **Fritz** — young Lurkers who hauled the bodies — vanished *"a couple of months ago,"* around Hexenstag; the Lurkers assumed they had been paid off and run. Told that two Lurkers were found half-eaten by ghouls in the sewers, **Petra faltered**
- **Gerd:** she knew him as a servant who slipped tips about the Physicians Guild to "Karel" so the Lurkers could get their wounded to the right doctor; hadn't seen him in months, knew of the fire but said *no Lurker* burned the house. *"Asmus worked for Asmus — a charlatan at heart."* She had **never heard of the Medical Union**
- **Hilde Muntz:** *"Nobody leaves the Lurkers"* when they know where all the operations are
- **The Shallyan orphanage:** the Lurkers **avoid it**. It has muscle that brings in unwanted or orphaned boys from surrounding towns; boys who escape say the new arrivals **"fall sick and disappear overnight"** with **plague going through the dormitory night by night**
- **Marla:** she had gone looking for Hilde Muntz and **Karel caught her first** — his idea

### The Temple of Verena
- Hilde and Boris disagreed: Hilde insisted on turning the prisoners in as a bounty hunter, on principle (*"they put a child in danger"*); Boris wanted them freed, being "small fry" and cooperative. Boris yielded as a player and said he would call in the favour later
- At the bounty hall of the [[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]], a servant checked Hilde's license, found a posting for the **Black Cloak Lurkers at 2 shillings alive for questioning**, and paid **3 shillings** for the pair. Hilde asked that their cooperation be noted. Ganav's surgery was left for between sessions
