# Session 37 — Especially the Hugs

## Summary
The docks fight from [[Session 36 - Most Discreet Ambush Ever|Session 36]] resolved decisively in the party's favour. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], still holding his elevated position, methodically shot the opposition apart — putting an arrow through the boat captain's knee and wounding crew until the entire opposing force was incapacitated. One stevedore bled out and died on the deck. [[Hildegarde]] twisted clear of [[Ansgar Knochenbrecher|Ansgar]]'s blows on the rain-slicked planking until he caught her a second time in a bear hug that pinned her arms to her chest, and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] cut through the hired stevedores with brutal efficiency.

With her crew broken and Boris still picking targets, **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]] called for terms** — surrender in exchange for safe passage. Hildegarde negotiated the ceasefire while still locked in Ansgar's grip; Ansgar released her but kept her between himself and Boris's firing line as insurance. Both sides lowered weapons and met by the cargo crane.

**The interrogation was the heart of the session, and it was a rich one.** Teuschel — arms crossed, lips pursed, furious — recognised Berin and Hildegarde from her house in [[Beeckerhoven]] and demanded to know why they had chased her across the Empire. She admitted **she had helped [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] escape Beeckerhoven**, connected him with the merchant **[[Klaus von Kolditz|Von Kolditz]]** in [[Ferlangen]], and arranged his onward passage to [[Wolfenburg]]. Beyond that she claimed ignorance of his whereabouts, and Boris read her as **protecting certain truths without being outright deceptive**.

She then named the thing the party has been circling for thirty sessions: **the [[Medical Union]]** — not a church and not a hierarchy, but small cells of like-minded individuals scattered across cities from Nordland to Ostland, united only by a willingness to practise medicine past what the authorities sanction. She spoke of Stradovski with something close to reverence, crediting him with curing a consumption that had left her bedridden with months to live, by weeks of potions and daily needle pricks. She dismissed **[[Elsie (Medical Union)|Elsie]]**'s death with chilling indifference — a horse tripped, the girl snapped her neck, she was of no consequence. And she gave up three things the party did not have: that **Von Kolditz has an arrangement with the [[Howling Ones]]** that lets his caravans pass the roads unharassed; that the Medical Union **operates in the plague village of [[Seuchenshof]] under the guise of a Shallyan priest**, harvesting live disease strains from the infected and **deliberately infecting people when natural samples run short**; and that **Stradovski lets slip a faint Kislevite accent** when deeply absorbed in his work.

Pressed on the stolen Nordland gold, Teuschel turned over **thirty crowns** from her purse and said the bulk had already gone ahead to **Altdorf**. [[Ansgar Knochenbrecher|Ansgar]] slammed the purse into Hildegarde's palm with a growl; Hildegarde smiled and told him she would remember the good times, **especially the hugs**. Boris put a final shot through the barge's last mooring rope, and the vessel spun out into the current, struck the bridge supports, and drifted away.

The watch arrived almost at once. Six armed watchmen came down the quay from the **[[Wolfen Bridge]]**, the surviving stevedore trailing behind and pointing. Boris and the others slipped away; Hildegarde and Berin stayed, reasoning that running would only look like guilt, and were marched to the [[Wolfenburg Courthouse|courthouse]] to face **[[Captain Crais]]**. It was there — mid-testimony — that Hildegarde learned **for the first time** that Berin had cut a man in the groin during the fight, and gave him a look that said so.

Crais was weary and unimpressed: warrants or no warrants, he cannot have bodies lining the streets of his city. The stevedore dropped **a freshly minted Nordland crown** on the desk as proof of his pay, and Crais pointed out that the Imperial warrant covers Stradovski, not stolen coin. Hildegarde argued the coin and the fugitive were the same thread, and that the stevedores had unwittingly helped a wanted criminal escape. Crais demanded corroboration; Hildegarde named **[[Hoffman Jannitzer]]**, **[[Christina Asper]]** and **[[Lucas Pollack]]**. Within half an hour Asper and Pollack arrived, spoke privately with Crais, and the party walked — with a firm warning not to take the law into their own hands again.

Outside, Asper asked the **full party** to meet her at sundown at the **[[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]**. The intervening hours went to restocking and armour repair, and to collecting Boris and [[Ganav]], who turned up smelling of liquor and a local street food called **stick rat**.

The party also called at the **[[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg]]** — a granite longhouse flanked by giant stone dwarfs, with twenty-foot doors — where the entire hall of malt-drinking dwarfs fell silent at their entrance. Clan elder **[[Borin Anvilhart]]** heard them out and volunteered two things: **a four-tumbler dwarven sewer key had been stolen in broad daylight** from one of his sewer jacks, almost certainly by one of the city's gangs; and a younger dwarf had seen **bodies wrapped in linen and sewn shut** being moved quietly through the tunnels. Borin loaned the party a key for **forty-eight hours under oath of return** and assigned the sewer jack **[[Snorri Belgrimson]]** to guide them through the northern sewers, warning that the route to the catacombs beneath the Temple of the All Gods is treacherous.

At twilight, at the **Altar of Solkan**, [[Christina Asper]] finally asked for something for herself. Her close friend **[[Hilde Muntz]]** — a young woman, ex-[[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]], since drawn to a Shallyan calling — is **trapped inside the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]**, hunted by both the [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] and the Lurkers over the stolen lottery purse. Pollack, barely hiding his amusement, observed that the ice queen has a beating heart after all, and earned a glare for it.

**And Pollack broke the case the party did not know they were carrying:** the stolen money was the **Steel Fist's illegal lottery take, not the Prince's** — the Lurkers had misdirected the party to bury their own involvement. Neither gang will violate the temple's sanctuary, which matters too much to their own people as a source of healing, but neither will let Hilde walk, and every exit is watched. Asper warned that she and Pollack are themselves watched whenever they move through the city. The party settled on **smuggling Hilde out via the merchant network that previously moved people to Averland**, reasoning a well-protected convoy beats carrying her through enemy ground — and turned to the sewers still waiting beneath them.
