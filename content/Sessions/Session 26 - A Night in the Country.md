# Session 26 — A Night in the Country

**Played:** 13 June 2026
**In-game date:** 9–10 Jahrdrung 2512 (the night camp at the shrine into the next morning)

## Summary

The battered caravan made camp for the night at the [[Wayside Shrine]] — an old temple to [[Taal]] amid a ring of standing stones — still nursing the wounds of the second ambush. [[Danderich Flisshausten|Sir Danderich]]'s horse was dead, [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s mount gravely wounded, and [[Ganav]] raging about the shattered clavicle that, before he fell in with this group, he had never even needed to know the name of. The halflings cooked a rabbit stew from meat shared by **[[Mathis Haack]]**, the self-described forest warden — though [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] still read him as a poacher at best.

The party walled the wagons with makeshift branch barricades and set a four-shift watch. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] "interrogated" the captured [[Howling Ones]] prisoner by reading [[Myrmidia|the books of Myrmidia]] at him at length; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] gagged the man with one of [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s socks before he could call out — but not before he whispered one name as a warning: **Astrid**, someone the party very much did not want to meet.

Through the night the forest worked at them: human-made howls, shadowy figures snapping branches by hand to amplify the sound, and **white-fletched arrows** thumping into the wagons every twenty minutes — never hitting anything vital, just grinding the caravan toward exhaustion. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] climbed a tree and spotted the trick; [[Mangold]] woke half-strangling Boris before signalling four or five scouts at the perimeter; [[Herman Wurt|Herman]] nearly panicked until [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] steadied him in Battle Tongue. On the third watch [[Ganav]] recognised the exhaustion tactic for what it was and quietly **countered it with care** — stoking the fire to keep the sleepers warm rather than raising an alarm. Then the great bone-shaking wolf howl returned, and this time real wolves answered it from every side. On the final watch [[Hildegarde]] refused to merely endure it: she fired one of the bandits' own arrows back into the dark, drawing mocking laughter — and was entirely unbothered, having made her point.

Morning brought two discoveries. **[[Mathis Haack]] had slipped away** during the night without a word, and a voice from the treeline asked for **parlay**. [[Ganav]] vouched that the [[Howling Ones]] would honour its sanctity, so [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] rode out to meet a single rider: **[[Astrid Hofhaimer]]** — a woman with **striking orange eyes and pinpoint pupils**, her **right arm wrapped in a blood-soaked bandage** (the same arm [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s arrow had pierced when he drove off the wolf). She gave no name, only a demand: hand over **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s promissory notes** by noon, or her bowmen would harry the caravan every mile to [[Dassel]]. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s every overture — alliance, appeals to leadership — met cold contempt, and she rode back into the trees.

Back at camp, [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] explained what the notes were — debts collected from merchants in [[Ferlangen]] — and named his best guess for the man behind the attacks: **[[Klaus von Kolditz|Augustus van Kolditz]]**, a powerful antique dealer who had owed him over five hundred crowns and been coerced into paying. He produced an actual banknote — a printed document with a stamped family crest, near-impossible to forge. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] floated reaching out to the rival merchant **Hofbauer**, who might help the caravan reach [[Wolfenburg]] if [[Klaus von Kolditz|Kolditz]] were truly funding the cult. After a debate ranging from Ganav's refusal to surrender to bandits, to Boris's "burn the forest down," to Hildegarde's forgery idea, the party rallied behind [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s plan: stake the gagged prisoner at a meeting point with a **decoy bundle** (perhaps [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]]'s cookbook dressed as a satchel of notes) and lure the bandits into an ambush on the party's own terms. The NPCs were eager — [[Christina Asper|Asper]] cocked her pistol and declared she owed the great wolf a reckoning; even one-armed [[Ganav]] took a crossbow pistol from [[Hildegarde]]. As noon crept closer, they began to prepare the trap.
