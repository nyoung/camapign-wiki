# Session 40 — A Wonderfully Thought-Out, Half-Baked Plan

## Summary
The party woke at the [[Tree and Rut Inn]] on what they took to be **the last day of the dwarves' key loan** and set the plan to free [[Hilde Muntz]] in motion: show the priest of Mórr the desecrated dead, win Hilde's trust through [[Christina Asper]], bring her out through the temple's laundry drain and the sewers, and smuggle her out of the city in a **funeral procession through the North Gate**, where Asper would be waiting by about half past one. They split into an above-ground group ([[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]]) and an underground group ([[Ganav]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]). As they left the inn an **Imperial messenger** nearly rode them down: a gaunt rider in black and white, the sign of Karl Franz on his cloak with a **curled horn** beneath it.

Asper agreed to the plan at the [[Witch Hunters Guild]] and gave Hildegarde a **silk handkerchief initialed "C.A."** as a token for Hilde. The underground group collected [[Snorri Belgrimson]] and was well into the sewers before everyone realised **nobody had fetched the priest of Mórr**. The party reassembled at the [[Garden of Mórr]], where **[[Ewalt Wrangel]]** — shocked at the evidence against [[Azmus Beham]] — reluctantly agreed to enter the sewers. On the way they found the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] **closed "until further notice."**

At the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]], Berin stirred the courtyard with a speech to cover them while Hildegarde, who had spotted **Steel Fist watchers** and an odd **hand signal** from the servant [[Nina Trielffen]], won Hilde over with the handkerchief. [[Marla Hengist]], a small orphan, let slip to Hildegarde that the sisters are **sheltering a man called Gerd, wrapped in bandages, hidden in the temple**. Hilde was lowered down the laundry drain on a rope of bedsheets and joined the party in the sewers. On the way to the catacombs the party was **ambushed by three Steel Fist thugs** demanding "the girl"; one was shot dead and two fled, and Snorri finally opened the human gate into the catacombs.

## Events

### Planning at the inn
- The party's reckoning: **this is the last day** the elder of the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|Dwarven Hall]] allowed them to use the borrowed sewer key (Berin raised it; no one disputed it)
- [[Hildegarde]] (back after missing Session 39) was brought up to speed: the laundry drain, the sewers, and the Lurker truce
- She still held the **30 gold crowns of minted coin** from [[Beeckerhoven]]; the party agreed to **keep it together** and hand it in for Empire Points rather than spend it
- **The plan, as argued out at length:** (1) bring the priest of Mórr to see the corpses in the sewers, so that he can back an accusation against [[Azmus Beham]] and perhaps exorcise the catacomb spirits; (2) warn [[Christina Asper]] and win [[Hilde Muntz]]'s trust; (3) get Hilde out through the laundry drain; (4) smuggle her out of the city **in a funeral procession through the North Gate**, since the Temple of the All Gods is only a stone's throw from the gates
- **Two groups:** Berin and Hildegarde above ground ("the respectable group": Berin in armour with real authority); Ganav and Boris below ("the dirty group")

### The Imperial messenger
- Stepping out the door, the party heard a shout and a clatter of hooves: a **thin rider** on a horse **drenched in foam and sweat**, in a black-and-white cloak with **Karl Franz's sign** sewn on the back and **a curled horn beneath it**, shouting *"Imperial Messenger — make way!"*
- He **brushed Boris's tunic** as he passed, would clearly have ridden over anyone in his way, and vanished around the corner and over the bridge
- Boris asked if he could shoot him. [[Ganav]]: *"Stay out of their way. Self-important people are very dangerous because they get you noticed"*; if you want to rob couriers, do it outside the city

### Asper at the Witch Hunters Guild
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] found the [[Witch Hunters Guild]]: a **looming black-stone building with a dark slate roof**, and a **tarnished brass** emblem above the door. A servant fetched Asper while other witch hunters passed through, **mostly Solkanites, some bearing the symbols of Verena, and one affiliated with Sigmar**
- Asper led them out to a quiet spot on the road. Hildegarde laid out the plan: the **laundry chute**, the sewers, the **[[Snorri Belgrimson|sewer jack]]** to escort Hilde under the walls, and the question of what happens beyond
- Berin proposed the **funeral procession** through the **North Gate** with the priest of Mórr. Asper: *"Then I can be waiting out there and accompany any funeral procession without anybody paying much attention"* — **outside the North Gate by half past one** (the GM first said "after one"). **She believes the Steel Fists are watching her**, so she will not be seen with the party beforehand
- Berin asked whether she or [[Lucas Pollack]] could be nearby when the party emerged from the Temple of the All Gods, in case [[Azmus Beham]] caused trouble. She noted she had **leverage** over him
- On the priest of Mórr: *"A bit of a toady. I wouldn't trust him, but he seems mostly harmless"* — though Hildegarde relayed that he is **related to people more dangerous than he is**
- **She would not have Hilde meet the party at the Temple of the All Gods:** Hilde fears **the Steel Fist are tracking her** and does not want to lead them to the party
- **Her token for Hilde:** a **silk handkerchief initialed "C.A."** — *"She'll know who it comes from and what it means"* — kept in an inner pocket of her leather jacket, which seemed *"a little odd for who she is"*

### Down the sewers — and back up
- [[Ganav]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] collected [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] from the **Dwarf Longhouse** (door open, smoke, dwarves grumbling and laughing at them), and he led them back to the **same sewer door** as before. The lock needs **a half turn and a back-turn with the unusual dwarf key**, which turned easily
- His **davich lamp** is **candle-based and casts less light**, but it **flares up and extinguishes itself in sewer gas**. Order: **Ganav, Snorri, Boris**
- **Red reflective eyes** looked back from one side. Snorri: *"Just keep going. Ignore them. We need to get a bit of a rat hunt down here"*; Boris hoped they weren't were-rats; Snorri: *"worse, but you manlings are a bit squeamish about such business"*
- Mid-journey it dawned on the pair that **nobody had gone to fetch the priest**. Snorri *"starts cursing… rubbing his temple"* and led them back up. Berin and Hildegarde were already **heading the other way, to the Temple of Shallya**; the two groups ran into each other in the street, with an annoyed Snorri looking on
- The group argued over what each had understood: Berin had *"envisioned the priest of Mórr entering the Temple of Shallya"* by the front door; Boris and Hildegarde expected to **meet in the catacombs** and **confront Azmus**; **Boris:** *"What a wonderfully thought-out, half-baked plan."* The **laundry drain is 21 inches** (a man's shoulders fit, claustrophobically), and the party settled on **bringing the priest down through the sewers** instead
- **Hildegarde gave Boris the handkerchief to carry. Boris** snatched it — *"this is my precious now"* — then gave it back: *"That's one less competition between me and my beloved"*

### The Garden of Mórr
- A **raven** cawed from above, and **the Keeper opened the door before they touched it**: *"Friends, you've returned? I hope with news, and not needing the services of Mórr"*
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] explained: the dead beneath the Temple of the All Gods **do not sleep quietly**; the Keeper there is **paying gangs** to disturb and move corpses; there are **unquiet spirits** and **bodies fed on** just outside the catacombs. They have **a member of one of those gangs** willing to inform, if the party can get her out of the city: **[[Hilde Muntz]]**, sheltering in the Temple of Shallya
- **Evidence:** the **note** and a **funeral shroud**. Berin's Charm roll (an easy test) went well. **Wrangel:** *"I knew that Azmus was somehow a charlatan, cheating us, but I couldn't quite put my finger on the extent of his shenanigans. I'm willing to help in whatever way I can… within the laws of Mórr"*
- He **balked at the idea of a sewer** (he went *green*). Berin noted the party had also **reached out to the "noble wardens" of the Sewerjacks Guild**, and Snorri snorted. Hildegarde and Boris then tried to talk him round as a group, **assisted: a challenging Charm at +20**. Hildegarde botched her own attempt (*"Hildegarde is blushing red"*)
- **Wrangel agreed.** Gone for hours (Boris: *"Three hours later, he comes out with a lot of makeup and his hair done"*), he returned in **travelling boots and old trousers**, not his robes

### The closed temple
- Leaving the Garden, the party found **a large gathering in front of the cathedral**. Wrangel: *a local Sigmarite holy day — a throng and a training day in the plaza*. **Berin:** *"It is the day before Festag, so it's not a holy day"* (he, at least, would know the church calendar)
- Round the back, the **main door of the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]** was **shut, with a hastily painted wooden sign: *"Closed until further notice."*** Hildegarde: *"I'm just hoping he's not on the run and we won't be able to interrogate him at all"*
- **Underground order:** Ganav, Snorri, Wrangel, Boris. (*Snorri keeps kicking Ganav, grumbling*)

### The Temple of Shallya
- **The courtyard:** the sick and hungry waiting for help, relatives on stretchers propped by the wall. **Perception checks:**
  - A man on a corner **pretending to be nonchalant** in a **copper-coloured beret** — a [[Steel Fist Thief Ring|Steel Fist]] watcher
  - **Hildegarde** recognised a man leaning from an **upper window**: *"the guy that you met in the alleyway behind the cart that one time"*, *"a nice fellow as far as bandits go"*, who *"will not hesitate a second to shoot"* once they leave
- A **small servant woman** pushing a mop, [[Nina Trielffen]], asked who they wanted. **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] had gone to the Cathedral of Sigmar** for the day's ceremony; **Hilde Muntz was available**
- **Intuition (difficult):** Hildegarde saw that **Nina positioned and twisted her hand oddly as she spoke**, in a way she couldn't read; Berin noticed nothing. Hildegarde whispered to Berin that Nina **might have signalled the watchers** that someone had asked for Hilde
- **Berin's distraction.** He stood on a **bucket or block** in the courtyard: *"I am a servant of Myrmidia, the sister of Shallya, and she has sent me with a vision: there is terrible intrigue and disease amongst you, and this place is no longer safe. Those of you who can must flee immediately."* **Some were convinced, some uncertain**; arguments broke out behind them as they went in
- Nina led them through *"a maze of small rooms, corridors, linked buildings"* to **a long-stay ward** of **burn victims**, heavily bandaged and unconscious people on makeshift beds. **Hilde Muntz** was tending them, with a girl of about **12–13** shadowing her
- **The handkerchief:** *her expression turned from distrust to interest*. The girl, **[[Marla Hengist|Marla]]**, offered a place to talk: *"in by the linen closet. Nobody goes there"*
- **Berin kept Nina busy** with Shallyan and Myrmidian theology (a **difficult** Fellowship test, since he lacked the Blather skill)
- Hildegarde, privately: *"the temple is surrounded… there's three crossbow bolts going through your chest"* if Hilde walks out. The party's plan: **the secret passage in the washroom, the sewers, the priest of Mórr, a funeral procession, and Asper at the North Gate.** Hilde: *"You come from Asper, right?"* — and, after thinking: **"If Asper is okay with your plan, then so am I."** *She had everything she needed.* **Marla** tugged at her cloak: *"you can't go… I'm going with you."* **Hilde, for the first time, pushed her back: "Hush."** Marla stood stunned
- **Hilde took her leave of Nina** with a hug; Nina: *"I hope that they know what they're doing."* Marla was brought along down a **circular stair** to a **basement laundry**: large basins, wet sheets hanging, a **floor sloped to a single grate**, **cropped up on one side** with **rough charcoal marks** — [[Ganav]]'s chalk from Session 39

### Marla's secret
- Out of Berin's earshot (he had asked Nina, not Marla, where the temple's laundry was), [[Hildegarde]] spoke to the girl. Marla said **[[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] "isn't like that"** (a bandit or burglar); *"he's just another one who's trying to escape… I thought maybe he could go along."* **The sisters are sheltering him in the temple — nobody's supposed to know.** She had seen him up in the night: **brown hair, a red tunic, wrapped in bandages**; she couldn't give a better description, *because she's a child*
- Hildegarde, posing as a **bounty hunter** looking after someone people had tried to kill, **gave Marla a silver coin** and promised to come back, saying *"we'll sleep on that"*
- **She did not tell Berin there.** Later, in the sewer, she told **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]** alone

### Down the drain
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] knotted **bed sheets into a rope** (Nina and Hilde winced at the "newly cleaned" linen) and lowered it down the pipe. **Berin sang the refrain of one of Hildegarde's drinking songs** down the pipe so those below would know it was a friend
- **Hilde Muntz went down first**, *without much comment: "you have a feeling she's done this before."* In the sewer: *"Well, hello. I guess you're the rest of the organization."* — and, to the priest: *"you're the priest from Mórr. I think we met briefly"*
- A **four-foot-tall tunnel** with a little pipe; Berin and Hildegarde followed down the drain

### Through the sewers
- **Order:** Ganav first (*"I don't mind going first again"*), Berin close behind, Hilde Muntz and the priest in the middle, Hildegarde guarding the rear with her **lantern** and **pistol crossbow** (Ganav had it; she took it back), Snorri guiding
- **A screaming mushroom** squealed ahead and to the right. **Boris and Hildegarde** heard **footsteps and splashing behind them**, someone following up the same tunnel, **150–200 yards** along
- **Boris stayed behind** in the dark with his lantern off and his sling ready, to ambush; Hildegarde relayed the news up the line
- At **a turn** near the catacomb gate, a **small wooden-and-steel arched bridge** crossing the channel; light puddles every **20 yards or so** from the street grates. A **makeshift torch** appeared behind them, **three figures** with a light

### Ambush at the bridge
- A **thug called out:** *"Give us the girl. You know she's wanted by the Steel Fist, and we know who you are."* [[Ganav]] suggested **swapping** Hilde for the wrong girl in a hasty clothes-change; [[Hildegarde]]: *"Respectfully, Ganav, go fuck yourself."* The party stonewalled: *"we don't know who you're talking about"*
- **Boris** shot first (the GM: *"that bloody git shot me"*). **Berin** prayed a **blessing of Myrmidia** on Boris and Hildegarde (*"Lady of Victory, grant us the skill to perceive the snare…"*) and **scattered caltrops** over a two-yard square in the walkway (bought for an earlier ambush). **Ganav's bad arm** meant he could not use a weapon, so he **guarded the gate and helped Snorri**; Hildegarde **lost a turn failing to load** her crossbow; she slid **a spare knife** to Hilde
- **Boris's sling bolt hit a thug in the head**: a **critical**; the man **fell off the platform into the water**, his **torch fizzling out**, *"effectively out of combat for good"* (the GM: *"I think he was pretty dead"*). **Hildegarde** then landed a **critical to a thug's head** (*"brain damage — blinded, stunned, fatigued"*)
- The **two remaining thugs fled** out of the light. Boris tempted to chase, but had to clear the caltrops — an **average Intelligence test** he **barely passed** — and **stayed**
- **Snorri** worked at the **human gate** (not a dwarf key — *"a human key"*); he would not let Ganav touch his tools, but Ganav's **assistance** gave +1s, and after a minute or two **he opened it**
- **[[Ewalt Wrangel]] inspected the two bodies** from before: only **rodent marks**, otherwise untouched. Verdict: **burials within the last two to three months**; *"That villain, he'll be brought to justice."* Berin told him about the **note** and the **catacombs**
- **Hildegarde, privately to Boris:** *"Gerd Breytenbach is in the Temple of Shallya."* Berin was not told

### The catacombs
- The gate opened and the party entered the catacombs. The session ended there (just past three hours); the next session starts in the catacombs
