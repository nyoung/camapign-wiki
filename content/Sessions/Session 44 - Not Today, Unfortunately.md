# Session 44 — Not Today, Unfortunately

## Summary
After a night's rest and a morning of repairs, [[Hildegarde]] and [[Ganav]] went to the [[Shallyan Orphanage]] posing as a couple who wanted to adopt (he as "Mr Costanza", an out-of-work innkeeper; she as "Berilder", an entertainer). They were let in by the porter [[Josef Skarn]], saw a spotless, well-appointed building with statues of Shallya, a class chanting the classical alphabet and a one-bed infirmary, and were interviewed by [[Dr Israhel Valera]] and his two apprentices, [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]] and [[Dr Mihai Buchner]]. A run of poor Charm and Intelligence tests (Hildegarde's especially) got them **refused: "not today, unfortunately."** As they left, Skarn tapped his nose and told them that if getting an orphan mattered enough, *"there might be things that can be done. Not here, not now, but get word to me."*

Regrouped with [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] at the [[Tree and Rut Inn]] by noon, the party compared notes and spent the rest of the session brainstorming ways in: contacting Skarn at closing time, finding an escaped street child, following Dr Imhot, finding the supplier, going underground, or planting a rumour at the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]. They agreed an order for the afternoon. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s player was absent.

## Events

### Repairs and gear
- [[Ganav]] swapped his leather jerkin for a **leather jack**, leather leggings and a skullcap, and bought a **one-handed warhammer** (the sap he wanted was not for sale; a brawling weapon needs Melee (Brawling) to get the full pummel quality)
- [[Hildegarde]] haggled the party's spare leather pieces for a few extra shillings and gave Ganav his share (five silver and six brass)
- Hildegarde's cape for [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] was due "on the fourth day": **after another night's rest**. She left her chainmail and armour with the wagon; Ganav kept a sword at his side

### The cover
- The plan from the earlier sessions: Hildegarde and Ganav would visit as a couple hoping to adopt, while [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] (and Boris) waited outside. Berin had a personal invitation from [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]] and was to knock on the door later if they did not come out
- Cover: Ganav as **"Mr Costanza"**, an unemployed innkeeper from the north, and Hildegarde as **"Berilder"** (the GM heard "Valerian"), an entertainer; they could not have children of their own

### The gate and the hall
- About eight or nine in the morning, foggy. A male voice drilling children could be heard from the gate. A bell pull brought [[Josef Skarn]], who made a show of lifting a heavy bar and unlocking the gate and led them inside
- Inside: wood panelling to waist height, whitewashed walls, a long hall with statues of Shallya at each end, a winding staircase opposite the door and the smell of roasting meat. A class was reciting the **Greek alphabet**
- A successful **Perception** test let Ganav see that the statue on the **right** was not the usual weeping Shallya but one with a **slight smirk, short spiky hair and an androgynous figure**, like the badly weathered statue at the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]; the one on the left was the ordinary crying Shallya
- A large handyman was mopping the hall and singing a nursery rhyme

### The interview with Dr Valera
- [[Dr Israhel Valera]] came out and said Hildegarde and Ganav looked familiar (he had operated on Ganav earlier)
- **Selection:** the orphanage places children with artisans for apprenticeships and sends the brightest to university; three have gone (Wittenheim, Altdorf and the engineers' school at Nuln). Children must leave at fourteen. Adoption needs an interview, a vocation judged healthy for the child and an **adoption fee** ("not a purchase")
- **Funding:** Valera was reluctant to discuss it: "not a public institution", supplied by the **Temple of Shallya and wealthy benefactors**. His two assistants, Imhot and Buchner, are unpaid apprentices working toward their credentials
- **Headcount:** Valera called out for the number and a voice answered **"67 right now."** He admitted an **influenza outbreak** had taken **eight or nine children in the past three months**, that they were still looking for the source and isolating the sick, and that three physicians oversee the children's care

### The tour
- [[Magister Emmerich Hobler]], an ink-stained instructor in spectacles and academic gown, came out behind a rush of children heading for the dining room. He starts the youngest on letters and numbers and takes the best as far as he can; he has placed one pupil at his own alma mater and one at the Collegium Theologica
- Hildegarde's Perception test of the passing children found **no scabbing, stitching or anything like Gerd's arm**; they wore a standard uniform, were reasonably well fed and showed ordinary bumps and bruises
- West wing for the boys, east wing for the girls; older children in the far rooms, younger near the main corridor. The ground floor's left wing is for lessons
- The **infirmary** is whitewashed, smells of vinegar, and has a single occupied bed: a green-faced seven-year-old, **Hansel**, with a supposed stomach ache from eating several days' sweets in one night. When Ganav asked if he would like new parents he said **"bugger off"**, and the nurse, [[Gretchen Pfodd]], showed a flash of anger. Ganav's Heal could not tell it from overeating or a stomach bug
- A **stair goes down beside the infirmary** ("our supplies; not really any of your business"). A second door under the **winding stair** in the main hall is **locked**, and Valera did not say who has the key. Valera steered them away from the dormitories and did not want to dwell on the influenza

### The tribunal
- In the office the three doctors sat on one side of a desk with notebooks. The GM set **three Intelligence and three Charm tests each at rising difficulty** (average, challenging, difficult). [[Dr Hartmut Imhot]] seemed warm and charismatic; [[Dr Mihai Buchner]] seemed naive and never warmed to them
- **[[Hildegarde]] rolled very badly** and came off as "an uncharming dullard"; Ganav seemed somewhat intelligent and honest but not charming. Valera said Mr Costanza seemed honest but "Miss Valerian" contradicted her own answers, and that he feared the children would be brought up in the wrong environment: **not today**
- Ganav apologised and argued that even doctors fail their patients; Hildegarde stormed off in crocodile tears hoping to snoop, but Skarn was waiting at the door and would not let them roam ("we don't have a privy for guests")

### Skarn's hint
- Skarn walked them to the gate, locked it behind them and said **if getting an orphan was that important, "there might be things that can be done"**. When Hildegarde asked how to reach him he said **"you'll figure it out"**. The party took it as an irregular adoption offer, and Ganav read him as the only warm one of the staff

### Berin's morning
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] (with Boris) watched the orphanage from the street. In the hours they were inside he saw **no children in the yard and no deliveries** and noted that the **gate stays locked** (two leaves, one wide, one narrow) in a rough neighbourhood of half-ruined buildings. The building backs onto another building, there is no rear alley and no sign of sewer access; a neighbouring shop was noted ("a pie shop")

### The brainstorm
- At the [[Tree and Rut Inn]] at noon the party worked through ways in. **Ideas:** pay Skarn for an irregular adoption or to smuggle a child out; rent a child on a trial basis; pay an escaped street child (Berin's irregulars or others) to show the way; find a way in from below (the infirmary stair, the locked door, the [[Dwarf Hall of Wolfenburg|dwarves' hall]] for service links); follow the food suppliers; follow Dr Imhot between the orphanage and the Physicians' Guild; plant a rumour at the Temple of Shallya to spook someone; tell [[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]] about [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] in the hope the Shallyans demand an inspection; send in [[Marla Hengist|Marla]] as bait (a joke); and break in at night
- **Why it is odd:** the orphanage looks far richer than its reputation; children are streamed into a favoured few and a manual majority; losing eight or nine children does not look like an institution that wants fewer mouths; the street children say kids who go in **never come back**; and Imhot carried jars between the orphanage and [[Jaie Schraeder]]'s house
- **Agreed order for the afternoon:** (1) find children who have escaped; (2) wait for Skarn at closing time and make contact; (3) if both fail, take Gerd's story to Gammer Isolde the next day so the Shallyans trigger an inspection
- Next session starts at **noon**
