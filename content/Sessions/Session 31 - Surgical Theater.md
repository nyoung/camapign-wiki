# Session 31 — Surgical Theater

**Played:** 18 July 2026
**In-game date:** 14 Jahrdrung 2512 (afternoon–evening)

## Summary

The party finally made it to the **Physicians' Guild** — first for treatment. **Hildegarde** was healed, and **Ganav** finally had the arrowhead embedded in his shoulder since the road ambush surgically removed — an agonizing operation that nearly bled him out before the arrow came free. Berin bought a healing poultice to aid his recovery afterward. While there, the party spoke with the elderly **Dr. Valera**, who claimed to know neither Professor Stradovski nor the murdered secretary Jaie Schraeder, and Berin spoke separately with **Dr. Imhort**, who invited him to visit the Shallyan Orphanage to go over the details of Ganav's surgery. Guild students offered nothing but contradictory, useless theories about who had killed Schraeder.

The party then turned to investigating **[[Jaie Schraeder]]**'s murder in earnest, questioning clerk **Danel Koberger**. He laid out the timeline: Schraeder received a letter purporting to be from his long-dead, penniless father on the morning of the 31st of Nachexen, left work early, and was never seen alive again — his townhouse burned the next day (killing his servants **Gerd Breytenbach** and **Louisa May**), and his body turned up the day after that, stabbed, on the road toward Kislev near a remote **Forest Inn**. Koberger also revealed that **Professor Melancthon**, the guildmaster who hired Schraeder, retired abruptly to Altdorf five years ago.

The party broke into the boarded-up **Schraeder's House** to search the burned-out ruin themselves. What they found was a deliberately staged scene: a crude, inaccurate blood-painting of a howling wolf (a frame-up, per Ganav — no real Howling One would draw it that badly), a ransacked study, and a master bedroom bonfire that had consumed Schraeder's books, papers, and even his personal shrine to Sigmar. **Ganav** found the real prize hidden beneath a dresser: a single surviving page of a crude, uneducated Classical-language copy of **Professor Udo Stradovski**'s forbidden disease research. Berin's read on the evidence — the house had been searched twice, once by whoever staged the murder and once by someone else afterward — combined with the letter from a "dead" father to produce a chilling theory: Stradovski may be using his own life-extension treatments on himself, meaning the "dead" father wasn't dead at all, and may have been Schraeder's actual sire.

The investigation was cut short when City Watch guards stormed the townhouse and marched the party to **Captain Crais**, furious that they'd read more authority into his gate-fee waiver than he'd intended. Berin talked him down by invoking the party's Howling Ones service and offering to translate the Classical documents the Watch couldn't read themselves — winning a look at the Watch's own evidence box, which contained more documents explicitly naming **Udo Stradovski**. Crais confirmed both servants' bodies were unidentifiable and that Schraeder himself had been stabbed and robbed to look like a bandit killing. The session ended with the party weighing their next move: the **Garden of Mórr** to examine the bodies, or the **Shallyan Orphanage** — tied to both the missing-children thread and the mysterious eastern-gate disease pattern.

## Events

### Treatment at the Physicians' Guild
- **Hildegarde** is healed of her injuries
- **Ganav** undergoes surgery to remove the arrowhead embedded in his shoulder since the road ambush; the operation is agonizing and he nearly bleeds out before the arrow comes free
- Berin buys a healing poultice afterward to aid Ganav's recovery
- The party speaks with **Dr. Valera**, an elderly physician, who says he knows neither Professor Stradovski nor Jaie Schraeder
- Berin speaks separately with **Dr. Imhort**, who invites him to visit the **Shallyan Orphanage** to go over the details of Ganav's surgery
- Guild students offer contradictory, largely useless opinions on who killed Schraeder

### Inquiry at the Physicians' Guild
- The party questions clerk **Danel Koberger** about Jaie Schraeder's death
- Koberger recounts the mysterious letter (supposedly from Schraeder's long-dead, penniless father) that drew Schraeder from the office on the 31st of Nachexen — his last day seen alive
- Timeline confirmed: townhouse burned 2nd of Nachexen (killing servants **Gerd Breytenbach** and **Louisa May**); Schraeder's stabbed body found the 3rd, near a remote **Forest Inn** a day's ride east toward Kislev
- Koberger reveals **Professor Melancthon**, the guildmaster who appointed Schraeder, retired suddenly to Altdorf five years ago
- Koberger describes Schraeder as usually reliable and someone who always stayed within the city — his sudden departure was very uncharacteristic; Schraeder also liked to brag about having come out of poverty
- Schraeder's desk and office are searched thoroughly and found to hold nothing — no secret compartment, no documents relating to Schraeder, Stradovski, Elfrieda Teuschel, or the network around them
- The party learns poor patients with the eastern illnesses are being routed to the **Shallyan Orphanage** rather than the Guild

### Investigating Schraeder's House
- Boris pries the City Watch's boarded seal off the front door with a crowbar
- The party finds a bloodstained hallway with drag marks, a kitchen meal abandoned mid-preparation, and a ransacked study
- Guest bedroom: a crude blood-painted howling wolf — Ganav immediately identifies it as a clumsy frame-up, not real Howling Ones iconography
- Master bedroom: gutted by a deliberate bonfire of Schraeder's books, papers, and his personal shrine to Sigmar; two body-shaped ash marks confirm where the servants died
- Ganav finds a hidden, uneducated Classical-language copy of **Professor Udo Stradovski**'s disease research beneath a dresser — Berin confirms the translation
- Berin determines the house was searched twice: once by the killers, once by someone else afterward who didn't take the remaining valuables

### Theories in the Ruins
- The party debates who's responsible: Ganav floats that Stradovski could be Schraeder's actual (not dead) father; Berin extends it further — Stradovski may be using his own life-extension treatments, explaining why the "dead father" letter didn't alarm Schraeder
- Ganav warns Stradovski may be systematically silencing anyone with knowledge of his methods
- The party debates their next move: the **Garden of Mórr** (to examine the bodies) vs. the **Shallyan Orphanage** (missing children, disease pattern)

### Caught by the Watch
- City Watch guards storm the townhouse at crossbow-point; Berin has one touch wolfsbane to rule out Howling One infiltration before the party surrenders
- The party is marched to **Captain Crais**, who is furious — his gate-fee waiver never authorized entering a sealed crime scene
- Berin smooths things over by invoking the party's Howling Ones service and offering to translate the Classical documents for the Watch
- Crais allows the party to examine the Watch's own evidence box: more Classical documents explicitly naming **Udo Stradovski**
- Crais confirms both servants' bodies were too burned to identify, and that Schraeder's own body was stabbed and robbed — staged to look like an ordinary bandit killing
