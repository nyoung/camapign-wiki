# Session 38 — A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make

## Summary
The party went underground. [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack]] left for the [[Witch Hunters Guild]], but not before Asper leaned on the **[[Azmus Beham|temple's proprietor]]** — who had arrived to close up for the night and went pale at the sight of a witch hunter — until he unbarred the iron gate to the **catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]** and let the party go down. The moment they were a short way in, they heard the bar crash back into place, the lock snap shut, and Beham's footsteps hurrying away across the temple floor.

Guided by the dwarven sewer jack [[Snorri Belgrimson]], the party followed the maze of stairs and corridors by lamp and lantern, marking the way with chalk. In the lower tiers, many of the sarcophagi had been **emptied, their nameplates scratched out and the symbols of the gods crudely defaced**. Then the air turned frigid and shadows flickered at the edge of the light — and a **translucent figure** appeared beside a sarcophagus, a man in relatively modern clothes, skin and skull overlaid on one another, weeping into transparent hands. Within moments there were **dozens**: men, women, children, even an infant; some fleshed, some skeletal or rotting; some clothed, some naked, some bound at wrist and ankle with chains. Silent, indifferent to the living, passing through stone and coffin alike. Prayers from [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Hildegarde]] had no visible effect. One skeletal spirit's hand passed through Hildegarde's shoulder and left it numb with a cold like death itself.

Past the spirits, at the far end of the lower catacombs, was **a short iron gate into the sewers** — **freshly oiled, worn from use**, and missing the dwarven security gate that should have stood on the far side, its hinges wrenched apart. Snorri said the lock was not the dwarves' own, pulled out his tools and had it open with little effort, and unrolled his **sewer map** to trace a route toward the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]], **no more than half a mile away**. He allowed the party to make notes of that section — but not to copy the whole thing.

In the cramped four-foot side tunnel beyond, [[Hildegarde]] spotted **a linen-wrapped corpse dragged to the side of the channel**; beside it lay a second, and on top of them a **folded note weighted down by four old Ostland silver coins**. The note read, roughly: **"Lurkers… more bodies by Festag. — A.B."** — initials that matched the man who had just locked them in. The bodies had been dead **more than a week and less than two months**, were **stripped of clothing and jewellery**, and had **healthy, well-kept teeth** — one **over seventy**, one middle-aged — not the dead of the poor. Hildegarde's tracking showed **a single barefoot, adult-human-sized person** had been **dragging bodies uphill** through the tunnel, and had not finished. At a dead end, someone had **punched a three-foot hole** into a **natural cavern** that reeked of rotting flesh.

Inside the cavern: discarded bones, another wrapped corpse with its head-covering torn away, and **ghouls**. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] spotted the first hanging from the ceiling, and the fight began. A larger **elder ghoul** — hung with spikes, bones and scraps of skin — came swinging down from a stalactite with a **sharpened tibia** and a paralysing bite. **The session ended mid-round**, with the elder ghoul **blinded, prone, stunned and bleeding — but not confirmed dead.**

## Events

### The catacomb gate
- Before the descent, the party asked whether [[Christina Asper]] had anything more to say. Nothing immediate — the rescue of [[Hilde Muntz]] was to wait until after the catacombs. Hildegarde reminded the table that the girl with the tattoos who had signed to Asper at the Shallyan temple was the friend in question
- **[[Azmus Beham]] arrived** to lock up for the night, cheerfully declaring it closing time — until he saw witch hunters. **Hildegarde's Charm test failed.** Asper excused herself and [[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]] to return to the [[Witch Hunters Guild]], pointedly saying she would hate to see these gentlemen and lady prevented from doing what they needed to in the catacombs — and knocked past Beham as she went
- Beham, visibly ill, agreed to let them down. **He unlocked a padlock, lifted a heavy iron bar, and opened the gate.** Snorri lit his dwarven lamp; Hildegarde brought out her lantern
- **Beham's ring of keys held no dwarven key** — only ordinary two- and three-notch iron keys. (Hildegarde had asked whether the stolen key was a real key or a modern manhole tool: it is a real key, a more complicated dwarven design)
- Once a short way in, the party heard **the bar go back, the lock snap shut, and Beham's quick footsteps** crossing the temple floor. Hildegarde: *"That was to be expected."*
- They **chalked** their route as they went

### The spirits
- In the lower tiers, **occupied** crypts sat beside **empty ones with nameplates scratched out and gods' symbols crudely removed**
- The air turned **frigid**; hairs rose; shadows flickered at the edge of the light. **Boris** compared it to the last time he felt this — the **Were-Rabbit**
- The first apparition: **a translucent man in fairly modern clothes, skull visible through skin, weeping into his hands**. Everyone rolled **Fear**; **Hildegarde and Boris passed** (Hildegarde was still hiding behind Berin). Berin offered a **prayer to Myrmidia**; Hildegarde asked **Shallya** for help
- **Dozens** appeared. Hildegarde and Berin's prayers **had no visible effect**
- A **Theology** check: when last rites are done properly the spirit is separated from the body and passes on — **something has captured these spirits**. They **were not here when the party last visited** in [[Session 33 - Plot Twist|Session 33]]. The party guessed **25–30 visible at least**. Berin lacked the expertise to say more — a specialist priest would be needed
- **Boris:** *"It looks like Mórr hasn't been doing his job properly around here."* No sign of disease, wounds or cause of death on any of them; **no pattern** to who is fleshed, skeletal, clothed or chained. The spirits seemed to **attend the occupied crypts**, not the scratched-out empty ones
- A skeletal spirit's hand **passed through Hildegarde's shoulder** — numb, freezing, **1 Wound lost**. She leapt back brushing at it and warned the others not to let the spirits pass through them
- Berin invoked a **Blessing of Righteousness**; the party pushed through and the spirits **faded** as they neared the sewer gate

### The sewer gate
- A gate **4½–5 feet tall**, with a bar and clasp. **Freshly oiled; rust knocked off the hinges; marks of use on both sides.** A perpendicular round tunnel beyond, with a slight downward slope and foul liquid **smeared to the left and right**, as if things had been dragged both ways
- Snorri: the lock is **not a dwarven lock** — it belongs to *"the people who control the catacombs"*. **There should be a dwarven security gate on the far side** so neither side can use the connection; **it has been dismantled and removed entirely, hinges wrenched apart**. *"Nobody should be down here. There hasn't been work done on this side in months"*
- The catacombs are **the old caves the "manlings" used**; Snorri had never entered them, and had **never seen a spirit before**. Some temples connect their catacombs to the sewers for drainage
- Snorri **picked the lock** without trouble and produced his **sewer map**: from this exit, the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]] is **half a mile or less** by the sewer branch. He would let the party **note that section** but not transcribe all of it
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] worried about the promise made to the groundskeeper to return within about an hour; the party decided to go on anyway, since the sewer trail was also what they had promised the dwarves

### The bodies
- In the **four-foot side tunnel** (single file, bent double), Hildegarde spotted the feet of **a linen-wrapped corpse**, dragged to one side of the channel. **A second lay beside it**, and on top of both, **a folded note under four old Ostland silver coins** ("regular Ostland" — not [[Beeckerhoven]] mints). Boris picked up the coins. *"Weird place for the Tooth Fairy to be operating."*
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] read the note aloud: **"Lurkers … more bodies by Festag. — A.B."** *(the count before "more" is unclear in the transcript)*. Boris's immediate guess: **Beham**. Hildegarde offered alternatives — payment to get someone out past the walls, or an advance for the next delivery in a couple of days — and pointed out that a single person dragging bodies one at a time is **probably coming back soon**, and so could be ambushed
- **The bodies:** dead **over a week, under two months**; **no visible cause of death** without unwrapping; **one over 70, one middle-aged**; both **well-cared-for, with good teeth**, buried **without clothes or jewellery** — *not* indigents
- **Hildegarde's tracking:** footprints go **both ways** — down empty, back up **dragging something**. At least one dragger is **barefoot**, **adult human**-sized (Boris: roughly a 42–44 EU foot). Hildegarde wondered about a **lost shoe** and whether the dragger had been chased. Later the GM clarified **two distinct bare feet** (Boris: *"are you saying there are bears now in the sewers?"*)
- Snorri: the dwarves **rerouted a mountain stream through the main channel** to keep it flowing into the river; these **side tunnels are a makeshift collector** built by the dwarves. **Downhill** is the main channel — running along the road past the Temple of Shallya toward the main bridge; **uphill** is a dead end of drains. Snorri: *"Aye, this must be the source — but who's moving them from here, and why, if there are still bodies here and we haven't seen any for several weeks?"*
- Hildegarde recalled that the [[Medical Union]] (per [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]) **only infects living subjects to multiply strains**, but also **extracts the dead of rare diseases** for the original source

### The cavern
- Uphill, the tunnel ended in **a rough three-foot hole** punched through the wall into **a wide natural cavern** — **stalactites and stalagmites, a ceiling from four feet to very high**, a smell of **rotten flesh** rather than sewage. Snorri: the catacombs were built into the earliest natural caverns found here; when one was breached, **the dwarves would wall it off**. The Temple of the All Gods is close to the city wall, so this may lie **under the wall itself**
- Inside the hole: **discarded bones, another wrapped corpse with its head-covering removed** (a bare skull), piles of other bones, and at least **one more dragged-in wrapped corpse**
- Order of march: **Boris** (lantern, sling in the other hand), then Berin, Snorri, Ganav, Hildegarde. **Perception (average) for Boris and Berin** spotted **a humanoid hanging from the ceiling — grey skin, red eyes, sharp teeth**

### The ghoul fight *(unfinished)*
- **Round 1:** Boris called a warning and shot the ghoul overhead. The GM applied a **+20 outnumbering** bonus. After a critical roll the GM called **one ghoul dead** — *"we will call him dead"* *(see Notes)*. **[[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] charged in** with his **hooked sewer spear** — a tool for clearing grates, not a weapon — and later **fumbled twice in a row, hitting himself**
- A **larger elder ghoul** — spikes, bones, skin — **ran the cavern walls, seized a stalactite, and swung down at Berin** with a **sharpened tibia**, then a bite to follow. The GM misapplied and undid the poison once; **Berin resisted the bite's paralysing venom** and later passed a **difficult Endurance test** against it. The **lesser ghouls carry a weaker poison**, which afflicted [[Ganav]]
- A second, **"drunk"/"insecure"** ghoul was **softened by Ganav** and **hit in the shoulder by Boris**, but survived a round on the brink
- **Berin, blessed by Myrmidia**, slammed his weapon into the elder's **secondary arm**, and later landed a **cutting critical to its left arm** — it **dropped one of its two tibias**
- **Hildegarde** joined with her mace (**Strike to Stun**; a 54, missed), tried to draw the elder's attention, and — when the GM refused a group scream as "a little OP" — was allowed **Singing vs. Endurance to scream into the elder's ear**, a **single-round stun**. A ghoul's critical attack on her cost her mail coat **1 point of armour damage ("critical deflection")**
- **Round 3:** **Boris's sling crit** to the elder's body ("aiming low") dropped it **prone**. **Berin, spending Advantage**, put a **sword thrust through its eye** — the GM: *"one to the donglers and one to the eye… he wishes he was dead."* A third player-side hit from **Hildegarde's mace — a crushing critical to the head** — **burst its remaining eye(s)**
- **End state:** the elder ghoul is **stunned ×3, blinded ×2, prone and bleeding — "nearly dead" — but not dead.** One of the other ghouls **"goes down"** to a late hit *(status unconfirmed)*; Ganav's last attack **missed**. **The GM will "mop up the battle at the beginning of next" session**
