# Session 41 — Too Warm for Me

## Summary
The party went down into the catacombs beneath the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] with [[Ewalt Wrangel]], who used a **spirit board** to commune with the stranded dead — the first to answer was a man named **Melchior Pellen**, buried less than three months ago, whose niche is **empty** — and then performed the **last rites** for the whole host, drawing them through into Mórr's realm. He told the party that the spirits had been **detached from their bodies** and left stranded in the hedge, and that **something deeper than greed** was behind it.

[[Hilde Muntz]] was pressed to turn witness against her old gang. She admitted she had **helped the Lurkers plan the lottery robbery** under a debt, and — in a private word with [[Hildegarde]] — named **[[Nina Trielffen|Nina]]** as the one who hid [[Gerd Breytenbach]] and described the burning of his master's household. At the temple gate the party found a **burning candle, a silver amethyst ring and a note signed "A.B."** — *"You've made this town too warm for me"* — and, under the ring, a **certificate from the Vermillion Pawn** in Middenheim. They left the ring as bait with [[Ganav]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] on watch, and took Hilde out in a funeral hearse. Two blocks on, a man in a black cloak stood in the road holding **[[Marla Hengist|Marla]]** at dagger-point and left with her. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and the procession carried on to the North Gate, where [[Christina Asper]] was waiting: *"I was starting to get worried. You were late."*

## Events

### Into the catacombs
- The party re-formed its marching order; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] collected the **caltrops** from the bridge and returned them to [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]
- Berin prayed to Myrmidia for guidance and was refused; he took it as a sign that **the servant of the Elder God should lead** and asked Wrangel to come up alongside. [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] fell back, calling directions from further and further behind
- Near the place where the spirits had swarmed the party in [[Session 38 - A Sacrifice I Am Willing to Make|Session 38]], the air went cold and the hair on the arm stood up

### The spirit board
- The first ghost appeared. Wrangel: *"This is what I always suspected about this place. Wait here."* He knelt, chalked a circle on a large black stone, inscribed **letters, numbers and the words "yes" and "no"**, and set a **glass wrapped in cloth** at its centre
- A ghost with a **complete body and clothing** rested its hand over the glass, which slid from letter to letter and spelled a name: **Melchior Pellen**. Wrangel: *"I performed the service myself… how can he still be here?"*
- **The niche was empty** — the body had been moved. Wrangel noted the catacomb had been **closed thirty years ago** because of *"a weakness"* between the hedge and the terrestrial plane; [[Azmus Beham]] **reopened it** on taking charge. The spirits were **detached but not moved on**, *"stranded in the hedge"*, which the Church of Mórr regards as **torture**
- He **questioned about a dozen spirits**. All had been interred in the **last three years**, during Azmus's keeping of the temple. Their memories only reached their last moments. He recognised almost all of the names

### The last rites
- Wrangel performed the **ritual of last rites**; the party repeated lines after him. Over **about twenty minutes** the spirits were drawn through invisible doors: some were met by a hand taking their forearm, one by a **flock of shadow ravens** that dragged a screaming zombified figure away. Roughly **a quarter went gladly, half tepidly, a quarter dragged unwilling**
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] scrubbed away the defaced marks with **charcoal** and redrew the **Myrmidian symbols** on the niches. The GM: the stone *remembers* what was inscribed
- Wrangel, soaked and shaking, asked the party **not to discuss the details**. On what else was done: *"there's something deeper here"* — **other magics, possibly necromancy**, to cut the spirits loose; he must consult his library. Ghouls, he added, are **not the work of necromancers** but of eating human flesh
- He pledged **support in bringing justice** to the Church of Mórr and the spirits

### Pressing Hilde Muntz
- Berin and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] pressed [[Hilde Muntz]] to turn witness. She: *"Don't blame me for their acts. I abandoned them some time ago"* — she joined the Church of Shallya **over a year ago**, but was **forced back** to work with the Lurkers on occasion
- A charm roll got her admission: she **helped plan the theft of the Steel Fists' lottery money**, as she knew the layout of their holdings, to **repay a long-standing debt**. That was her last involvement
- [[Hildegarde]], having missed the exchange in Session 40, **drew her aside** while walking (a challenging Charm, rerolled) and asked directly about [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]]. Hilde at first said she was *not* sheltering him: **[[Nina Trielffen|Nina]] brought him and hid him**
- **Gerd's story, as Hilde heard it in one afternoon's conversation:** his household was **sacked and burned**, the staff and master killed, **another body placed in his place**, and he believed *"they're looking for him in order to complete their objective of eliminating the household"*. He came back as they were burning the house
- Hilde on who: **not the Lurkers' style** — *"they're not into grand conspiracies and plots"* — and not the Steel Fists. She knew of no other group. **Nina is not gang-affiliated; she is a follower of Ranald**, a *"principled thief"*
- **The sign:** Hilde taught Hildegarde a **sequence of hand gestures** that tells Nina that Hilde is safe and the party can be trusted
- **The party's reading:** a far more **organised, professional** group is behind the burning of Gerd's household; the falsified notes tie the master's murder to [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]; [[Gerd Breytenbach|Gerd]] may have **seen the killers**. Berin: the priest's suspicion of **necromancy** is worth taking to Asper

### The temple gate
- The rest of the way was **dark and quiet**; no more ghosts. The **gate into the temple was ajar**, a **single candle** burning on a wooden side table near the entrance — a sign-in podium — with two papers and a ring. The temple's main door was shut; light fell through the dome windows. **Berin failed his Perception** (a Blessing of Fortune reroll failed too) but nothing happened
- **The note**, on stained parchment, hastily written: *"You've made this town too warm for me. Keep the ring. I have no further use for it. Enjoy your victory while it lasts. — A.B."* Wrangel: **the same hand** as the note found with the corpses in the sewers
- **The ring:** smallish, silver, *"somewhat crackled and worn"* and tarnished, with a **single amethyst** under a **grey cloud that shifts position** when you look away. Berin's Evaluate: **ten or twenty crowns**; not a fine piece
- **The second paper**, folded in quarters under the ring: a **certificate of authenticity** from the **Vermillion Pawn** in Middenheim, a **dwarf-run appraiser of rare and magical antiquities**. Berin: the ring was **assessed in Middenheim**, but that is *the provenance*, not the most recent owner
- The candle was **two and a half inches across**, burning **for several hours**, nearly spent, with spilled wax: someone expected a visitor within a window of time

### What the party made of it
- **Berin:** Stradovski fled Middenheim; the ring came from Middenheim; perhaps he **traded it to Azmus**, and the party got in the middle of the deal — *"we are the ones who have made this town too warm"*
- **Hildegarde:** the ring was **surrendered to someone else**, who forced Azmus to give it back — *"he wouldn't have given us the ring"*. Azmus gave up an operation of over a year *"without even trying to defend his position"*, which doesn't fit
- **Ganav:** set an **ambush** while the candle still burns; Boris agreed, preferring to control the place of a fight. **Ring back on the table** — *"we absolutely need the ring there to catch the person in the act"*. **Berin kept the certificate** to show [[Christina Asper|Asper]]
- **The split:** Ganav and Boris stay behind (Hildegarde lent Ganav her **loaded pistol crossbow**; the **caltrops** were to be set where a runner would cross); Berin, Hildegarde, Wrangel and Hilde take the hearse

### The funeral hearse
- Berin **climbed a plinth** to a window (an easy climb): a **narrow hearse**, a **jet-black horse** with a single **black feather**, a driver in the **tunic and pantaloons** of Mórr's temple, and **six mourners in black** — women with veils flipped up. A legitimate funeral procession, as Wrangel had arranged
- **The outer door** was not locked; a **board nailed over it** had to be wrenched off. A **perception test** outside: a **black cloak watching** from the direction of the gate **withdrew** when the party looked again
- The driver and mourners carried in a **light enamelled coffin**, lined with linen and padding. Wrangel and Berin dressed in **robes** from a box under the driver's seat; **Hilde climbed in reluctantly**, checking it had no latch. Berin sat up beside the driver

### The man with the girl
- **Two blocks on**, the road was cleared by the crowd except for **an older man in a black cloak with a salt-and-pepper beard**, holding **[[Marla Hengist|Marla]]** by the back of her collar, a **dagger loose in his hand** pointed at her side
- [[Hildegarde]] slipped off the procession to circle behind him through the alleys (a contested Stealth/Urban test); she **failed**, and then took a **Dark Deal** on a re-roll — her first — and **gained a Corruption point**. The GM: it did not help
- **Berin's denunciation** from the hearse (*"consorters with necromancers… the hand of Myrmidia will fall upon you"*) was ruled an **Intimidate** — about *making* someone do what you want, not calling him out; the man replied *"Keep it up if you want this girl to die"* and **backed down a side passage with Marla**, *"manhandling"* her
- **Hilde stirred in the coffin** (*"what's going on?"*); Berin talked her into staying with the plan, **Asper's plan**, to rescue those her gang had endangered once outside the city
- **Hildegarde shadowed him** on a challenging Stealth/Urban (the GM allowed it, with a penalty for the hostage). The shadowing roll **worked**; **she is on his trail** as the session ends

### Through the North Gate
- **The guards saluted** and cleared the road; the procession passed out of the **North Gate**
- **Asper** emerged from behind one of the first outbuildings and rode **behind Berin, ahead of the hearse**: *"I was starting to get worried. You were late. Everything go okay?"*
- **The destination changed:** not the inn where [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] waits, but a **northern burial site**, far enough that the Lurkers could not reach her. Berin will spend a day there, then return (**his player is away next session**)
