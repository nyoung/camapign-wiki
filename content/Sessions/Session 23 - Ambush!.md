# Session 23 — Ambush!

**Played:** 23 May 2026
**In-game date:** 7–8 Jahrdrung 2512

## Summary

The caravan's first day out of Ferlangen was shadowed by a persistent unease — shapes at the forest's edge, glimpses of movement that never resolved into anything concrete. By nightfall the group arrived at [[Aukrug]], a tiny palisaded village centred on a rough coaching inn called [[The Dangling Mutant]], its sign depicting a hanged mutant that [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] cheerfully reinterpreted as "The Dangling Appendages." Inside: broken furniture, years of trampled nutshells, rotten orc bitters, miners packed alongside bar girls, and a pit fighter named [[Igor]] pummelling all challengers in a sunken ring. [[Danderich Flisshausten]] purchased his room with cutting commentary. [[Hildegarde]] tried to draw out the innkeeper with a counterfeit coin question and got only narrowed eyes. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] slipped out to the stables and learned the Hofbauer Bodelstein caravan had passed through one day ahead. The inn fell quiet well past midnight.

The following morning, the miners trooped out before the party had broken camp. Back on the road, [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] grew uneasy: the route had been cut through hills, leaving steep embankments on either side — perfect ground for an ambush. He rode forward and warned [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] directly. Danderich listened with visible impatience and dismissed the warning, declaring that no one would dare attack a well-armoured column. Almost before the words left his mouth, a hail of black arrows screamed down from the heights.

The first volley tore into the lead coach's horses. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] fought to control his own mount and eventually dismounted, handing the reins to [[Hoffman Jannitzer|Hoffman]]. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] leapt from the wagon and bellowed about cannons, black powder, and master gunners — a magnificent bluff with no cannon behind it. [[Mangold]] erupted from the wagon and charged straight for the cliff base. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] urged [[Orazio]] up the slope but the horse grew reluctant, sensing something in the treeline. The second volley was worse — more horses hit, one taking a devastating wound near the eye. [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] called out desperately for guidance. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] reached for his new longbow and promptly emptied his entire quiver onto the road, spending frantic seconds on his knees recovering arrows.

Then the **Howling Ones** materialised from the woods on the opposite side of the road and turned their bows on [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]. An arrow drove straight through his mouth — cracking teeth, lacerating his tongue, punching out through the angle of his jaw in a spray of blood. He kept moving. A final precise shot severed the harness connecting the lead coach's horses to the vehicle. The horses bolted left; [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]]'s coach careened out of control and slammed into a stone wall, rolling onto its side. [[Mangold]] sprinted back and heaved it upright alone. [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]] rolled clear, shaken but unhurt. [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] was pulled from the wreck with a painful hip contusion, cursing in Gnomish.

The tide turned. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] drove [[Orazio]] through the brush and cut down a fleeing bandit. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], arrows recovered, put a devastating shot into another's head. [[Hildegarde]] wrestled with a jammed crossbow mechanism, forcing a field repair. The remaining bandits melted back into the forest. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] ran down the last standing man and knocked him unconscious with flat blade and pommel — a prisoner taken alive. He tended the wounded horses and said a quiet prayer to Myrmidia, noting her holy day of the spear was nearly upon them. The party began to reckon with what the road still held.

## Events

### Day One — A Feeling of Being Watched
- Persistent unease from first hours out of Ferlangen — shapes at the forest's edge, movement never materialising
- Caravan travelled without incident through the morning and afternoon

### Arrival at Aukrug — The Dangling Mutant
- Arrived by nightfall at [[Aukrug]]: small village, working wooden palisade, rough coaching inn
- Inn sign: hanged mutant; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] declared it "The Dangling Appendages"
- Inside: years of trampled nutshells, broken furniture, rotten orc bitters, miners and bar girls
- Pit fighter [[Igor]] in a sunken ring, taunting and near-fatally pummelling all challengers; bets flying everywhere
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] quietly reminded [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] to say nothing about the local beverages
- [[Danderich Flisshausten]] purchased his room and meal with cutting commentary about everything
- [[Hildegarde]] showed the innkeeper a suspicious coin and asked about counterfeits — he claimed ignorance
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] visited the stables: stable hands confirmed **Hofbauer Bodelstein caravan had passed through one day prior**
- Witch hunters and Sigmarite knights kept entirely to themselves; two factions barely tolerating proximity
- Inn loud with pit fight bets until well past midnight

### Departure and Warning Ignored
- Miners gone before the party finished breaking camp
- Caravan set out; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]]'s eye quickly troubled by the terrain: road cut through hills with steep embankments on both sides — **perfect ambush ground**
- He rode forward and warned [[Danderich Flisshausten|Danderich]] directly, citing bandit reports gathered north of Ferlangen
- Danderich dismissed the warning: *no one would dare attack a well-armoured column*
- A hail of **black arrows** came screaming down from the heights above almost immediately

### The Ambush — First Volleys
- First volley: lead coach's draft horses torn into; animals screamed and lurched
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] fought to keep his mount, eventually dismounted and handed reins to [[Hoffman Jannitzer|Hoffman]]
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] leapt from the wagon bed and bellowed about **cannons, black powder batteries, and master gunners** — delivered with total conviction; no cannon existed
- [[Mangold]] charged headlong toward the cliff base, axe at belt, murder in his eyes
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] attempted to ride [[Orazio]] up the rocky slope; [[Orazio]] grew increasingly reluctant, sensing something hidden in the treeline
- Second volley: more horses struck; one took a devastating wound near the eye
- [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] called out desperately for someone to tell him what to do
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] reached for his new longbow and fumbled — entire quiver cascaded onto the road; spent frantic moments recovering arrows while the battle raged

### The Howling Ones Attack the Other Side
- A second group — **the Howling Ones** — materialised from the woods on the **opposite** side of the road
- Turned their bows directly on [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]
- One found his mark: **an arrow drove straight through Pilpoul's mouth** — cracking teeth, lacerating his tongue, exiting through the angle of his jaw in a spray of blood
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], by sheer force of will, kept moving

### The Coach Wreck
- A single precise arrow severed the **harness** connecting the lead coach horses to the vehicle
- Horses bolted left; the coach careened out of control and **slammed into a stone wall**, rolling onto its side
- [[Mangold]] heard the wreck, abandoned his cliff charge, and sprinted back
- [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]] rolled clear — shaken but alive
- [[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] pulled from wreckage: **painful hip contusion**, cursing in Gnomish with considerable creativity
- [[Mangold]] heaved the overturned coach back onto its wheels alone, with the casual strength of a dwarf who had seen worse

### The Tide Turns
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] drove [[Orazio]] through brush and **cut down a fleeing bandit** with a single stroke
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], arrows finally recovered, put a **devastating shot into another attacker's head**
- [[Hildegarde]]'s heavy crossbow **jammed** at the worst possible moment; she forced a field repair before bringing it to bear
- Remaining bandits melted back into the dense forest
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] ran down the last standing man and knocked him unconscious with flat blade then pommel — **one prisoner taken alive**

### Aftermath

- Said a quiet prayer to **Myrmidia**, noting her holy day of the spear was nearly upon them
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]], face a ruin of blood and broken teeth, was helped down from the embankment
