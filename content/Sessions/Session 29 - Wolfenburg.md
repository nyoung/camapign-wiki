# Session 29 — Wolfenburg

**Played:** 5 July 2026
**In-game date:** 12 Jahrdrung 2512 (morning) – 13 Jahrdrung 2512 (evening)

## Summary

The party rose at sunrise in [[Dassel]] as the rain finally broke. **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] paid out the escort contract in full — five gold crowns** — completing the job that began in [[Session 21 - The Gnome and the Slayer|Session 21]]. The road improved with every mile toward [[Wolfenburg]], farmland thickening around them, until the city itself came into view from a ridge: a great walled provincial capital astride a wide river, dominated by a Sigmarite cathedral. The gates were already shut for the night, so the party settled at a fortified outpost inn a mile outside the walls.

That evening's rumor-gathering turned up a string of leads: a deserting crown prince, a doctor murdered by a rival physician, an expelled cell of witch hunters — and confirmation from a caravan driver that **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** had entered the city with the caravan boss she'd been travelling with. The next morning the party entered [[Wolfenburg]] proper, [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] talking his way into the **city guest register** under cover of a toll dispute and spotting Elfrieda's name — with no sign of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]. A mob of street children, a town crier's job posting for a **Dr. Hubertus von Bora**, and a reunion with [[Christina Asper]] and [[Lucas Pollack|Pollack]] followed before the party split up: [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] left for care at the **Temple of Verena**, and **[[Ganav]]** was turned over to the city courts to await a hearing for his time with the [[Howling Ones]].

The day's real payoff came at the **[[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]**, where the party finally met **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]]** (the contact named to them all the way back in [[Session 20 - Never Mention Prostitutes|Session 20]]) and laid out everything they'd learned about Stradovski. She responded with three troubling threads: children under seven are being **collected from local farms and vanishing**, with the law powerless to intervene; the Physicians Guild's secretary **[[Jaie Schraeder]]** was recently **murdered** and his home burned to hide evidence; and victims of rare wasting diseases have been arriving in the city almost exclusively through the **eastern gate**. She wrote the party a letter of introduction to **Dr. Hubertus von Bora**, named three physicians friendly to the temple, and sent them off with protective talismans — the threads of Stradovski's trail pulling tighter with every step deeper into the city.

## Events

### The Road to Wolfenburg
- The party gathers at sunrise in [[Dassel]] as the rain breaks; **[[Gromril van Aelst|Gromril]] pays five gold crowns**, fulfilling the escort contract
- The trail improves dramatically — stone bridges, cobbled stretches, intensely farmed Ostland countryside
- From a ridge, [[Wolfenburg]] comes into view: a walled provincial capital on a wide river, dominated by a Sigmarite cathedral
- The city gates are already closed for the night; the party settles at a fortified outpost inn a mile outside the walls

### Rumors at the Outpost Inn
- [[Hildegarde]] gathers rumors: a deserting crown prince, a doctor murdered by a rival physician who burned down his house, an Ostland witch hunter cell expelled for attacking a Templars of Sigmar village
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] confirms hunters have abandoned the road to [[Ferlangen]] as too dangerous
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] questions caravan drivers about the Hofbauer Bodelstein expedition; learns its leaders (and **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]**) entered the city before the gates closed, with no sign of a doctor traveling among them

### The Gates of Wolfenburg
- The party leaves [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] and the wagon at the outpost inn and walks to the gates
- Debates how to smuggle Stradovski's forbidden plague book past inspection; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] agrees to carry it
- An excise official demands a gold crown per visitor and dismisses [[Hildegarde]]'s Temple of Verena warrant
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] uses his knightly status to insist on signing the **guest register** — and secretly spots **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]**'s name; no sign of Stradovski
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] draws the attention of a suspicious hawkish man while fumbling for coins; the man melts away
- A mob of street children swarms the party inside the gates; [[Hildegarde]] scares them off by lowering her mask and screaming — earning "the Banshee of the Northern Gate"

### Splitting Up
- The party reunites with **[[Christina Asper]]** and **[[Lucas Pollack]]** inside the walls; hears the town crier announce a **Dr. Hubertus von Bora** seeking secretarial guards at the House of Magdalena Amman
- [[Pollack]] guides the party through the temple district to the **Temple of Verena**, where **[[Pilpoul Ivri]]** is left for care
- **[[Ganav]]** is turned over to the court's bailiff to await a preliminary hearing
- A temple clerk confirms the party's next payday is the 18th in Altdorf, and that **[[Augustus Steiner]]'s trial has been delayed at least another month**
- The clerk mentions rumors of disturbed corpses in the catacombs beneath the **Temple of the All Gods**, and directs the party to the Physicians Guild

### The Temple of Shallya
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] bribes a pair of street children with brass pennies to watch for **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]**, a woman in a red cloak
- The party reaches the **Temple of Shallya**, meets **[[Gammer Isolde Ulricht]]** in a private back room, and lays out the full Stradovski case
- Isolde shares three troubling threads: children under seven are being **collected from farms and vanishing**; Physicians Guild secretary **[[Jaie Schraeder]]** was recently **murdered**, his home burned; rare wasting-disease victims are arriving almost exclusively through the **eastern gate**
- She writes a letter of introduction to **Dr. Hubertus von Bora**, names three physicians friendly to the temple (Israhel Valera, Hartmut Imhot, Mihai Buchner), and provides protective talismans
