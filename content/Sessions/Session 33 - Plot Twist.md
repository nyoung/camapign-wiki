# Session 33 — Plot Twist

## Summary
The party returned to the **[[Garden of Mórr]]** to press **[[Ewalt Wrangel]]** on the Schraeder fire victims, and confirmed a troubling inconsistency: the body attributed to butler **[[Gerd Breytenbach]]** was older, malnourished, and missing teeth — nothing like the well-kept servant description — and its wounds didn't match the precise, professional kills on **[[Louisa May]]** and **[[Jaie Schraeder]]** themselves, raising the possibility the body was a planted assassin rather than Breytenbach. Wrangel also confirmed the [[Session 30 - Hanging Judge|Session 30]] rumour: bodies have been going missing from the **[[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]]**, and named its proprietor, **[[Azmus Beham]]**, as worth speaking to. After collecting wages at the **[[Temple of Verena (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Verena]]** — where Berin's library research turned up a Kislevite nature-spirit symbol matching markings from Schraeder's house — the party visited the Temple of the All Gods under cover of scholarly interest. There Berin spotted a freshly painted, heretical shrine to a twisted, androgynous Shallya — nearly identical to one Hildegarde recalled from **[[Professor Udo Stradovski]]**'s private office. Beham showed them the catacombs below, where a heavily used iron gate led to a tunnel smelling of river water, running roughly beneath a major city road — but the moment Hildegarde asked about the missing bodies, Beham's cheer evaporated into open fear and he hustled the party out. A follow-up visit to the **[[Physicians' Guild (Wolfenburg)|Physicians Guild]]** and the surrounding neighbourhood confirmed it: clerk **[[Danel Koberger]]** described Breytenbach as tall, healthy, and sharp-toothed, and the local bookseller confirmed he'd stopped exchanging books two weeks ago — exactly when the townhouse burned. Breytenbach is almost certainly alive, and the trail now points to **[[Anselm Geissmar]]**'s manservant, his one known close friend.

- Approached the [[Temple of the All Gods (Wolfenburg)|Temple of the All Gods]] under cover of scholarly curiosity via their connection to **Dr. von Bora**
- Berin spotted a freshly painted (less than a month old) shrine depicting an androgynous, twisted Shallya — Hildegarde recognized it as nearly identical to a shrine she recalled from [[Professor Udo Stradovski]]'s private office

- Beham led the party into the catacombs below the temple, older than the temple itself; Berin noted Sigmar and Shallya markers deliberately scratched out on unused burial slots, and a heavy iron gate with freshly oiled hinges and a worn scrape mark, beyond which a tunnel ran toward the river — Berin estimated it runs roughly north–south beneath a major city road
- The moment Hildegarde asked about the missing-bodies rumour, Beham's demeanor cracked — Boris caught genuine fear, not embarrassment — and Beham hustled the party out, locking the gate and hurrying off
- Visited the [[Physicians' Guild (Wolfenburg)|Physicians Guild]] and spoke with clerk **[[Danel Koberger]]**, who described Breytenbach as tall, healthy, well-toothed, close to six feet — flatly contradicting the Garden of Mórr corpse
- Canvassed the neighbourhood around Schraeder's townhouse; learned Breytenbach was a quiet, well-read man who traded books twice weekly at a local bookstore and had one close friend: the manservant of merchant **[[Anselm Geissmar]]**
- At the bookstore, confirmed Breytenbach hadn't returned to trade a book in two weeks — timing that lines up exactly with the townhouse fire; Berin bought a cheap medical treatise as cover for a future orphanage visit, Ganav bought a wound-treatment text as a gift
