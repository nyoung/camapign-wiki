# Session 22 — Meddling Kids

**Played:** 16 May 2026
**In-game date:** 6–7 Jahrdrung 2512

## Summary

With the Bear Claws thugs bound and at their mercy in the alleyway, the party turned their attention to extracting what they could. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] led the interrogation with sweetcakes, beer, and philosophical musings while [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] audibly wished for simpler methods. The thugs confirmed they were Bear Claws — identifiable by bear-paw tattoos on forearms and hands — hired for several dozen crowns to steal [[Gromril van Aelst]]'s wallet by a balding, dark-haired bearded man who oversaw the watchmen around the [[Illium Warehouse]]. [[Hildegarde]] pressed them on [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] and [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] but drew blanks. A city watchman arrived, took the prisoners, and walked off with [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s rope and manacles without apology or reward.

The party split up. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] rode hard to the [[Fist of Amber]] to find [[Marretta Schongauer]]; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] went to audit the Merchants Guild; [[Hildegarde]] investigated [[Klaus von Kolditz]]'s warehouses and questioned the city gates about missing girls. Boris and Berin arrived at the Fist of Amber at dusk and spent the evening with Marretta, who confirmed the Oeslander refugees were safe, named the **Howling Ones** as a multi-year threat with **werewolves among their number**, confirmed von Kolditz's deep involvement in human trafficking, and drafted a letter of endorsement for [[Karl Helpmann]]'s peddler store. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] paid for dinner.

In [[Ferlangen]], [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s attempt at charm and then bribery at the Merchants Guild collapsed spectacularly, ending with an irate clerk and a theatrical exit. [[Hildegarde]]'s reconnaissance of von Kolditz's warehouses found eight to ten private watchmen on patrol but genuinely ignorant workers — the dirty work is clearly outsourced. Her attempts at the city gates to gather intelligence on the missing girls came to nothing.

That evening, [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] and [[Hildegarde]] were accosted by [[Danderich Flisshausten]], who jabbed a finger at [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] and accused the party of responsibility for Heinz Benz's death and for abandoning [[The Forest Inn]] after the battle with [[Melmoth]]. [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] questioned whether the ghouls had personally named him; [[Hildegarde]] argued that the party had purged Melmoth's evil, turned his weapons over to witch hunters, and reported the danger to [[Skjaldberg]]'s garrison. Danderich refused to hear any of it and stormed off promising they would pay.

The following morning, [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] returned with considerable satisfaction, noting that the two socially awkward party members had gathered intelligence while the two charming ones had struck out. At [[Karl Helpmann's Peddler Store]], Marretta's letter of endorsement produced useful news: the runaway serfs had already found passage south with the **Hofbauer Bodelstein** trading company — but Helpmann had glimpsed a secretive dark-haired woman and a large pit-fighter type being quietly smuggled aboard one of their wagons. [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] and her companion had left the same day the party arrived in Ferlangen, heading south toward [[Wolfenburg]] via [[Dassel]].

At the south gate, a substantial caravan assembled: [[Gromril van Aelst]] and [[Mangold]] with their coach driven by [[Lefty Longshanks|Lefty]]; [[Christina Asper]], [[Hoffman Jannitzer]], and [[Lucas Pollack]] (now in battered full plate); and [[Danderich Flisshausten]] with his Sigmarite knights, who made it wordlessly clear they intended to travel the same road. Danderich claimed the vanguard position without asking. The caravan rolled south toward [[Wolfenburg]], the Howling Ones somewhere ahead, the Inquisition riding uncomfortably close behind.

## Events

### Alley Interrogation — Bear Claws Identified
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] led the interrogation: sweetcakes, beer, and philosophical reflection
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] sighed and expressed his preference for more direct methods
- Thugs confirmed: members of the **Bear Claws** gang — identifiable by bear-paw tattoos on forearms and hands
- Hired for **several dozen crowns** to steal [[Gromril van Aelst]]'s wallet specifically
- Their contact: a **balding, dark-haired man with a beard** who oversaw watchmen around the [[Illium Warehouse]]
- No knowledge of [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] or [[Elfrieda Teuschel]]
- A city watchman arrived, took the prisoners, and departed with [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s rope and manacles — no reward for street thugs

- **[[Hildegarde]]** → [[Klaus von Kolditz]]'s warehouses; questions city gates about missing girls

### Boris and Berin at the Fist of Amber
- Rode hard through the fading afternoon, arrived just after sunset; guard grumbled about late riding
- Found [[Marretta Schongauer]] at her usual table, pipe in hand
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] bought a round of fine wine
- Marretta confirmed the two Oeslander refugees had been safely delivered south
- **The Howling Ones:** a multi-year threat on these roads; glanced around before leaning in to add that the cult includes **werewolves who use transformation as a weapon**; [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] immediately reached for salt to throw over his shoulder
- **[[Klaus von Kolditz]]:** deeply involved in human trafficking, moving people in and out of [[Kislev]]; systematically cutting off Marretta's trade contacts
- Named **Fugger** and **Teuschel** as smaller rivals
- Drafted a **letter of endorsement** for the party by candlelight, stamped and handed it over
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] paid for dinner for all three; they stayed the night

### Pilpoul at the Merchants Guild — Double Failure
- Arrived with confidence; charm attempt on the clerk to access financial ledgers failed immediately
- Subsequent bribery attempt made matters worse — left an irate clerk
- Dramatic exit, lamenting his mother's low expectations to bystanders
- No intelligence gained

### Hildegarde at Von Kolditz's Warehouses
- Observed **eight to ten private watchmen** patrolling the storefronts
- Questioned workers posing as a bounty hunter hired by local businesses
- Workers were **genuinely ignorant** — whatever dirty work von Kolditz runs is carefully outsourced, kept separate from his antique trade
- Attempts at the city gates to gather intelligence on missing girls: strangers unwilling to talk

### Danderich Confronts Pilpoul and Hildegarde
- [[Danderich Flisshausten]] stepped into their path and jabbed a finger at [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s chest
- Accused the party of responsibility for **Heinz Benz's death** and of abandoning [[The Forest Inn]] undefended
- Evidence cited: innkeeper [[Van Moll]]'s note and the inn's own registry, bearing the party's signatures as last guests
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]]'s response: questioned whether the ghouls personally named him when calling for brains
- [[Hildegarde]] argued the party had purged Melmoth's evil, turned his weapons over to witch hunters, and reported the danger to [[Skjaldberg]]
- Danderich refused to hear it, pointed at both and declared **they would pay**, then stormed off toward the church

### The Morning Reckoning
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] returned and heard the full account over breakfast
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] noted with satisfaction that the socially awkward party members had succeeded in gathering intelligence while the charming ones had struck out

### Karl Helpmann's Store — Elfrieda's Trail
- Marretta's letter of endorsement presented; Helpmann confirmed the runaway serfs had already found passage south with the **Hofbauer Bodelstein** trading company
- But paused: he had glimpsed a **secretive dark-haired woman** and a **large man with a broken nose** (the pit-fighter sort) being quietly smuggled aboard one of their wagons
- Description matched **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]]** and her companion
- They had left **the same day the party arrived** in Ferlangen, heading south via [[Dassel]] toward [[Wolfenburg]]

### Caravan Assembles — Departure
