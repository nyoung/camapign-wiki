# Session 36 — Most Discreet Ambush Ever

## Summary
The sundown clock from [[Session 35 - The Heist of the Heist|Session 35]] ran out, and the party spent most of it arguing. With [[Elfrieda Teuschel]] due to board a barge at the far north docks before the river nets went up, the group debated the shape of their ambush at length — [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] pushing a clean, brutal answer of oil and caltrops in a narrow alleyway, [[Hildegarde]] sketching far more elaborate schemes involving coaches, bags of flour and bellows to raise a blinding cloud of dust. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] quietly produced a card no one knew he was holding: **a potent drugging substance he had been carrying for many nights**, usable if they could get close enough to lace Teuschel's wine.

What they settled on was modest by comparison — scout the route from **[[Megrita's Anchor Boarding House]]** down to the docks, buy **manacles and caltrops** on the way, and pick an ambush site among the quayside warehouses. They also agreed what to do with her afterwards: interrogate her themselves first, then hand her over to the witch hunter **[[Christina Asper]]**.

The plan did not survive contact. [[Hildegarde]] put on **[[Melmoth's Mask|the mask taken from the dark priest Melmoth]]** to hide her scars and her face, and tried to melt into the riverbank crowds to shadow Teuschel and her bodyguard. She was made almost immediately, and a careful stalk turned into a flat sprint through the docklands to keep the pair in sight. [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] broke for high ground near the bridge to open a line of sight for his longbow; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] drove hard for the grey house at the edge of the quay and struggled to keep up as Teuschel's group made for the water.

The docks dissolved into a brawl. Hildegarde threw herself down a flight of stairs to reach the lower quay and only just avoided breaking herself on the landing. Berin was intercepted by a thug whose blow rang harmlessly off the **breastplate hidden beneath his priestly robes**. Dockworkers and hired muscle closed in from all sides, and Hildegarde was caught in a crushing headlock by a brute she could not shift — almost certainly **[[Ansgar Knochenbrecher]]**, Teuschel's bodyguard, engaged directly by the party for the first time in the entire chase. She hurled her steel-capped hat into another thug's face to buy an opening and wrenched herself loose by sheer stubbornness. Berin, meanwhile, put a man's leg out from under him and left him crumpled in a spreading pool of blood, then lifted his hem and picked his way delicately around the gore, complaining that bloodstains never come out of white vestments.

With the boat already pulling off the dock, **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] dropped his axe, drew his longbow, and put an arrow across thirty-six yards of open water into one of the boatmen** — the vessel slewed and rotated away from its moorings. [[Hildegarde]], who had got aboard, swung her mace at [[Elfrieda Teuschel|Teuschel]]'s head in a last attempt to drop her before the river took her. **Teuschel defended herself.** And then the day got worse: **some of the allies the party had been counting on turned on them.** The session ended with the party split between the dock and a drifting boat, battered, outnumbered, and their quarry still standing.
