# Session 39 — Vacate It for a Week, or Vacate It Permanently

## Summary
The ghoul fight in [[The Ghoul Cavern]] was finished. [[Ganav]] burned his last point of Resolve to throw off the ghoul poison and killed a lesser ghoul with a critical to its leg; [[Hildegarde]] finished the **elder ghoul** with a coup de grace from her mace; [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] and [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] crippled a third; and [[Snorri Belgrimson]] killed the last, hooking its leg and driving his sewer spear through its throat.

Searching the cavern, the party found burial wrappings, slips of paper like those on the tunnel bodies, **40 shillings** scattered among the bones, and a body whose ribcage had been opened and eaten. In a corner lay **two dead [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]** in their black hooded cloaks, with swords, and a **dwarf key**, which Snorri declared a skilled **[[Forged Dwarf Key|forgery]]** of the guild's sewer key before snapping it in fury. The cavern proved a dead end with a **ghoul nest** of old bones. The party took the two Lurker cloaks as disguises and a strip of burial cloth as evidence, and talked about taking their case against [[Azmus Beham]] to the [[Church of Morr|priests of Mórr]].

Snorri refused to go back past the catacomb spirits and led the party through the **main sewers** instead, past colonies of **[[Screaming Mushrooms|walking, screaming mushrooms]]** that act as an alarm and showed nobody had passed that way in a week or more. He took them to a **20-inch ceramic laundry drain** rising into the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]; Ganav climbed it, marked the liftable grate, and wedged it slightly open. Following fresh boot prints to a major **sewer junction**, the party ran into a group of Lurkers. A prisoner ruse with Berin in unlocked manacles went wrong when **Myrmidia rejected Berin's blessing**, but Ganav's blunt threat worked: **the Lurkers agreed to abandon the junction for a week.**

Snorri let the party out through a **hidden street door** between two businesses, explained that the only sewer route out of the city is a heavy stream grate the dwarves are unlikely to open for "manlings", and went home wounded. Back at the [[Tree and Rut Inn]], the common room was full of news that **Bögenhafen, in the Reikland, has been declared off limits** after some disaster. The party now has **half a plan** for [[Hilde Muntz]]: out through the laundry drain, through the sewers, and away by boat from the [[Wolfenburg Docks|docks]].

## Events

### Finishing the ghoul fight
- At the start of the session: the **elder ghoul** was down — *"any further attacks will kill him"* — with **three other ghouls** still up. [[Ganav]] was poisoned (6 of 17 Wounds, then 5); [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] was on **1 Wound**. [[Snorri Belgrimson|Snorri]] was also poisoned
- Ganav failed a poisoned Endurance test at −10 (the poison stacks penalties on its own resistance roll). Parrying with his sword instead of dodging, at −30, he was hit again
- **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]**, *"outraged by the ghoul attacking his social acquaintance"*, put a sling stone into the ghoul savaging Ganav — a big hit (*"Boris is a machine"*)
- **Berin** turned from the downed elder to defend [[Hildegarde]] from the **"grieving sewer ghoul"** attacking her. Hildegarde's player was absent; **EndyShrike ran her** — she dodged a ghoul's slash at her arm on a reroll after a 99
- **Hildegarde killed the elder ghoul**: the GM allowed a **free coup de grace** with her mace, and she used her Advantage to attack again
- **Ganav spent his last Resolve** to remove the Poisoned condition (the GM confirmed Resolve can clear poison and bleeding), then struck at −20 at the damaged ghoul in reach: a **critical to the right leg** that cracked the shin in half. The GM: *"For the purposes of one of these lesser ghouls, we'll call it dead. **Ganav kills the ghoul.**"*
- **Boris**, using *Fast Shot*, landed a **critical to a ghoul's left leg** as its defence fumbled
- **Berin**, with an outnumbering bonus, attacked the **"cruel" ghoul** hopping on one leg; the GM: *"the cruel ghoul is not going to be doing much more"*
- **Snorri killed the last ghoul**, the stunned one in front of Hildegarde: he hooked its leg with his short spear, tripped it, and drove the blade through its throat, spraying blood across Hildegarde

### The ghoul nest
- Among the bones: **burial wrappings**, **silver coins**, and **little slips of paper like the ones on the bodies in the tunnel**. The dead were **buried without clothing, jewellery or belongings**; only the odd **gold tooth** or healed break could identify anyone. A few are **still wrapped**. One body's robes had been torn and **its ribcage spread open**, and its contents were being eaten
- Berin healed himself; **Ganav healed Boris, then himself** (only one healing per person after an encounter)
- Berin's **Lore (Medicine)** check found **no common thread**: the bodies are a hodgepodge
- Boris gathered **10–12 sling stones** from the cavern
- **Two dead Lurkers.** In a corner lay **the remains of two dressed men** whose torn clothing included **the black hooded cloaks of the [[Black Cloak Lurkers Below|Lurkers]]**, each with **a sword**
- **The [[Forged Dwarf Key]].** On them, a **dwarf key**, with two paddles either side of the stem punched with shaped holes (square, circle) at twisted angles. Snorri: *"A forgery. Somebody's been copying their key. This takes some great skill. It has to be extremely accurate to work… This isn't the one that we made."* He **twisted and snapped it**: *"This is manling trash. We need to find these fiends and take our key back from them."* **Boris kept the broken pieces** as evidence for the dwarves
- **40 shillings** among the bones (the GM rolled 8d10 and got exactly 40), **split eight each** among the four PCs and Snorri. Mostly **local Ostland and Imperial mint**, about one in ten from other provinces, **none from [[Beeckerhoven]]**. The ghouls had no interest in coins; they came with the bodies
- **Gold teeth** were judged not worth harvesting
- **Round the bend: a dead end.** The only way in is the way the party came. The largest heap of old bones has been built into **a nest for the ghouls**
- The party **salvaged two Lurker cloaks** in good condition, worn now by **Ganav** and **Hildegarde** as a disguise; Boris **cut a strip of burial wrapping** as evidence
- Hildegarde's **Tracking** found only the party's and the ghouls' prints
- Berin **prayed over the disturbed dead**: *"Lady of the Ages, receive to thy halls those who lie here… May Mórr unbar the silent gate."*

### What to do about Beham
- The party is sure it has uncovered a scheme to dispose of bodies rather than keep them in the catacombs, but **[[Azmus Beham]] is a powerful man** and the party has run afoul of the law before
- Berin suggested going to the **priests of Mórr** at the [[Garden of Mórr]], who could lodge the accusation in a way the courts would respect. **No decision yet**
- Berin, out of character: Beham locked them in, so he'll expect them either dead or knowing, and the party needs **a different way out** than the haunted catacombs

### The screaming mushrooms
- **Snorri would not go back past the spirits.** He led the party back down the side tunnel, past the two corpses, and on toward the [[Temple of Shallya (Wolfenburg)|Temple of Shallya]]
- The side tunnel opened into **a main sewer**: an **arched vault** of dwarf height (5′5″–6′, so taller humans brush the walls) with **yard-wide walkways** either side of a **6–8-foot channel** of fast black water. Detritus on the walkways suggests it has overflowed before
- **[[Screaming Mushrooms]]:** red-and-white caps **3–4 inches tall** that **squeal**, sprout tendril legs, and **skitter away from the light**. One fell into the channel; another fell over and couldn't right itself. **Boris kicked one** screaming across the channel; **Ganav** called it a bad omen to hurt lesser beings. **Snorri stomped and sliced** every one that came near
- **Snorri blamed goblins** (*"gobbos"*): *"Only a messed-up gobbo on some kind of mushroom hallucinogen would decide to bring fungus to life."* Goblin bands have occasionally got in, never a full infestation. The mushrooms **alert anyone to the presence of others**
- By their size they've grown **at least a week, maybe a fortnight** undisturbed. **Nobody has come this way recently**, unless they found a way past without setting them off
- Snorri: the jacks have been **working a collapse on the other side of town** and have neglected this side, which had been **running without incident for months**. Boris, whispering: *"typical union excuses"*
- **Berin's read:** if Beham is paying the Lurkers to collect bodies, **nobody has been collecting them**. Something has happened to the Lurkers

### The laundry drain
- Down a **twisty side passage**, Snorri pointed out **a ceramic pipe about 20 inches across**, angled steeply up, carrying **soapy lye water**: *"the entrance to the Shallyan Temple"*
- About **20 feet up**, a **round floor grate** with dim candle- or lamplight beyond. The room above smells of **laundry**
- **Ganav climbed it** (challenging **Cool** for the claustrophobia, then average **Climb**, made by one). The pipe keeps its 20-inch diameter all the way; at the top it turns **vertical** to a **riveted metal crosshatch grate** set in the floor
- A **woman** was moving about above, **humming softly**. Ganav waited until she and her light had gone, then **chalked marks around the rim** so they blend with the tile. The grate **rocks in its seat like a manhole cover and can be lifted**. He **wedged a piece of metal** under the lip so it sits slightly raised and will be easier to lift from below
- He made a controlled descent onto the patch Boris had **cleared of muck**
- Party thinking: this is an awkward way *in* but a workable way *out*. Ganav suggested **pre-rigging ropes** (cut behind them to stop pursuit). Berin noted **his breastplate won't fit**; the party may need to go in through the temple's front door (Boris suggested disguise as religious) and leave through the drain

### Standoff at the junction
- Snorri's route led on to **another main channel**. Here Hildegarde spotted **muddy boot prints**, **recent** (*"within the last several hours, if not minutes ago"*), heading toward a junction with an exit **halfway between the Temple District and the waterfront**. No signs of anything dragged or rolled. **No mushrooms** in this western tunnel
- The vault has **holes up to the street drains** at intervals, but **no ladders**; they can't be used to get out
- **Snorri spotted at least two figures** ahead at the intersection, with **candlelight**
- **The ruse:** Berin put on **[[Boris Dorchen|Boris]]'s manacles**, unlocked; Ganav, **hooded in his Lurker cloak**, led him forward on a cord like a captured intruder
- A shout echoed down: *"Hail, Lurker! Karl Franz!"* *(hard to make out; possibly a challenge and countersign)*. Ganav answered in a **muffled** shout (*"Hail Lurker! Praise the Emperor! We found some sewer scum!"*) and rattled the chains: a **hard Charm test**
- **Berin prayed to Myrmidia** to bless the deception: *"Grant the cunning of the fox to make it through the snare."* **The roll went badly; Myrmidia rejected it** and Berin took an **Endurance test** against the visions. The table read it as the goddess of strategy disapproving of plain deception
- Blades were drawn ahead. A gruff voice: *"Who are you? You're no Lurker."*
- **Ganav's Intimidate** (+10 from Berin's support): the prisoner "escaped" as Berin dropped the shackles, and Ganav laid it out: they need access here; *"Either vacate this place for a week, or vacate it permanently through your deaths."*
- The reply: *"We're not leaving because of you. We were leaving anyway, but we won't be back this way for another week if that's what you need."* Whispering, clunking, and then **dark-cowled figures moving off to the east**
- Berin: *"I was wrong to doubt you."* Ganav: *"A life that is spared is a life that has a chance to redeem itself."* He noted a smell of urine; fear or just the sewer, he couldn't tell

### The hidden street door
- **The junction**: crisscrossed arches, **sluice mechanisms** to raise and lower little dams and divert the flow, and **rusty but functional iron bridges** over the channels
- Snorri led them up **about two storeys of stairs** to a door he opened with the **dwarf key** (*a half turn one way, a full turn back*). It opened onto a **main street** through **a door between two businesses** that looks like an entrance to one of them but is **a hidden way into the sewers**. The GM placed it on the city map
- **Snorri on leaving the city by sewer:** the only chance is **the stream access**. The dwarves redirected a **tributary of the Wolfen** underground to flush the sewers, and **a large grate** protects that point. It can be removed to clear debris, but it takes **several dwarves several hours**, and Snorri doubted the dwarves would go to that effort *"just to let you manlings out"*
- **The plan so far:** get Hilde out of the temple (the laundry drain), through the sewers to this door, then **away by boat** from the docks. Berin: *"half a plan."* The party believes it still has **a full day** on the borrowed key, and the Lurker truce gives **six or seven days** of clear passage
- It was **nightfall**. Berin was about **50% wounded**. Snorri, **also poisoned and wounded**, bade them good night and returned to the dwarven quarter alone

### News at the inn
- Back at the [[Tree and Rut Inn]], **nervous gossip**: **Bögenhafen, in the Reikland, has been declared off limits** after something disastrous. Rumours abound. A **boatman with a cargo bound for Bögenhafen** had no idea what to do with it
- Baths, food and drink, and a night's rest. Ganav healed Berin; Hildegarde's injury countdown ticked down a day
- **Next steps agreed:** go to **the docks** and find **boat passage** for [[Hilde Muntz]], then spring her from the temple. Ganav's reasoning: if the temple is watched, the **gates** likely are too, but **the gangs can't watch every dock**. Boris will **ask [[Christina Asper]] to pay** for it
