# Session 19 — Ferlangen

**Played:** 25 April 2026
**In-game date:** 3–4 Jahrdrung 2512

## Summary

Departing the chaotic [[Hemp Merchant]] at first light, the party found that [[Christina Asper]] had ridden ahead at dawn, leaving only a curt note dismissing their work as "meandering provincial" distractions and ordering them to meet her in [[Ferlangen]]. They reached the **[[Fist of Amber]]**, a quiet, almost empty coaching inn, where they fell in with a peddlars'-guild headmistress named **[[Marretta Schongauer]]** (who hired [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] to carry a sealed letter to a Ferlangen warehouse operator named **[[Karl Helpmann]]**) and two evasive Ostlander travellers asking to walk behind the wagon to the city. The innkeeper later confided to [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] that the two were not peddlars but **runaway serfs** fleeing oppressive northern lords, and Schongauer was no guildmistress but a **human trafficker** moving paying refugees south. Pilpoul kept the secret from the rest of the party, and the next morning gave the male refugee his own brown cloak. The party arrived at **[[Ferlangen]]** by evening, queued at the single south gate to touch the portable Sigmar altar, and made for the [[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]] — where the Abbess delivered a flat, unhappy reading of [[Christina Asper|Asper]]'s report on them, then clarified that the party held command of the investigation and Asper was a *resource*, not a director. [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] used a turn in the Verenan library to identify the symbol on the cult artefacts as **[[Khaine]] — god of murder**. The party collected their wages, were billeted at **Frau Busch's boarding house**, and turned to fresh business: counterfeit coins, the Cult of the Howling One bounty, a letter for Helpmann, and Boris's quiet plan to send money home to his sick sister.

## Events

### Morning at the Hemp Merchant — A Letter and a Note

- The yard was crowded with soldiers and wagons; the night had been cramped and restless
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] composed a long, elaborate letter to **[[Lady Steiner]]** at the [[Temple of Shallya]] in [[Middenheim]], asking after the provenance and heraldry of the brass ring once belonging to [[Hildegarde]]'s mother — entrusted to a pigeon carrier (the innkeeper warned the bird might struggle under the prose)
- The innkeeper handed the party a folded note from [[Christina Asper]], delivered before her dawn departure: she had ridden ahead to meet **[[Lucas Pollack]]** in [[Ferlangen]], dismissed the party's investigations as "meandering provincial" distractions, and ordered them to follow when they were done indulging themselves
- Pilpoul read the note aloud with delight, declaring it "happy news" — the party would proceed unburdened by Asper's oversight
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], her sole named ally among them, was the most measured in his reaction

### On the Road — The Fist of Amber

- The road continued through flat Ostland farmland and reached a quiet coaching inn by evening — the **[[Fist of Amber]]**, sign a weathered carving of a fist gripping a lump of orange-golden amber
- The yard was nearly empty — chickens, a patient ox, no other coaches
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] noticed while settling the horses that **all the feed and hay were stored inside the inn's stable** rather than in the attached farm buildings, which sat unused and silent
- Inside, two local Ostlanders — a mousy man and a broad-shouldered, thick-jawed woman — sat at a table; a pockmarked woman smoked a pipe in a shadowed booth

### Marretta Schongauer — Letter for Karl Helpmann

- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] read the pipe-woman's subtle nod as a personal invitation and sat down across from her
- She introduced herself as **[[Marretta Schongauer]]**, headmistress of the local **peddlars' guild**, coordinating goods for small settlements across the northern empire
- She hired Boris on the spot to deliver a **sealed letter to a warehouse operator named [[Karl Helpmann]]** in [[Ferlangen]]
- Pressed **5 silver** into his hand and emphasised the need for speed

### The Two Travellers

- The two Ostlanders approached the rest of the party, hat in hand, asking if they might walk along behind the wagon to [[Ferlangen]]
- They claimed to be **poor peddlars** heading to pick up a shipment of fish hooks, pots, and pans from a warehouse in the city
- [[Hildegarde]] and [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] questioned them carefully — no goods, no backpacks, no weapons, not even a knife
- The story held just enough to be plausible; [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] extended a cautious welcome, with [[Hildegarde]] insisting the whole party should weigh in

### The Common Room — Performance and Gossip

- [[Hildegarde]] performed for locals filtering in from surrounding farms, earning a warm reception and a modest purse of brass pennies
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] worked the crowd for gossip:
  - **[[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]] passed through on the 17th Nachexen**
  - **[[Elfrieda Teuschel]] stayed two nights ago**, still travelling with the same large, broken-nosed companion
  - Locals warned of a dangerous group of highwaymen called the **[[Howling Ones]]**, raiding roads across eastern Ostland up to the Kislev border
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] also took the innkeeper aside and warned him about **counterfeit coins circulating from [[Beekerhoven]]**, showing him one of the false pennies as an example

### A Confidence in the Quiet

- That night the innkeeper pulled [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] aside and confided the truth:
  - The two travellers were not peddlars at all — they were **escaped serfs**, fleeing oppressive lords in the north who treated them little better than slaves
  - **[[Marretta Schongauer]]** was no guildmistress but a **human trafficker** who smuggled desperate people to cities for a fee
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] kept the secret, telling the rest of the party only that the travellers were down on their luck and deserving of kindness — quietly resolved to protect their dignity on the road

### Departure — The Cloak and the Sealed Letter

- The refugees were already in the yard before the party had finished breakfast, their belongings amounting to little more than the clothes on their backs
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] pressed his own **brown cloak** into the male refugee's hands, hoping it might help him pass unnoticed
- [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] hitched the wagon and the group set out
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] asked [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] to examine the sealed letter; the distinctive wax seal and Karl Helpmann's name printed clearly on the front confirmed it was legitimate — and that breaking it would be a mistake

### The Road to Ferlangen

- Distant **howling** drifted across the fields
- A wolf appeared in silhouette on a high cliff above the road, watching the wagon with cold patient eyes before letting out a long howl and vanishing into the grey sky
- The squat graystone walls of **[[Ferlangen]]** rose out of the flat plain — square towers, streaked with fresh mortar
- A single south gate; above the arch the carved **Ostland bull** and the motto **"Halten was zu halten ist"** (Hold What You Have)

### The Gate Ritual

- [[Mungo Mudfoot|Mungo]] elected to stay at the **fortified inn outside the walls** with the wagon and animals — and was immediately distracted by a **female halfling** sitting outside, the first moot folk he'd seen since [[Middenheim]]
- The party joined the queue at the gate, where guards required every traveller to **touch a portable stone altar of [[Sigmar]]** before being allowed entry
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]] whispered a quiet prayer to [[Ulric]] as he pressed his hand to the stone
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] seized it with both hands
- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] offered a solemn salute to [[Myrmidia]] before complying
- [[Hildegarde]] asked the guard a series of earnest questions before being ushered through
- All passed without incident

### Inside Ferlangen

- Town criers were announcing **rewards for members of the [[Howling Ones]] cult** and officially **denying** the existence of any **Kislevite revolutionary forces** operating in Ostland — a denial that seemed only to confirm what it was meant to dismiss
- The two refugees spotted something familiar near a warehouse by the gate and parted ways with the party — the broad-shouldered woman pulled [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] into a bone-crushing bear hug and whispered her thanks before they vanished into the streets
- [[Hoffman Jannitzer]] informed the group that the **[[Temple of Verena (Ferlangen)|Temple of Verena]]** had arranged lodging at **Frau Busch's boarding house**

### The Temple of Verena — The Abbess

- The Abbess, a stern woman behind a great hardwood desk, peered at the party over her spectacles. She had already received a full report from **[[Christina Asper]]** — and it was **not flattering**:
  - Withholding heretical artefacts
  - Goading her into acting as a judge in a matter at an inn
  - Speaking ill of her to soldiers
- [[Pilpoul Ivri|Pilpoul]] and [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] defended the party's conduct, arguing Asper's severity had made cooperation difficult and that the artefacts had been turned over as soon as possible
- The Abbess, unmoved, **clarified the chain of command**:
  - **The party holds the right of command over the investigation**
  - **[[Christina Asper|Asper]] is a resource, not a director**
- She also noted, with a pointed look at [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], that he alone had been named by Asper as a trustworthy ally
- The Abbess confirmed that **neither [[Christina Asper|Asper]] nor [[Lucas Pollack]] had located useful leads** within Ferlangen, and that the **missing Ulrican knights had not yet arrived**
- She suggested the **Merchants Guild** as a reliable avenue for investigating the **Beekerhoven counterfeit coins**, noting several senior members were devoted patrons of the temple

### The Verenan Library — Khaine

- [[Berin Garibaldi|Berin]] slipped into the temple's library to research the heretical symbols encountered during the confrontation with [[Melmoth]]
- He uncovered the name **[[Khaine]]** — identified as the **god of murder** — before the library staff politely but firmly showed him the door
- The discovery cast a grim new light on the **dark sword** and the **tome** the party had carried unknowingly across the Empire

### Practical Matters

- The party collected their **wages** from the temple
- [[Boris Dorchen|Boris]], mindful of his sick sister back home, began arranging to send money through the [[Four Seasons Coach Line]], using a trusted local proxy — **Jenny the Butcher** — to ensure the funds reached his family in their remote village
- The party settled into [[Ferlangen]] battered and road-weary, with [[Professor Udo Stradovski|Stradovski]]'s trail growing warmer by the hour
